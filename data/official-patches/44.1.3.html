Arsenal Retuning Inbox Message

  • 1 Riven Splicer + 100,000 Kuva
  • NOTE: This inbox requires a script to run, which we will do shortly after the hotfix launches. 

Ammo Efficiency Changes
Our recent introduction of Ammo Efficiency traits to Riven mods via Riven Splicers has made this stat more accessible to players, and led to much discussion internally on how the availability of infinite ammo builds affects Warframe’s game balance. 

Warframe is about the power fantasy, and infinite ammo is the epitome of that. Shooting endlessly into hordes of enemies can feel incredible, and we want players to be able to experience that — sometimes. The recent introduction of Riven Splicers has changed that “sometimes” for infinite ammo into a much more guaranteed thing, which has brought around this the following changes:

  • Ammo Efficiency is now capped at 75%. 
    • For those unfamiliar with the Ammo Efficiency mechanic, this means only 1 in 4 shots will consume ammo. 
    • This applies to Ammo Efficiency increases from multiple sources — they will only increase the stat up to this cap. 
    • The Ammo Efficiency cap applies to all equipment that uses Ammo, including Necramechs and Amps.
  • Sources that intentionally gave 100% Ammo Efficiency (ex: Void Relic cracking buffs) have now been rephrased to indicate they give Infinite Ammo.
  • There are other Mods, Arcanes, and Incarnon Evolutions that come with their own edge-cases, which we’ll explain in detail below.

Sources of Infinite Ammo
These mechanics remain unchanged, but their buff descriptions have been updated to specify that they give Infinite Ammo

  • Void Relic Cracking Buffs
  • Akmagnus Prime and Magnus Prime unique trait: Chamber Cascade
  • Athodai (Prime) unique trait: Overdrive
  • Dual Toxocyst unique trait: Frenzy
  • Knell (Prime) unique trait: Death Knell
  • Perigale (Prime) unique trait: Gale Force
  • Vasto Incarnon evolution: Reaper’s Plenty

  • Eternal Logistics:

  • Brain Storm Augment (Grakata)
    • Was: On Headshot: +100% (25% at Unranked) Ammo Efficiency for 0.5s (0.125s at Unranked)

  • Skull Shots Augment (Viper)

    • Also fixed this Blessing not applying properly in-mission.

Recursive “Explode on Death” Changes
Acid Shells has been a hot topic of late — for those unaware, it’s a Sobek Augment that causes enemies to explode on death and deals radial damage that scales with enemy health. Alone, it’s powerful, but it was made even more so via a niche interaction with Elemental Ammo, most commonly used with Cyte-09 (but also seen with Grendel, Oraxia, and Saryn, among others). These Elemental Ammo upgrades could cause Acid Shells to recursively trigger — meaning the one-time death explosion from a shot could instead be triggered infinitely if there were enough enemies to sustain it.

This build type has been on our radar for a while, but we didn’t feel it was the right time to address it. As developers, we’ve been pretty transparent about our threshold to “nerf” content; the Cyte-09 and Acid Shells build never felt dominant or disruptive enough to warrant a closer look, but over the past few weeks that has changed. Arbitrations, discussions about Ammo Efficiency, and the introduction of the Epoch Operation: Taubound have rapidly increased the popularity of this build, to the point where we feel we need to address it.

For the sake of consistency, we have reviewed all death detonation effects as part of the following changes, since this mechanic is not unique to Acid Shells: 

  • Fixed status effects caused by Elemental Ammo on “enemies explode on death” effects being able to retrigger the death explosion effects for the following Mods:
    • Note: these Mods will still benefit from increased damage and status effects gained from Elemental Ammo — they just won’t retrigger the death effects infinitely.
    • Acid Shells
    • Amalgam Furax Body Count
    • Vulcan Blitz
    • Explosive Demise
    • Combustion Beam
    • Thermagnetic Shells
    • Entropy Detonation
  • Reduced Acid Shells’ explosion radius from 15m to 10m.

Riven Mod Changes
First introduced alongside the launch of Rivens in 2016, Riven Dispositions were internal multipliers that guided how strong Rivens would be on individual weapons — ranging from 0.5 on popular weapons, up to 1.55 on those lesser-used.  

The intent of these Dispositions was to incentivize players to use less powerful weapons, but ultimately we saw that investment was still focused on making powerful weapons more powerful. Incarnons were a more successful attempt at breathing life into older outdated weapons, and the overlapping of these systems only multiplied their power further.

With all that in mind, we are making the following change:

  • Removed Riven Dispositions.
    • All Rivens now use the same multiplier for their possible value ranges, the equivalent of 1.2 Disposition in the old system.
    • For more information on the history behind this decision, please review our Dev Workshop. 
  • Restored the ability to trade Riven Mods.

General Additions & Changes 

  • Added the new Axi C13 Relic to mission drop tables to address the Braton Prime Receiver unintentionally being vaulted.
  • The search function in the Peely Pix menu now also searches Peely Pix descriptions.
  • Credit Bundles now have a multiple purchase option (notably for the Credit Bundles in Praghasa's wares).

Fixes

  • Fixed casting the Void of the Lotus ability with specific controller inputs causing players to experience extreme slow mode in the Lua quest.
  • Fixed the Vessel's Gaze dealing no damage if the ability was used before a specific segment in the Vessel quest.
  • Fixed being unable to chatlink Riven Splicers.
  • Fixed the Security Bypass terminal waypoint disappearing once Yareli mounts Merulina.
  • Fixed performance issues caused by Yareli casting Jade Stars while on Merulina.
  • Fixed the gas clouds spawned by Toxic Amphors dealing damage to players.
  • Fixed the Adarza and Smeeta Kavats missing their hair tufts.
  • Fixed the Lotus' Epoch Operation: Taubound inbox message not specifying that Praghasa is found on Eris.
  • Fixed upgrading Boreal's Hatred sometimes displaying incorrect stats if certain Numerical Separator settings were applied.
  • Fixed subtitles for a certain character during the Lua quest not indicating they are a foe.
  • Fixed rare cases of players not receiving the Vessel quest key upon completion of the Lua quest in the Prologue to Tau series. 
    • A small number of players were affected by this issue, and should see the missing quest key in their inventories.
  • Fixed the End of Mission cinematic zooming in too closely to the squad in The Icebind.
  • Fixed being unable to use Fast Travel while in the hallway leading to the Dark Refractory in the Sanctum Anatomica.
  • Fixed ambient music not stopping when returning to the Mountain Pass in The War Within quest.

Script Error Fixes
Script errors mean that something went wrong when the game client was executing a script, which can have various outcomes. Usually it means the related content didn’t function properly under specific circumstances — often we don’t actually know what the error outcome was, so we list it as a Script Error fix to indicate it was addressing an unintended behaviour.

  • Fixed a script error related to Market search sorting.
  • Fixed a script error caused by the electric traps in the Kuva Fortress tileset.
  • Fixed a script error related to the Belfri Ephemera.

For a list of known issues that are on our radar, visit our dedicated thread: https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1523875-known-issues-iceblade-of-narin
