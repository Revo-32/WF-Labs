==Acquisition==
The Cyath blueprint can be acquired after reaching the Rank of '''Neutral''' with the [[Ostron]]s and buying it from [[Hok]] for {{sc|1,000}}.

==Characteristics==
This Strike primarily deals {{D|Slash}} damage.

* '''20%''' {{D|Impact}}
* '''5%''' {{D|Puncture}}
* '''75%''' {{D|Slash}}

Compatible weapon types:
*Machete
*Polearm
