'''Exodia Epidemic''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] for [[Zaw]]s that unleashes a wave of Infested energy that suspends enemies caught in the blast radius, generated from the melee ground slam performed immediately after a [[Maneuvers|bullet jump]] or [[Maneuvers|double jump]].

==Acquisition==
*Exodia Epidemic is obtainable from limited-time events:
**[[Operation: Plague Star]] sold Exodia Epidemic from [[Nakak]] for {{sc|5,000}} + {{cc|1,500}}, requiring '''Rank 2 - Defender''' with [[Operational Supply]].
**The October 2020, 2022 and 2024 [[Nights of Naberus]] event sold Exodia Epidemic from [[Daughter]] for 50 {{Resource|Mother Token|Mother Tokens}} each.

==Notes==
*Perform a bullet jump ({{Keybind|Ctrl}} + {{Keybind|Spacebar}}) or double jump ({{Keybind|Spacebar}} x2) then perform a ground slam (look downward and press {{Keybind|E}}) to create a wave of energy that applies the {{A|Rhino Stomp}}  effect to enemies.
**Enemies affected by this effect take increased damage from {{A|Rhino Charge}} but will not restore overguard while {{M|Reinforcing Stomp}} is equipped.
**This effect cannot activate the effects of the [[Saxum Set]] mods or {{Arcane|Melee Afflictions}}.
**This effect does not add damage to weapons via [[Condition Overload (Mechanic)]].
**Unaffected by [[Ability Strength]], [[Archon Shard]] ability damage bonuses,
*The wave is ~'''5''' meters wide, and will travel forward for ~'''31''' meters, going over terrain elevations and passing through enemies, dealing 10 {{D|Blast}} Damage, before exploding at the end and dealing {{D|Viral}} damage in a ~'''7''' meters radius. Enemies hit by the wave will tumble backward and be suspended in the air similarly to {{A|Rhino Stomp}} for a short time period, up to 4 seconds with a full set.
**This wave has 0% status chance, 0% crit chance and 1x crit multiplier and is unaffected by most modded Damage increases.
***Multiplicative damage scaling when [[Condition Overload (Mechanic)]] and [[Universal Weapon Bonuses|Universal Damage Bonuses]] are combined.
***Flat bonuses like {{M|Proton Snap}}, {{A|Wrathful Advance}}, {{Arcane|Arcane Avenger}}, and {{Arcane|Arcane Crepuscular}} apply normally.
***Damage does NOT scale with [[Combo]] count.
**The explosion is treated as a melee attack dealing ~30% damage distributed as 100% {{D|Viral}} damage that copies your Zaw's, Crit Chance, Crit Damage and Status Chance.
***Modded damage, status chance, crit chance, crit damage all apply appropriately.
***Damage does NOT scale with [[Combo]] count or [[Condition Overload (Mechanic)]].
***Elemental mods such as {{M|Shocking Touch}} and {{M|Proton Snap}} have no effect and cannot inflict their status effects.
***Damage scales additively with [[Universal Weapon Bonuses|Universal Damage Bonuses]].
***Explosion can trigger {{Arcane|Melee Duplicate}} and {{Arcane|Melee Influence}} but will not add combo count or trigger {{Arcane|Melee Animosity}}.
*[[Trading]] Exodia Epidemic requires the trader to be [[Mastery Rank]] 11 or higher.

==Trivia==
*Installing this arcane (or Exodia Contagion) on a Zaw will give the Zaw's Links an infested visual appearance.
*Unlike other Exodia Arcanes, Exodia Epidemic depicts a symbol, instead of [[Ostron Language|inscription]].

{{ver|36}}
*Trading Legendary Arcanes now requires you to be Mastery Rank 11.
**It’s important to note that only the player trading requires MR 11 and not the receiver.
***We needed to address an issue with exploitative Legendary Arcanes farming and ban-evasion account creation used to trade Legendary Arcanes. Other options we experimented with had the risk of innocent players getting caught in the crossfire of the ban-evasion subjects. When we took a look at Trading stats we found that a very small number of players below Mastery Rank 10 trade Legendary Arcanes, and therefore decided to go this route. This is purely a means of deterring the bad actors and ensuring legitimate trading for players.
