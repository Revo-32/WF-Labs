'''{{D|Heat}} Damage''' is one of the four primary elemental damage types. Deals increased damage to {{D|Infested}} but reduced against {{D|Kuva Grineer}}. Its [[Status Effect]]s ignites enemies with [[Damage over Time]], causing them to panic and temporarily reduces [[Armor]].

==[[Status Effect]]s==
===General===
The status effect of '''{{D|Heat}}''' damage is '''Ignite'''. It sets the enemy ablaze, which makes them panic, reduces their [[armor]] by up to '''50%''' and, after a 1 second delay, deals a tick of damage every second for '''6''' seconds.

Enemies killed while under or by a Heat proc will be left in ashes, burning away shortly afterward.

====Damage Over Time====
Each tick deals {{D|Heat}} damage equal to:

:<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Heat Proc Damage per Tick} &= 0.5 \times \text{Modded Base Damage} \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Heat Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Status Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times \text{Additional Multipliers}
\end{aligned}
}
</math>
:Additional Multipliers include modded critical multiplier on [[Critical Hit]] and multipliers on [[Enemy Body Parts]]; these stack multiplicatively with each other.
:Modded Base Damage is not the same as normal damage calculations, ignoring physical and elemental damage bonuses and calculated as:
::<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Modded Base Damage} &= \text{Base Damage} \times (1 + \text{Base Damage Bonuses}) \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses})
\end{aligned}
}
</math>

For example, tick damage for a weapon with an innate 100 damage, {{M|Serration}}, {{M|Hellfire}}, {{M|Bane of Grineer}}, and {{M|Rifle Elementalist}} will be:
:Modded Damage = {{Math|100 {{mul}} (1 + 1.65) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y}}
:Heat Mod Multiplier = {{Math|(1 + 0.9) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y|m=y}}
:'''Tick Damage''' = {{Math|0.5 {{mul}} 344.5 {{mul}} 2.47 {{mul}} (1 + 0.9)|t = y|bf-a=y}}

As can be seen in the above calculations for tick damage, Faction Damage is applied twice, making their effective bonus {{MathText|{{eql}} (1 + Faction Damage bonus)<sup>2</sup>}}. This is a 1.30*1.30 = 1.69 or +69% increase for the 30% Faction Damage mods and 1.55*1.55 = 2.4025 or +140.25% for the 55% Primed Faction Damage mods.

====Heat Inherit====
{{Community|"Heat Inherit" is a community term}}
{{UpdateMe|Clarify Heat inherit mechanic. There is no mention of first application's modded Heat percentage bonus and Faction Damage applying to all subsequent procs.}}
'''"Heat Inherit"''' is a community-created term that describes the interaction of {{D|Heat}} status application between various weapons.  Repeated application of Heat procs do not stack tick damage in the same way other damage over time (DoT) [[Status Effect|status effects]] like {{D|Toxin}}, {{D|Electricity}}, or {{D|Slash}} do. Instead, a Heat proc both adds a stack '''and''' refreshes the duration of '''all''' currently active heat stacks on the target. This allows for ramping up Heat tick damage indefinitely (scaling up linearly) as long as heat status is kept refreshed within the 6 second effect duration (modified by [[Status Effect#Status Duration|Status Duration]]). Additionally, all Heat status stacks are automatically consolidated into one tick of damage per second, with new heat procs refreshing and further adding to this tick damage. 
*Modded bonuses that can apply to the damage of the Heat status, such as {{M|Hellfire}} and [[Faction Damage Mods]], will be lost if the first Heat proc on the enemy is from a source without any such Heat modifiers, like Warframe abilities or [[Manifold Bond]], hindering the performance of Weapons that rely on Heat status to deal damage. 
**To avoid this, the first Heat proc on the enemy must always come from a Weapon that has those modifiers.
**Warframes that can widely apply Heat, such as [[Ember]], can make this a difficult task.
**Status damage mods such as {{M|Rifle Elementalist}} are excepted, and will count towards the Heat status damage from their weapon regardless of modifiers on the first proc.
*All heat procs on an enemy share the same remaining duration, and the longest [[Status Effect#Status Duration|Status Duration]] modifier is always used when resetting the duration. 
**For example: A Tenno sets an enemy on fire using a secondary with no status duration mods. Each time it receives a new heat proc, the remaining durations of all existing heat procs are reset to 6 seconds. While the enemy is still burning, the Tenno switches to their primary, which has {{M|Continuous Misery}} equipped, and applies a heat proc: the remaining duration of all existing heat procs are reset to the updated max duration of 12 seconds. The Tenno switches back to their secondary and applies more heat procs; each time, the remaining duration resets to the max duration of 12 seconds, despite the secondary having no status duration mods.

====Crowd Control====
Enemies affected by a {{D|Heat}} proc are ignited in flames and will panic and flail around for a few seconds, preventing them from taking any other actions. The length of the panic animation depends on enemy type: approximately '''4''' seconds for humanoids, '''3''' for most Infested, and '''2''' for [[MOA]]s and [[Charger]]s. However, [[Osprey]]s, [[Bosses]], [[Tenno]], and enemies with [[Overguard]] are immune to this crowd controlling effect. Repeated application of {{D|Heat}} status will reapply the panic effect, and the effect will also trigger as soon as an enemy's Overguard breaks, even if new Heat procs are not being actively applied.

====Armor Stripping====
The {{D|Heat}} status effect's armor strip has a ramp-up time when it first procs, and a ramp-down time when the proc ends. Every 0.5 seconds '''after''' the initial proc, the enemy will have 15%, then 30%, 40%, and finally 50% of its armor stripped. It therefore takes 2 seconds to reach the maximum armor strip.<ref>[https://imgur.com/a/V2Oc6hl]</ref> Proccing more Heat status effects within this time frame will not speed up the armor stripping process.

After the proc ends, the target will regain armor every 1.5 seconds for 6 seconds. The enemy's armor strip will decrease as follows: 50%, 40%, 30%, 15%, 0%.<ref>[https://imgur.com/a/Zfkv66b]</ref>

Against an enemy with capped armor, giving 90% [[Armor#Enemy Damage Reduction Formula|damage reduction]] (such as level 57 [[Heavy Gunner]]), each tick of armor strip gives an effective damage increase of approximately '''x1.7''', '''x2.5''', '''x3''', '''x3.6''' respectively.<ref>https://www.desmos.com/calculator/fc3wqdepnc</ref>

Status Duration mods will '''''slow down''''' the rate of armor strip. For example, having +100% Status Duration will make the amount armor strip ramp up every second, instead of every half second. As a result, the full 50% armor strip will only be achieved after 4 seconds. Similarly, -50% Status Duration modifier from a [[Riven Mod]] will make armor strip occur every 0.25 second, fully stripping an enemy in just 1 second. 

{{D|Heat}} status [[armor]] reduction is '''multiplicative''' with other armor reduction sources. This can be expressed as the following:

{{MathText|Armor after reduction {{eql}} (1 - {{Tooltip|50%|Heat status max armor reduction of 50% after 2 seconds.}}) {{mul}} [1 - ({{Tooltip|20%|Innate Corrosive status armor reduction.}} + {{Tooltip|6%|Corrosive status armor reduction of 6% per stack.}} {{mul}} Number of Corrosive stacks)] {{mul}} (1 - {{Tooltip|18%|18% armor reduction from one max-rank Corrosive Projection.}} {{mul}} Number Corrosive Projections)}}
{{CustomCollapsible|View  detailed examples|ARI|&nbsp;}}
For example, a full 4-player squad with max-rank {{M|Corrosive Projection|Corrosive Projections}}, an active {{D|Heat}} proc, and max stacked {{D|Corrosive}} proc will result in an enemy's armor being reduced to:
:{{Math|(1 - 50%) {{mul}} [1 - (20% + 6% {{mul}} 10)] {{mul}} (1 - 18% {{mul}} 4)|mt=y|p=y}}.

If only one player has a max-rank {{M|Corrosive Projection}} with the above statuses, an enemy's armor would instead be reduced to:
:{{Math|(1 - 50%) {{mul}} [1 - (20% + 6% {{mul}} 10)] {{mul}} (1 - 18%)|mt=y|p=y}}.

An active {{D|Heat}} proc, and max stacked {{D|Corrosive}} proc ''without'' {{M|Corrosive Projection}} would result in an enemy's armor being reduced to:
:{{Math|(1 - 50%) {{mul}} [1 - (20% + 6% {{mul}} 10)]|mt=y|p=y}}.

Currently, the only non-[[Abilities|ability]] method to ''completely'' remove armor is to use {{M|Shattering Impact}}, {{M|Sharpened Claws}}, or a [[Dagger]] with {{M|Amalgam Argonak Metal Auger}}.
{{CustomCollapsible/End}}

===Railjack===
{{Quote|Railjack {{D|Heat}} Damage results in a Sear Status Effect which causes rounds to burn through enemy ships and deal Damage Over Time.|In-Game Description}}
The status effect of {{D|Heat}} damage against [[Railjack]] space enemies is '''Sear''', dealing '''6''' ticks of damage over '''5''' seconds. Against the Tenno's [[Railjack]], it causes a '''Fire Hazard''' which causes ship [[Railjack/Armaments|armaments]] to overheat faster and cool down slower.

'''Sear''' ticks deal '''100% {{mul}} {{Tooltip|''Modded Damage''|The innate Physical and/or Elemental damage of the weapon, including Vidar bonuses and Plexus bonuses to: Base damage, Critical damage, and bonuses on weak-spots}} {{mul}} (1 + Heat Damage bonuses)''' as {{D|Heat}} damage. Unlike standard heat, each proc has an independent duration and is not refreshed by additional heat procs.

==Elemental Combinations==
{{D|Heat}} damage mods can be combined with other elemental mods to create a new elemental damage type.
*{{D|Heat}} + {{D|Cold}} = {{D|Blast}}
*{{D|Heat}} + {{D|Toxin}} = {{D|Gas}}
*{{D|Heat}} + {{D|Electricity}} = {{D|Radiation}}

==Notes==
*Enemies killed while under the effect of a Heat proc will immolate, causing their body to burn to a crisp and vanish in a few seconds. This will cause {{A|Magnetize}} to stop pulling in more enemies.

==Tips==
*{{Arcane|Magus Accelerant}} is a rare case of resistance reduction that will multiply the effects of heat.
*{{D|Heat}} damage and its status effects can make it a partial replacement for {{D|Corrosive}} damage when it comes to reducing armor.
