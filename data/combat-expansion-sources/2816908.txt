'''Innodem''' is an [[Incarnon]] ceremonial [[dagger]]. Once its Incarnon form is unlocked, Tenno can transform this weapon in battle by executing a [[Heavy Attack]] with a [[Melee Combo]] of 5x or higher, increasing the weapon's range and attack speed, enabling Innodem's Incarnon Resilience, and causing aim glide melee attacks to throw projectiles.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Stinging Thorn}} and {{M|Pointed Wind}} stance.
*As an [[Incarnon]] weapon, Innodem has '''5''' evolution tiers with different perks that allow players to customize their Innodem's stats.
**Once unlocked, activate Incarnon Form by executing a [[Heavy Attack]] with a [[Melee Combo]] of 5x or higher (3x with the  '''Incarnon Imago''' evolution). 
***When the transformation is ready, the combo counter will shimmer with Void energy.
***Incarnon Form lasts for '''90''' seconds and behaves as follows:
****'''+3''' [[Range]]
****'''+40%''' [[Attack Speed]]
****Aim glide melee attacks launch projectiles which deal 360 {{D|Radiation}} damage.
*****Projectile has infinite Punch Through.
*****Projectile has a range of 21 meters.
*****Projectiles do not contribute to but benefit from [[Melee_Combo#Combo_Multiplier|Melee Combo Counter]].
****[[Finisher]]s grant '''Incarnon Resilience''', increasing [[Damage Reduction]] by '''10%''' up to four stacks. These {{Tooltip|stacks are multiplicative to each other|Totals to 34.39% Damage reduction, or 1.52x EHP.}} and last for the duration of Incarnon Form. Mercy finishers with a [[Parazon]] do not apply a stack of '''Incarnon Resilience'''.
***The transformation persists through all states such as holstering, [[bleedout]] and [[disarm]], but is removed upon [[death]].
*Grants a '''10%''' [[Movement Speed]] bonus while held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).

== Incarnon Form ==

When newly acquired from the [[Foundry]], players must acquire '''100''' kills with the Innodem in order to gain access to its Incarnon transformation. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below.

=== Overview ===
*Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 5x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''90''' seconds.
**Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

==Tips==
*Using an [[Exalted Weapon]] will negate Evolution V challenge.
*Incarnon Genesis weapons are counted for Evolution V challenge.
*The Innodem synergizes particularly well with {{Focus|Naramon}} Focus, due to {{Focus|Sling Stun}} granting an easy way to open enemies up to finishers.
**In addition, {{Focus|Killer's Rush}} causes a finisher from Operator form to increase critical chance. Performing a finisher in this fashion will still activate the Innodem's finisher-related evolutions.
**As this weapon is of the dagger classification, it is also able to force slash procs and open enemies for finishers upon usage of a heavy attack.
*Combining {{M|Corrupt Charge}} and {{M|Covert Lethality}} grants a passive minimum 3x [[Melee Combo]], allowing '''Incarnon Imago''' to immediately transform Innodem at will.
*The Innodem's '''Hawk Momentum''' evolution can build and maintain [[Melee Combo]] out of combat at no cost.

==Trivia==
*"Innodem," in Latin, is a present subjunctive form of ''innodo'', which is a verb meaning 'to fasten with a knot' or 'to entangle'.

==Bugs==
*Damage for Innodem's projectiles seems to fluctuate semi-randomly based on a struck enemy's location, revealing a unique damage formula: ((Condition Overload bonus + Location multiplier * Stance multiplier * (1 + Pressure Point bonus)) * (1 + Elemental damage bonus)) * Base damage * Other multipliers
**Direct hits also seem to use this formula, but without the randomness from enemy location.
**Bonus damage from Condition Overload is sometimes doubled for projectiles.
