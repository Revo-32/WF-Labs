{{Arcane|Melee Duplicate}} is a Melee [[Arcane Enhancement]] that causes base (yellow) [[Critical Hit|Critical Hits]] to inflict a [[Extra Hit|second instance of damage]].

==Notes==
*Creates a separate damage instance that will reroll the chance to apply status and also the critical tier.
**It is possible to create a duplicated hit that is either non critical, tier 1 (yellow) or tier 2 (orange).
*The effect can proc on {{Arcane|Exodia Contagion}}, unless the first burst of damage kills the enemy (or one shot it).
*Forced status procs will apply to the duplicated hit if they originate from the melee weapon.
**Duplicated hit will not apply a forced status from {{A|Seeking Talons}}.
*With rank 5 Melee Duplicate the formula for the most optimal [[Critical Chance]] is following:
::{{MathText|Critical Chance {{eql}} (3 {{mul}} [[Critical Damage]] Multiplier {{min}} 4) {{div}} (2 {{mul}} Critical Damage Multiplier {{min}} 2)}}
**With a low [[Critical Damage]] multiplier, other Arcanes such as {{Arcane|Melee Exposure}} will likely provide more damage than Melee Duplicate.
**This formula isn't entirely accurate for weapons with low total crit damage multipliers (shouldn't ever be relevant but should at least be noted).
*[[Trading]] Melee Duplicate requires the trader to be [[Mastery Rank]] 11 or higher.

==Bugs==
*If used with {{A|Toxic Lash}} or {{A|Xata's Whisper}}, this arcane will duplicate the extra damage instance coming from these abilities rather than the original melee hit.
**If {{A|Toxic Lash}} and {{A|Xata's Whisper}} are active simultaneously, the arcane prioritizes the ability occupying the earlier ability slot.
***For example, on a {{WF|Saryn}} with {{A|Spores}} replaced with {{A|Xata's Whisper}} through the [[Helminth]]'s Ability Replacement System, if both {{A|Xata's Whisper}} and {{A|Toxic Lash}} is active, Melee Duplicate would duplicate the extra hit from {{A|Xata's Whisper}}. Conversely, if {{A|Miasma}} is instead the ability replaced with {{A|Xata's Whisper}}, Melee Duplicate would duplicate the Extra Hit from {{A|Toxic Lash}} instead.
*The separate damage instance generated from area of effect damage such [[Slam Attack|Melee Slam Attacks]] or {{Arcane|Exodia Contagion}}'s explosion does not bypass enemy [[Shield Gating]] despite abilities such as {{A|Toxic Lash}}  or {{A|Xata's Whisper}} bypassing them properly in the same scenario.
