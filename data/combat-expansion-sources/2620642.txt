'''Exodia Force''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] for [[Zaw]]s that grants a 50% chance when inflicting a [[Status Effect]] to cause a radial blast around the player, inflicting a percentage of weapon damage to targets within a 6m range. The damage scales with [[melee]] mods and combo multipliers.

==Effect==
{| class="emodtable"
|-
! Rank
! Percentage of Melee Damage Dealt
|-
| 0 || 50%
|-
| 1 || 100%

| 2 || 150%
|-
| 3 || 200%
|}

==Acquisition==
Can be bought from [[Hok]] for {{sc|10,000}} per arcane upon reaching the rank of '''Kin''' with the [[Ostron]]s.

==Notes==
*The radius appears from your warframe, not your weapon.
*Damage has a 100% falloff scaling across 6m.
*The area of effect damage has 1.0 follow through.
*Affected by the combo counter, [[Stance]] damage multipliers, base damage, elemental damage and faction damage mods.
**Follows the same scaling from combo counter as pseudo-exalted abilities like{{A|Blade Storm}}, {{A|Landslide}}, {{A|Shattered Lash}}, {{A|Slash Dash}} and {{A|Whipclaw}}: {{MathText|1 + (combo level - 1) / 4}}
*Not affected by most other melee mods such as {{M|Condition Overload}}, and {{M|Reach}}.
*Not affected by the [[Stealth#Stealth_Damage_Bonus|melee stealth multiplier]].
*Scoring a [[Critical Hit|critical hit]] with a weapon will not affect Exodia Force's damage.
*Unable to produce critical hits or status effects, even with flat bonuses such as those from {{Arcane|Arcane Avenger}}, {{M|Cat's Eye}} or {{A|Covenant}}.

We’ve adjusted the values of older Arcanes with the goal of making lower rank Arcanes more viable, and more desirable while collecting as a result. Certain Arcanes increase all of these stats per rank (making them scale exponentially), but we are changing it so that only one stat is affected by rank (making it scale linearly).
