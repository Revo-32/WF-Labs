'''Enraged''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Valkyr}}'s {{A|Hysteria}} that increases its damage and [[critical chance]] but fixes its duration to 15 seconds and adds a matching cooldown. The base [[Energy]] cost is also increased to 50, while the Energy drain over time is removed.

==Acquisition==
*Can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''Partner''' under {{Faction|The Perrin Sequence}}, or the rank of '''Flawless''' under {{Faction|New Loka}}, and spending {{sc|25,000}} to purchase.

==Notes==
*Cooldown starts '''after''' the duration ends, and cannot start while Transferenced out.
*Cooldown is equal to the length of time {{A|Hysteria}} was active. For example, if Hysteria was activated for 5 seconds, the cooldown will be 5 seconds.
*Duration is affected by [[Ability Duration]].
*Bonus damage and critical chance are '''not''' affected by mods and stack additively with mods like {{M|Pressure Point}} and {{M|True Steel}}.
*Cannot be equipped at the same time as {{M|Hysterical Assault}}.
*Recurring bug, where critical bonus mods stack in the simulacrum but not in game.
