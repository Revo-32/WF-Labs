'''Melee Careen''' is a [[Melee]] [[Arcane Enhancement]] that grants a damage multiplier against frozen targets (enemies afflicted with 10 {{D|Cold}} stacks) and passively allows [[Rolling]] to pass through and apply {{D|Cold}} stacks to nearby enemies with a short cooldown.

==Acquisition==
*Can be bought from [[Roathe]] at [[La Cathédrale]] in the [[Sanctum Anatomica]] for 5 {{Resource|Maphica}}.
*May appear as a [[Steel Path]] [[The Descendia]] reward on a weekly rotational basis.
**Infernum 6 and 13 awards 1.
**Infernum 20 awards 3.

==Notes==
*The damage multiplier only affects enemies that are fully frozen, i.e. 10 {{D|Cold}} stacks.
*{{D|Cold}} stacks are applied to nearby enemies during the entire rolling animation.
*{{D|Cold}} status applied by rolling lasts for 6 seconds.
**Status duration is affected by universal bonuses, such as {{Weapon|Harmony}}'s passive and {{WF|Lavos}}' passive.
**However, it is <u>not</u> affected by mods, such as [[Lasting Sting]].
*Rolling only ignores enemy collision when it applies {{D|Cold}} from rolling every 2s.
*{{M|Shivering Contagion}} affects the rolling {{D|Cold}} passive, regardless if the modded weapon is currently equipped.
**This also applies if the mod is equipped on a [[Robotic]] weapon.
*Synergizes well with {{Arcane|Arcane Ice Storm}}, {{Arcane|Primary Frostbite}}, and {{Arcane|Secondary Shiver}}.
*Freezing enemies does not alert them.
*When activated, a particle effect surrounds your warframe during a roll. 
**The colour of the effect is based on the equipped melee's energy colour.

==Bugs==
*Does absolutely nothing when equipped on {{Weapon|Shadow Clones}} (and possibly other [[Exalted Weapon]]s).
