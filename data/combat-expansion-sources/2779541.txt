'''Cobra & Crane Prime''' is the [[Prime]]d counterpart of the {{Weapon|Cobra & Crane}} sword and shield, acting as a direct upgrade. Cobra & Crane Prime was released alongside {{WF|Baruuk Prime}} and {{Weapon|Afuris Prime}}.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Impact}} damage.
*Aerial attacks throw the shield, knocking down enemies on hit.
**This does reliably trigger {{Arcane|Melee Afflictions}}.
*Blocking attacks stores a charge that increases [[critical chance]] and [[status chance]] by '''15%''' to the next attack. Up to '''4''' charges can be stacked before being consumed.
**Critical Chance bonus is additive to {{M|True Steel}}, {{M|Blood Rush}}, and other similar buffs.
**Status Chance Bonus is additive to {{M|Melee Prowess}}, {{M|Weeping Wounds}}, and other similar buffs.
*When used by {{WF|Baruuk}}/{{WF|Baruuk Prime}}, the first strike of a combo will put the enemy to sleep for '''2''' seconds.
**Hitting a Nullifier bubble will put the respective Nullifier or Corrupted Nullifier to sleep, regardless of weapon range.

==Notes==
*When used by {{WF|Baruuk}}/{{WF|Baruuk Prime}}, every first hit on an enemy will be put to sleep for '''2''' seconds.
**Sleeping enemies are open to [[Melee#Finishers|melee finishers]].
**Can be used on groups of enemies for effective crowd control.
*The throw attack is done by simply tapping the melee button while in mid-air; holding down the button is not necessary, which makes it very fluid.
**The shield is thrown up to '''20''' meters, which is '''not''' affected by range mods.
**The shield knocks down enemies, opening them up for ground finishers.
*Throw attacks can be done both while the weapon is fully equipped or with a quick melee attack.
*The thrown shield has a very slight homing effect, similar to thrown Glaives, though it is still very much capable of missing.
*The throw attack can be done in any direction (dictated by your aim), and therefore the usual downward melee slam attack is done by looking straight down.
*Attacks stagger enemies on every hit.
*The thrown shield attack uses base model of {{Weapon|Cobra & Crane}} shield as a projectile.
