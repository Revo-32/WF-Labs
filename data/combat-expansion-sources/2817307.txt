'''Okina Prime''' is the [[Prime]]d counterpart of the {{Weapon|Okina}} dual sai, acting as a direct upgrade. Okina Prime was released alongside {{WF|Protea Prime}} and {{Weapon|Velox Prime}}.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Vazarin}} polarity, matches {{M|Spinning Needle}}.
*Innate two {{Pol|Madurai}} polarities.

{{Advantages|
