'''Amalgam Argonak Metal Auger''' is an Amalgam [[Weapon Augments]] mod exclusive to the {{Weapon|Argonak}}. It provides increased [[Punch Through]], [[armor]] stripping when using a [[:Category:Dagger|dagger]], and allows the Argonak's enemy highlighting to work through walls.

==Notes==
*The special effect is only accessible while aiming Argonak and has a range of 100m.
**Only enemies that are near your aiming reticle will be highlighted.
**Enemies will be highlighted through walls even if you do not have enough punchthrough to hit them.
*Armor reduction works like {{M|Shattering Impact}} and stacks additively with it.
**All damage effects from daggers, including [[Status]] damage ({{D|Slash}}, {{D|Heat}}, {{D|Toxin}}, {{D|Electricity}}, {{D|Gas}} and {{D|Blast}} procs) will reduce armor.
***{{D|Electricity}}'s chain damage, {{D|Gas}}'s damage cloud and {{D|Blast}}'s AoE explosion will trigger the armor reduction, allowing them to remove armor on multiple targets.
***Damage from status effect transferred via {{Arcane|Melee Influence}} will trigger the armor reduction.
*Not [[Trading|tradeable]].

==Tips==
*The {{M|Pointed Wind}} stance contains the two highest hit/sec combos of dagger stances (~3.2 hits/sec), however lacks the slashing of {{M|Stinging Thorn}} (~2.7 h/s on forward combo, ~2.5 h/s on neutral).
