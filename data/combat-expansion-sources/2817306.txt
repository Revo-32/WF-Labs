The '''Okina''' are {{Faction|Tenno}} dual sai that excel in [[attack speed]].

This weapon can be sold for {{cc|5,000}}.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Vazarin}} polarity, matches {{M|Spinning Needle}}.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

{{Advantages|
*Grants a '''5%''' [[Movement Speed]] bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
}}

{{Disadvantages|}}

==Trivia==
*The Okina's name is likely derived from [[wikipedia:Okinawa_Prefecture|Okinawa]], a prefecture in Japan traditionally associated with the [[wikipedia:Sai_(weapon)|sai]].
*The Okina is based on the Japanese Sai. The game however does not support traditional Sai fighting style and does not convey the damage types a Sai would inflict. If compared to a Sai the weapon should predominently deal {{D|Impact}} and {{D|Puncture}} damage and should not deal {{D|Slash}} damage.
