==Drop Locations==
*[[The Descendia]] - 1% chance from [[Storage Container]]s.

==Notes==
*The Block combo shot is considered a melee attack:
**It builds '''Vinquibus Ferocity''' and benefits from '''Vinquibus Precision'''.
**It can trigger headshot conditions on a warframe (e.g. {{Arcane|Arcane Precision}}), but '''not''' on weapons (including {{Weapon|Vinquibus (Primary)}}).
*The Block combo shot has pinpoint [[Accuracy]] and can pierce '''1''' killed enemy.
