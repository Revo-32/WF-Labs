'''Praedos''' is an [[Incarnon]] ceremonial [[tonfa]]. Once its Incarnon form is unlocked, Tenno can transform this weapon in battle by executing a [[Heavy Attack]] with a [[Melee Combo]] of 5x or higher, increasing the weapon's range and attack speed.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Gemini Cross}} and {{M|Star Divide}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*As an [[Incarnon]] weapon, Praedos has '''5''' evolution tiers with different perks that allow players to customize their Praedos' stats.
**Activate Incarnon form by executing a [[Heavy Attack]] with a [[Melee Combo]] of 5x or higher (3x with '''Swift Transmute'''). 
***When the transformation is ready, the combo counter will shimmer with Void energy.
***Incarnon form lasts for '''90''' seconds and behaves as follows:
****'''+3''' [[Range]] 
****'''+40%''' [[Attack Speed]]. 
***The transformation persists through all states such as holstering, [[bleedout]] and [[disarm]], but is removed upon [[death]].

==Incarnon Form==
When newly acquired from the [[Foundry]], players must acquire '''100''' kills with the Praedos in order to gain access to its Incarnon transformation. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below.

=== Overview ===
* Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 5x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''90''' seconds.
** Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

==Tips==
*Combining {{M|Corrupt Charge}} and '''Adept Reflexes''' grants a passive minimum 3x [[Melee Combo]], allowing '''Swift Transmute''' to immediately transform Praedos at will.
*{{M|True Punishment}} affects '''Shockwave Synergy''', effectively doubling the Combo Count gain from 4 to 8 per enemy hit by the Slam radius. It is also affected by other sources that grant additional combo count chance.
*Using an [[Exalted Weapon]] will negate Evolution V challenge.
*Incarnon Genesis weapons are counted for Evolution V challenge.
*The increase to Parkour Velocity from '''Evolved Ascension''' is passive, and does not require the Praedos to be held to be active.

==Trivia==
*Praedo means ''robber'' or ''plunderer'' in Latin.
*While in Incarnon form, Praedos will exude a turquoise aura.
