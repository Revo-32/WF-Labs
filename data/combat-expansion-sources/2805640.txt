{{Arcane|Melee Influence}} is a Melee [[Arcane Enhancement]] that grants melee {{D|Electricity}} status procs a 20% chance to grant a timed buff that causes melee elemental status effects to spread to enemies around the targeted enemy. The buff cannot have its duration refreshed.

==Notes==
*Eligible status effects that can be spread by Melee Influence include:
**Primary Elemental ({{D|Cold}}, {{D|Electricity}}, {{D|Heat}}, {{D|Toxin}})
**Secondary Elemental ({{D|Blast}}, {{D|Corrosive}}, {{D|Gas}}, {{D|Magnetic}}, {{D|Radiation}}, {{D|Viral}})
*Melee Influence cannot spread the following:
**Physical ({{D|Impact}}, {{D|Puncture}}, {{D|Slash}})
**{{D|Void}}, {{D|Tau}}, {{D|Knockdown}}, {{D|Stagger}}, {{D|Ragdoll}}, {{D|Lifted}}, and {{D|Microwave}}
*When an elemental Status Effect is spread by Melee Influence, affected enemies are also dealt damage equal to that element's damage from the original attack.
**Example: When a melee attack that deals 100 physical base damage modded with {{M|Shocking Touch}} and {{M|Magnetic Rush}} procs Magnetic, every other enemy within Melee Influence's range will receive 60 Magnetic damage and a Magnetic status effect.
**Damage spread is based on the amount of matching elemental damage after quantization, including effects such as {{M|Condition Overload}} and [[Critical Hit#Critical Multiplier|critical multiplier]].
***For damage spread by Melee Influence, Condition Overload's damage bonus is based on the number of status effects on the target directly struck by the attack that caused Melee Influence damage to be spread; the number of status effects on the enemies receiving Melee Influence damage is not part of the calculation.
** Performing a finisher with a forced elemental proc will not spread any [[Finisher]] damage, as 0% of the damage dealt is made up of the elemental status that was procced.
*Spread radius extends from the position of the enemy hit.
**Thrown weapons like {{Weapon|Xoris}} and gunblades like {{Weapon|Redeemer}} greatly benefit from this 
*Melee Influence only triggers from direct melee strikes. Secondary melee effects such as {{D|Magnetic}}'s breakage effect and {{Weapon|Dual Ichor|Dual Ichor Incarnon}}'s toxin fields cannot trigger Melee Influence.
**Melee ''projectiles'' passing through {{A|Electric Shield}}, {{Weapon|Mutalist Quanta}}'s alternate fire, or {{M|Conductive Sphere}} can also trigger Melee Influence.
***Does not work with [[Glaive (Weapon Type)]] weapons
***Examples of this are {{Weapon|Nepheri}}, {{Resource|Hate Incarnon Genesis}}, {{Resource|Ceramic Dagger Incarnon Genesis}}, or {{Weapon|Syam}}'s projectiles.
*Proccing multiple elements at the same time will show the sum of the spread damage.
*Due to the nature of [[Faction Damage Bonus|Faction Damage Bonuses]], they are applied '''twice''' on damage done by Melee Influence and '''thrice''' on damaging status procs caused by it.
** A melee with 100 base damage modded with {{M|Shocking Touch}} and with a 55% Increased Faction Damage Bonus, will deal 294 Hit damage, with a 228 Electricity Proc Damage. If Melee Influence procs, the spread Electricity procs will deal 353 damage. 
* Examples: A {{Weapon|Skana}} modded with {{M|Pressure Point}}, {{M|Vicious Frost}}, {{M|Virulent Scourge}}, and {{M|Voltaic Strike}}.
**A hit that procs {{D|Electricity}} will do {{Math|120 {{mul}} (1 + 1.2) {{mul}} (0.6)|mt=y}} {{D|Electricity}} damage to all enemies in range and apply an {{D|Electricity}} proc dealing {{Math|120 {{mul}} (1 + 1.2) {{mul}} (1 + 0.6) {{div}} 2|mt=y}} {{D|Electricity}} damage per tick.
** A hit that procs {{D|Viral}} will do {{Math|120 {{mul}} (1 + 1.2) {{mul}} (0.6 + 0.6)|mt=y}} {{D|Viral}} damage to all enemies in range and apply a {{D|Viral}} proc.
** A hit that procs {{D|Slash}} will do nothing.
**A hit that procs {{D|Electricity}} and {{D|Viral}} will do {{Math|120 {{mul}} (1 + 1.2) {{mul}} (0.6)|mt=y}} {{D|Electricity}} damage and {{Math|120 {{mul}} (1 + 1.2) {{mul}} (0.6 + 0.6)|mt=y}} {{D|Viral}} damage to all enemies in range, then apply a {{D|Viral}} proc and an {{D|Electricity}} proc dealing {{Math|120 {{mul}} (1 + 1.2) {{mul}} (1 + 0.6) {{div}} 2|mt=y}} {{D|Electricity}} damage per tick.
*The AoE ignores the [[Rift Plane]], affecting all enemies within range regardless of what plane the attack was made from.
*Elemental damage gained through abilities such as {{M|Smite Infusion}} will also be spread by Melee Influence.

==Tips==
*It can sometimes be strategic to focus on only one elemental type, since adding additional types makes it less likely for your weapon's highest elemental damage type to trigger. But it can also be strategic to use multiple elemental types; for example, Viral procs can multiply the damage of subsequent Eletric procs, and more types of status procs means increased damage bonuses from Condition Overload.

*{{Weapon|Xoris}}: In-flight detonation (100%)
*{{Weapon|Falcor}}: In-flight detonation and charged throw (100%)
*{{Weapon|Arca Titron}}: Slam attacks (chance), Slam Capacitor slam attacks (100%) and Heavy slam attacks (100%)
*{{Weapon|Obex}}/{{Weapon|Prisma Obex}}: Slam attacks (chance)
*{{Weapon|Ohma}}/{{Weapon|Prisma Ohma}}: Slam attacks (chance)
*{{Weapon|Lacera}}/{{Weapon|Ceti Lacera}}: Slam attacks (chance)
*{{Weapon|Galvacord}}: Slam attacks (chance)
*{{Weapon|Rumblejack}}: Slam attacks (100%), Heavy slam attacks (chance)
**Unique finisher cannot proc Melee Influence 
*{{Weapon|Lecta}}/{{Weapon|Secura Lecta}}: Slam attacks (chance), Heavy slam attacks (chance)
*{{Weapon|Ninkondi}}: Slam attacks (chance), Heavy slam attacks (chance)
*{{Weapon|Korumm}}: Unique block combo (100%)

==Bugs==
*Hits that one-hit-kill enemies cannot trigger nor benefit from Melee Influence.
*Melee Influence works with [[Unattributed Damage|Unattributed Damage Sources]] causing certain abilities, decrees and weapons to benefit from it whilst not usually being able to. Some examples are:
**{{M|Electro Pulse}}
**{{M|Synergized Prospectus}} only on clients.
**{{A|Breach Surge}} only on clients.
**{{A|Elemental Ward}} on {{D|Cold}}
**{{Decree|Wyrmling's Aid}}
