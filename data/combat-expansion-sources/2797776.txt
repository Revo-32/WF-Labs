'''Cantare''' is {{WF|Jade}}'s [[Signature Weapon|signature]] throwing knives and counterpart to [[Stalker]]'s {{Weapon|Despair}}. Cantare's knives lodge onto surfaces and return upon [[reload]] with significantly increased [[Critical Chance]]; enemies struck by returning knives increase ally [[Reload Speed]].

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Blades lodges onto surfaces. Upon reloading, the blades return and damages anything in their path as they fly back to the wielder, with an enhanced [[Critical Chance]] set to '''300%'''.
**Set Critical Chance ignores most other modifiers, whether from mods or Warframe abilities. However, {{D|Puncture}} [[Status Effect]] can increase the damage to that of a Tier-4 critical hit.
**Effects that reload while [[holster]]ed (such as {{M|Eject Magazine}}) will restore ammo without affecting the previously thrown blades, which will return upon normal reload, resulting in a higher amount of blades that can be generated and then returned simultaneously.
***Once the amount of embedded blades exceeds 30, older blades will begin returning with each shot in an inconsistent order.
****This amount includes shots produced by [[Multishot]].
**{{M|Synth Charge}} will affect all returning blades if reloading from empty.
*Striking enemies with the returning reload grants '''Cantare Chorale'''. increasing [[Reload Speed]] by '''20%''' for '''30''' seconds to all allies within [[Affinity Range]].
**When combined with the effects of {{Weapon|Evensong}}, and {{Weapon|Harmony}}, all three buffs merge into '''Jade's Ensemble''', which lasts '''60''' seconds and increases the Reload Speed bonus to '''60%'''.

==Notes==
*Returning knives seem to target the midpoint between the two ammo pouches on the Warframe's thighs rather than the location of the aiming reticle above its shoulder. In order to consistently strike an enemy with the returning blades, it may be necessary to aim above and slightly to the side of them (i.e., if you're aiming past the Warframe's left shoulder, aim to the left of the enemy) to counteract this.
*The Cantare Chorale buff ''can'' be refreshed on subsequent triggers while it's still active.

==Tips==
*Cantare's reload speed buff can make it useful to use alongside a primary weapon that has a long reload time, or has to reload often, as long as the damage or utility of a different secondary weapon isn't important to the current loadout. Weapons that reload after every shot, like the {{Weapon|Exergis}} or {{Weapon|Vectis}}, will see the largest benefit.
**Cantare Chorale can also be applied to other secondary weapons if a teammate carries the weapon and activates the buff.
***While it also works on the wielder's equipped [[Archgun|heavy weapon]], activating Cantare Chorale and switching back to it within its buff duration may not be possible if enough ammo is spent before switching weapons, unless {{A|Transmutation Probe}} or {{A|Dispensary}} are available for use.
**Cantare Chorale's easy conditional, comfortable duration, and ability to be refreshed make it very easy to keep the buff up constantly in normal gameplay.
**This also makes it useful for [[Incarnon]] weapons that transform frequently, such as {{Resource|Kunai Incarnon Genesis}}, as reload speed modifiers affect how long Incarnon weapons take to transform as well.
**If Cantare is being built as a utility and support "weapon" instead of as your main source of weapon damage, {{Arcane|Secondary Fortifier}} may be worth considering over other Arcanes meant to increase damage instead.
*Cantare's returning knives will always critically hit ("red crit", to be specific), meaning that critical multiplier bonuses will always be active for this component of Cantare's damage. However, the weapon's actual critical chance for when its primary fire is used is somewhat below average by comparison, if still usable. 
**{{Arcane|Secondary Enervate}} ''does'' count Cantare's guaranteed red crits on returning knives as "Big Critical Hits". This will sometimes cause its critical bonus to reset earlier than normal, making the Arcane less effective than expected, though its low base critical chance and the Arcane's more passive trigger condition means it may still result in more critical hits on average than {{Arcane|Cascadia Accuracy}} or {{Arcane|Cascadia Overcharge}}.
*Cantare's returning knives can proc status effects as expected, effectively giving each magazine twice as many chances to inflict them.
*As with any weapon with unlimited ammunition, Cantare is an effective choice in situations where ammo pickups may be scarce, such as long trips on the [[Plains of Eidolon]], or certain [[Deep Archimedea|Archimedea]] [[Temporal Archimedea|modifiers]].
