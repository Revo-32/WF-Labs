The '''Sepfahn''' is a {{D|Slash}}-focused Strike used in [[Zaw]] construction, and is currently the only Strike able to make a Nikana.

==Acquisition==
The Sepfahn blueprint can be acquired after reaching the Rank of '''Neutral''' with the [[Ostron]]s and buying it from [[Hok]] for {{sc|1,000}}.

==Characteristics==
This Strike primarily deals {{D|Slash}} damage.

* '''10%''' {{D|Impact}}
* '''25%''' {{D|Puncture}}
* '''65%''' {{D|Slash}}
This Strike also has a flat +2% Critical Chance bonus and a flat +2% [[status chance]] bonus.

Compatible weapon types:
*Nikana
*Staff

Gives a {{Pol|Madurai}} stance polarity.

==Notes==
*Has 10% base critical chance and [[status chance]], while other Zaw strikes, excluding Plague Keewar and Plague Kripath, have 8%.
**Increases to 20% when gilded.

==Trivia==
*The text at the top of a sheath for a Zaw with this strike roughly translates as "Slash" from the Ostron language.
*The text at the bottom of a sheath for a Zaw with this strike roughtly translates as "Kits" from the Ostron language
