'''Evensong''' is {{WF|Jade}}'s [[Signature Weapon|signature]] bow and counterpart to [[Stalker]]'s {{Weapon|Dread}}. Shots apply empowered {{D|Puncture}} status effects and kills grant increased [[Multishot]].

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Fully charged shots explode in a '''4''' meter radius on impact with a surface or enemy.
**Charged shot projectiles cannot benefit from [[Punch Through]], and will explode on the first surface or enemy hit.
**Initial hit and explosion apply status separately.
**Explosion does not need direct line of sight to deal damage and will penetrate walls.
**Can benefit from {{M|Firestorm}} ({{M|Primed Firestorm|Primed}}) and {{M|Cautious Shot}}.
**Can equip the bow-exclusive {{M|Thunderbolt}} mod.
*[[Fire rate]] mods apply double their bonuses.
*Direct hits and explosion apply '''7''' {{D|Puncture}} status effects (bypassing the normal limit of 5), reducing enemy damage by '''100%''' and increasing weapon [[Critical Chance]] that the victim receives by '''35%'''.
*Kills grant '''Evensong Devotion''', increasing [[Multishot]] by '''35%''' for '''30''' seconds to all allies within [[Affinity Range]].
**When combined with the effects of {{Weapon|Cantare}}, and {{Weapon|Harmony}}, all three buffs merge into '''Jade's Ensemble''', which lasts '''60''' seconds and increases the multishot bonus to '''70%'''.

==Notes==
*Triggering '''Evensong Devotion''' while it is already active will refresh the duration.
*Enemies killed with [[Status Effect|status effects]] inflicted by an Evensong arrow will trigger '''Evensong Devotion'''. 
**This includes enemies killed by damage-over-time status effects inflicted by the charged radial attack. 
**However, enemies killed by damage caused by area-of-effect status effects such as {{D|Gas}} clouds will not trigger it.
*Enemies that are usually capped at 4 stacks of a status, such as [[Acolytes]] and enemies with [[Overguard]], will receive 5 stacks of {{D|Puncture}} from Evensong.
*Blast radius of the radial charged attack can be increased by using {{M|Firestorm}}.
**All enemies struck by the radial attack will be inflicted with 7 stacks of {{D|Puncture}}.
**A fully charged projectile will despawn on causing a radial explosion, even if it had punch-through.
*{{M|Thunderbolt}}'s explosions will apply Evensong's [[Puncture]] passive to enemies it hits, enabling uncharged shots to apply [[Puncture]] status in a 10m radius, albeit with only a 30% chance of an explosion per projectile.
*When Evensong is shot into {{WF|Zephyr}}'s {{A|Tornado}}, instead of {{D|Puncture}} adding to the final critical chance of the weapon, it appears to get multiplied by critical chance mods.
*The specter from [[Duality]] will not apply the 7 stacks of [[Puncture]] when hitting enemies with the Evensong.
