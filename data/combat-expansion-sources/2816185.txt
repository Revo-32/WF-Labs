| info = 
*Immortalized by his reaping of souls, Sevagoth passively collects the life essences of enemies affected by or slain by {{A|Reap}}, {{A|Sow}}, {{A|Gloom}}, or {{A|Death's Harvest}}, gradually filling the '''Death Well''' gauge displayed above his ability icons. Once <u>'''75%'''</u> of the gauge is filled, Sevagoth may leave his mortal body behind to transfer control into {{WF|Sevagoth's Shadow}}. Sevagoth's Shadow is [[Invulnerable]] for <u>'''5'''</u> seconds when cast and drains <u>'''1%'''</u> of the Death Well per second to remain summoned. Sevagoth's Shadow possesses four new Abilities: {{A|Embrace}}, {{A|Consume}}, {{A|Death's Harvest}}, and {{A|Reunite}}. Exalted Shadow will deactivate when the Death Well fully depletes, when the Shadow receives fatal damage, when [[Transference]] is initiated, when {{A|Reunite}} is cast, or an ability nullification effect comes into contact with Sevagoth's body, such as [[Nullifier Crewman]] or [[Isolator Bursa]].
**Sevagoth's physical body is invulnerable to damage and untargetable by enemies while taking control of Shadow.
***Any status effects applied to Sevagoth will remain active however; status effects are not removed from him when casting Exalted Shadow or {{A|Reunite}}.
**The Shadow will always spawn with a 5 second of [[Invulnerability]] grace period, while fully restoring it's health, shields and initial [[Starting Energy]].
***The Shadow can have its energy bar fully restored on each cast with {{M|Preparation}}
**The Shadow is propelled forward for <u>'''5'''</u> meters when cast.
***Propulsion from summoning the Shadow carries from the previous Shadow's momentum before casting {{A|Reunite}}. If aim gliding while casting this ability, Sevagoth's body will be propelled forward in an arcing trajectory instead.
**When the Death Well reaches at least '''75%''', a sound indicator plays for the player, while the well's energy visually glows intensely, as energy veins emanate outside the well.
*While controlling Sevagoth's Shadow, he arms himself with his melee [[Exalted Weapon]], the '''{{Weapon|Shadow Claws}}'''.
**Exclusively uses the {{M|Ravenous Wraith}} Stance.
**Base damage is {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''100 / 125 / 200 / 250''' damage distributed between '''30%''' {{D|Impact}}, '''20%''' {{D|Puncture}}, and '''50%''' {{D|Slash}}.
**Attacks have a '''<u>38%</u>''' [[critical chance]], '''<u>2.6x</u>''' [[critical multiplier]], '''<u>24%</u>''' [[status chance]], '''<u>1.0</u>''' [[attack speed]], and a [[Melee#Range|range]] of '''<u>1.5</u>''' meters.
**Shadow Claws can be equipped with melee mods and [[Arcane Enhancement]]s.
***[[Riven Mods]] are '''not''' generated for Shadow Claws.
**Because Shadow Claws are counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as {{M|Steel Charge}} and {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}.
**Primaries and secondaries equipped on Sevagoth can '''not''' be used by the Shadow.
*Casting Exalted Shadow is a full-body animation that restricts movement and other actions.
*Sevagoth visually summons his Shadow which materializes in front of him. Sevagoth's body remains stationary in a levitating stance.
*While within an ability nullification sphere from enemies such as [[Nullifier Crewman]] or [[Isolator Bursa]], Sevagoth's Shadow receives damage to its [[Health]] pool as '''15%''' of its maximum health pool every '''0.5''' seconds.
*Sevagoth's Shadow will benefit from [[Focus Schools]] and [[Archon Shard]]s slotted into Sevagoth himself.
*Sevagoth's Shadow, and Shadow Claws, can benefit from Helminth [[Invigoration]]s applied to Sevagoth as they are both considered exalted weapons.
**Any primary, secondary, and reload speed buff from invigorations will '''not''' have any benefit during Exalted Shadow as it uses melee exclusively.

| interactions =
* Sevagoth's Abilities will stay active during Exalted Shadow.
* Each enemy affected then killed by any source, or directly killed by {{A|Reap}}'s Shadow, {{A|Sow}}, or {{A|Death's Harvest}} fills <u>'''5%'''</u> of the Death Well. Each enemy ''alive'' within {{A|Gloom}}'s radius fills <u>'''0.1%'''</u> per second of the Death Well.
** The player must be directly controlling Sevagoth with Exalted Shadow deactivated to gain Death Well percentage from the eligible Abilities noted above.
** Death Well gain stacks when Sown enemies die from Reap, but '''not''' vice-versa.
* For ''each'' percent of the Death Well drained during Exalted Shadow, Sevagoth's health is healed by <u>'''1%'''</u> of his maximum health pool once {{A|Reunite}} is complete.

| tips = 
*If Sevagoth would inevitably fall [[Out of Bounds]], the {{A|Exalted Shadow}} can be summoned to prevent [[Channeled Abilities]] or buffs like {{A|Gloom}} from turning off on Sevagoth.
*Each {{A|Exalted Shadow}} and {{A|Reunite}} recast will respectively reset double-jumps, allowing easier combat against aerial units or bosses like the [[Hunhullus]].
*Sevagoth and Shadow can benefit from multiple [[Unattributed Damage]] effects while controlling the {{A|Exalted Shadow}}.
