'''Spinnerex''' is {{WF|Oraxia}}'s [[Signature Weapon|signature]] whip. Dealing {{D|Toxin}} damage with high [[Status Chance]], enemies tagged by Spinnerex and afflicted with {{D|Toxin}} status have a chance to explode upon their death, spreading {{D|Toxin}} status effects.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals {{D|Toxin}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Zenurik}} polarity, matching {{M|Coiling Viper}} and {{M|Lashing Coil}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*Enemies hit by Spinnerex and then killed from any source have a '''10%''' chance to explode for each stack of {{D|Toxin}} status, spreading {{D|Toxin}} damage.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

==Notes==
*Enemies hit by the Spinnerex within the past '''5''' seconds have a '''10%''' chance to explode per {{D|Toxin}} status effect on them when killed, dealing '''480''' {{D|Toxin}} damage in a '''10''' meter radius, with a forced {{D|Toxin}} status effect.
**Spinnerex does <u>not</u> need to deal the killing blow, nor apply the {{D|Toxin}} status effects in order to trigger the effect.
**Damage is affected by Melee damage buffs, Elemental buffs, and Faction damage buffs, but not by {{M|Condition Overload}}.
**Damage falls off with distance.
**Explosions cannot perform critical hits, or apply modded status effects.
**Explosions will cause an additional damage instance from {{WF|Garuda}}'s {{A|Seeking Talons}}, {{WF|Saryn}}'s {{A|Toxic Lash}}, and {{WF|Xaku}}'s {{A|Xata's Whisper}}.
*Despite being her [[Signature Weapon|signature weapon]], {{WF|Oraxia}} can never take full advantage of the Spinnerex due to her melee slot being blocked while using her ultimate ability, {{A|Silken Stride}}.
