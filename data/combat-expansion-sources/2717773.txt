'''Bayonet''' is a subclass of combined [[Primary]] and [[Melee]] weapons. It was first introduced with the {{Weapon|Vinquibus (Melee)}} in {{ver|41}}.

==Characteristics==
*Equipping a Bayonet will occupy both primary and melee weapon slots.
**[[Affinity]] and [[Mastery Rank]] are shared between forms.
**Primary and melee forms are modded separately, using [[Primary Mods]] and [[Melee Mods]] respectively.
***[[Riven Mod]]s for primary and melee forms are generated separately.
**{{Resource|Orokin Catalyst}}, [[Focus Lens]], {{Resource|Exilus Weapon Adapter}}, and {{Resource|Forma}}/[[Polarization]] are applied to both forms.
**Does not appear as an available loadout option in [[Deep Archimedea]]/[[Temporal Archimedea]] or [[Duviri]].
