'''Arcane Universal Fallout''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] for the [[Warframe]] that gives enemies a chance to drop a large [[Pickups#Universal Orb|Universal Orb]] per {{D|Radiation}} Status Effect on death.

==Notes==
*Applying a {{D|Radiation}} status effect on an enemy '''<u>permanently</u>''' increases the chance for an enemy to drop a [[Pickups#Universal Orb|Universal Orb]] when killed.
**Chance is capped at '''60%''' for all ranks of the arcane.
**Drop chance increase persists after {{D|Radiation}} status effect wears off; No {{D|Radiation}} status effect need be present when an affected enemy is killed.
*Universal orbs created from this Arcane give '''50''' health and '''50''' energy.
*The following abilities can apply the {{D|Radiation}} status effects:
