*Citrine expends {{stat|Ability Efficiency|icon=only}}'''75''' [[Energy]] to summon her Prismatic Gem, launching it forward horizontally for <u>'''5'''</u> meters from her location before it ceases movement. The '''Prismatic Gem''' is an untargetable [[Invulnerable]] floating object that lasts for {{stat|Ability Duration|icon=only}}'''23 / 25 / 27 / 30''' seconds, during which it emits a rainbow aurora spanning a radius of {{stat|Ability Range|icon=only}}'''10 / 12 / 13 / 15''' meters.
**The gem object is not affected by gravity and remains intangible to other entities, allowing players, enemies, and their attacks to phase through it.
**When deploying, the gem slides above higher terrain elevation in its travel path and stops movement on collision with walls.
**Citrine can move the aiming reticle during the casting animation before she fully summons the gem, to push it forward to the desired direction.
*Prismatic Gem's rainbow aurora enhances [[Status Effect]] application from Citrine and her allies' weapons in various ways:
<div class="tabber-borderless" ><tabber>
|-|Status Bonuses=<div style="display:none;">'''Status Bonuses'''<br></div>
*Citrine and allied [[Warframes]] inside the aurora gain a {{stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''60% / 70% / 85% / 100%''' [[Status Chance]] bonus for their [[Universal Weapon Bonuses|weapons]], and {{stat|Ability Duration|icon=only}}'''60% / 70% / 85% / 100%''' [[Status Duration]] bonus for both weapons and abilities.
**Citrine and allies gain or lose status bonuses whenever they enter or leave the aurora.
**[[Companions]] and allied NPCs do '''not''' benefit from status bonuses.
**Abilities that are [[Universal Weapon Bonuses#List of Abilities Affected by Universal Bonuses|coded as a weapon]], such as {{A|Breach Surge}}, can benefit from the Status Chance bonus.
**Status Chance bonus is additive to mods such as {{M|Rifle Aptitude}}, {{M|Sure Shot}}, and {{M|Melee Prowess}}.
**Status Duration bonus is additive to mods such as {{M|Continuous Misery}}, {{M|Perpetual Agony}}, and {{M|Lasting Sting}}.
**Multiple Citrines in the squad can stack Prismatic Gem's status buffs.
*Status chance percentage bonus is displayed as a Prismatic Gem buff icon beside Citrine and her squadmates' hitpoints indicators. Status duration bonus remains hidden.
*While affected by status bonuses, Citrine and her allies are visually followed by burrowing mineral deposits that puncture through the horizontal surface directly below them. Movement causes the minerals to retract and re-emerge in response while creating digging sounds.

|-|Prismatic Beams=<div style="display:none;">'''Prismatic Beams'''<br></div>

*When Citrine or her allies ''inside'' the aurora lands a weapon attack on an enemy, or when an enemy inside the aurora is hit by a weapon attack from Citrine or her allies ''from anywhere'', the Prismatic Gem projects <u>'''1'''</u> light beam for each individual ally onto the targeted enemies. Each beam attacks <u>'''1'''</u> target then induces a <u>'''0.4'''</u> seconds delay before Prismatic Gem can generate another beam triggered by the same ally.
**Eligible allies include other Warframes, [[Companions]], summoned allied units such as [[Specter]]s, [[Air Support Charges]] or [[On Call Crew]], allied [[Invasion]] units, [[Rescue|Hostages]], converted [[Kuva Lich|Liches]] and [[Sisters of Parvos|Sisters]], [[Kavor Defector]]s, and [[Sortie]] and [[Arbitrations]] Defense Operatives.
**Prismatic Gem does '''not''' require line-of-sight with its target to strike them with its beams.
**Multiple consecutive hits from continuous fire, beam, and other rapid-fire weapons will allow the gem to focus its beams onto the same target.
**Multiple beams for different allies from the same Prismatic Gem may attack separate numbers of targets per second.
**Weapons with deployable extensions such as {{Weapon|Azima}} and {{Weapon|Zenistar}} trigger Prismatic Gem when the owner is standing in the aurora or when they hit enemies inside it.
*Each beam deals '''4''' hits in rapid succession per attack, dealing {{stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''500 / 650 / 800 / 1,000''' {{D|Heat}}, {{D|Cold}}, {{D|Electricity}}, and then {{D|Toxin}} damage, with each hit proccing its respective damage type.
**The gem's beam possesses unrestricted range and bypasses all obstacles in the environment to strike its target.
</tabber></div>

*Can be recast while active. Only <u>'''1'''</u> gem may be active at once; recasting will remove the previous crystal and place a new one. 
*Casting Prismatic Gem is a full-body animation that interrupts all movement, [[Maneuvers]], and other actions.
*Ability is '''not''' dispelled when Citrine herself enters an ability nullification zone. The gem is destroyed when it enters an ability nullification zone or when Citrine falls [[Out of Bounds]].
| interactions = 
* Enemies hit by {{A|Fractured Blast}} while Citrine is inside the Prismatic Gem's aurora will extend its {{D|Impact}} and {{D|Slash}} status duration, while the gem sweeps its beam to each impacted enemy individually in rapid succession to inflict one instance of damage and a set of status effects.
* Prismatic Gem contributes kills and assists to increase {{A|Preserving Shell}}'s damage reduction.
| tips = 
**Greatly synergizes with {{M|Galvanized Aptitude}}, {{M|Galvanized Savvy}}, {{M|Galvanized Shot}}, and {{M|Condition Overload}}. Even weapons with low status chance will make highly effective use of these mods when used in the gem's range. 
**Because of its ability to inflict status effects, Prismatic Gem synergizes well with some of the [[Archon Mods]] such as {{M|Archon Continuity}}, {{M|Archon Flow}}, {{M|Archon Stretch}} and {{M|Archon Vitality}}.
**Synergizes well with status-activated weapon arcanes such as {{Arcane|Primary Frostbite}}, {{Arcane|Conjunction Voltage}}, and {{Arcane|Cascadia Flare}}. These buffs will stack and refresh their duration each time Citrine or any of her allies cause Prismatic Gem to fire a beam at any target. 
**Prismatic Gem reacts to weapon attacks made by [[Companions]], allowing for semi-automated gem beams within the companion's attack range.
***Equip a [[Sentinel]] with the continuous beam weapons ({{Weapon|Artax}}, {{Weapon|Cryotra}}, {{Weapon|Tazicor}}, and {{Weapon|Verglas}}), or high fire rate weapons ({{Weapon|Burst Laser}}, {{Weapon|Multron}}, {{Weapon|Stinger}}, and {{Weapon|Deth Machine Rifle}}) to maximize auto-target acquisition frequency by the gem.
***Control the Sentinel's attack range by equipping targeting mods, such as {{Companion|Carrier}}'s {{M|Striker}} (10 meters), {{M|Assault Mode}} (30 meters), {{Companion|Djinn}}'s {{M|Thumper}} (60 meters), and {{Companion|Diriga}}'s {{M|Calculated Shot}} (70 meters). Zoom mods such as {{M|Eagle Eye}} equipped on their weapon further extend this targeting range.
**In cooperative squads, deploy the gem near her allies to assist them with status bonuses and help Citrine secure assists and kills to sustain {{A|Preserving Shell}}'s damage reduction. This is particularly useful whenever Citrine does not have line of sight to enemies from her position to fire her ranged weapon or use {{A|Fractured Blast}} to focus-fire the gem.
***Due to the sheer quantity of various damage per second from {{D|Heat}}, {{D|Electricity}} and {{D|Toxin}} status effects, Prismatic Gem excels at securing the lethal hit on enemies for Citrine.
**Be cautious around destructible objects such as explosive and nitrogen canisters, as the {{D|Blast}} and {{D|Cold}} status effects are affected by Prismatic Gem's status duration buff.
**Damage from {{D|Blast}} status effects trigger extra beams for the affected enemy in the case of fewer than '''10''' blast stacks and also for all enemies within '''5''' meters in the case of '''10''' stacks.
***Note that Prismatic Gem's extended status duration extends the blast delay for fewer than '''10''' blast stacks.
