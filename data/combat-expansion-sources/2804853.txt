'''Pride''' is {{WF|Sirius}}' [[Signature Weapon|signature]] Heavy Scythe. The weapon gains increased [[Status Chance]] upon hitting enemies with a [[Slam]] Attack.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals equal {{D|Puncture}} and {{D|Slash}} damage.
*[[Slam]] attacks grant the '''Coronal Ejection''' buff, increasing [[Status Chance]] by '''15%''' (additive to mods such as {{M|Melee Prowess}}) per enemy enemy hit, stacking up to '''10''' times for '''150%''' additional Status Chance. Lasts '''10''' seconds and can be refreshed.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.
