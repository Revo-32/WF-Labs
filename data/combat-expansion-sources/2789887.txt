The '''Ether Daggers''' are pair of daggers using the same technology as the {{Weapon|Ether Sword}}.

This weapon can be sold for {{cc|5,000}}.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has a {{Pol|Zenurik}} polarity, matches {{M|Sinking Talon}} stance.
*Innate {{Pol|Vazarin}} polarity.

{{Advantages|
*Grants a '''5%''' [[Movement Speed]] bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
}}

{{Disadvantages|}}

==Acquisition==
The Ether Daggers' blueprint is rewarded by completing the [[Stolen Dreams]] [[quest]]. Additional blueprints can be bought from [[Cephalon Simaris]] for {{sc|100,000}}.

Alternatively, its blueprint can be bought from [[Nakak]] (as [[Operational Supply]]) in [[Cetus]] during the [[Operation: Plague Star]] event for {{sc|3,000}} + {{cc|5,000}}.

{{BuildAutomatic|type=Weapon}}

==Notes==
*Stealth attacks do '''+900%''' damage instead of the normal bonus of '''+700%''' damage.

==Trivia==
*The Ether Daggers were released on April 12, 2013 with {{ver|7.7.3}}.
*This weapon was removed from the [[Market]] in {{ver|10.3}}, though it was temporarily re-released for PS4 from Saturday, August 9, 12pm EDT to Sunday, August 10th, 12pm EDT as part of a special alert event prior to {{ver|14}}'s introduction. The Ether Daggers have since made its return as a reward in the [[Stolen Dreams]] quest added in {{ver|15.13}}.
