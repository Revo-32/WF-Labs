==Passive==
{{D|Slash}} procs inflicted by {{WF|Ash}}'s weapons and abilities deal <u>'''25%'''</u> more damage and last <u>'''50%'''</u> longer.
*Damage bonus stacks additively with other [[Status Damage]].
**For example, when using {{WF|Ash}}, a weapon that deals 100 damage with {{M|Bane of Grineer}} will have a {{D|Slash}} proc that deals {{MathText|(100 * (1 + {{M|Bane of Grineer|0.3}})) * 0.35 * (1 + {{M|Bane of Grineer|0.3}}) * (1 + {{WF|Ash|0.25}}) {{eql}} 73.94}} damage per tick.
*Bleed duration bonus stacks additively with status duration mods such as {{M|Lingering Torment}}.
*Works in [[Archwing]] missions. Note that [[Railjack]] {{D|Slash}} status effect {{D|Tear}} has a base '''20s''' duration and is '''not''' a DoT.
