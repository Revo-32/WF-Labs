'''Ruvox''' are {{Faction|Entrati}} fists made into an [[Incarnon]] weapon by [[Albrecht Entrati]]. Heavy Slams in Incarnon Form causes spikes to rise from the ground to impale enemies.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals pure {{D|Impact}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Vazarin}} polarity, matching {{M|Seismic Palm}} and {{M|Gaia's Tragedy}} stances.
*As an [[Incarnon]] weapon, Ruvox has '''5''' evolution tiers with different perks that allow players to customize their Ruvox' stats.
**Once unlocked, activate Incarnon Form by executing a Heavy Attack with a [[Melee Combo]] of 6x or higher.
***When the transformation is ready, the combo counter will shimmer with Void energy.
***Incarnon form lasts for '''90''' seconds and behaves as follows:
****Heavy Slam attacks impale enemies on spikes.
****'''+3''' [[Range]]
****'''-35%''' [[Attack Speed]]
****'''100%''' of {{D|Impact}} damage is converted to {{D|Puncture}} damage.

== Incarnon Form ==

When newly acquired from the [[Foundry]], players must acquire '''100''' kills with the Ruvox in order to gain access to its Incarnon transformation. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below.

=== Overview ===
* Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''90''' seconds.
** Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

**The goal with the change was to ensure that the perk can provide at least a full 2x damage.

*Fixed Ruvox Incarnon’s Damage stat not updating from Impact to Puncture when transformed to Incarnon Mode in the Arsenal stats.
*Fixed Ruvox Finisher animation being unintentionally slow when Transforming.

{{ver|35.5.8}}
*Fixed Eximus kills not counting towards Ruvox’s Evolution II challenge (kill 8 Eximus with this weapon's Incarnon Transmutation) for Clients.

{{ver|35.5.3}}
*Enemies that are already in a ragdoll state will no longer be impaled by Ruvox's Heavy Slam Void Spikes.
**This fixes several scenarios in which enemies could be insta-killed from a combination of the Void Spikes and other abilities with ragdoll effects (such as Nezha's Divine Spears or Hydroid's Tentacle Swarm).

{{ver|35.5.2}}
*Updated the Ruvox Blueprint description to indicate that they also drop in the Armatus Disruption mission on Deimos.
*Fixed progress not tracking for the Ruvox’s Evolution IV challenge (Open X Conduits in Disruption on Armatus, Deimos), making it impossible to unlock.
