'''Sampotes''' is a [[Duviri]] [[Hammer]] wielded by [[Dax Malleus]]. Its Slam and Heavy Slam attacks generate multiple shockwaves, [[ragdoll]]ing enemies and sending them flying.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Impact}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Crushing Ruin}} stance.
*Unique Slam Attack unleashes a line of '''8''' explosions every '''5''' meters, each dealing ? damage in a '''5''' meter radius.
**Specific Stance Combos that contain slams can trigger the line of explosions too. {{M|Crushing Ruin}} and its block+neutral can make it easy to spam this unique effect.
*Unique Heavy Slam Attack unleashes 3 lines of '''4''' explosions in a fan, each dealing ? damage in a '''7''' meter radius.
===Drifter Combat===
*Power Strike (blocking + heavy attack, default {{Keybind|RMB}} + {{Keybind|MMB}}) slams the ground to release a shockwave that travels forward in a line, inflicting {{D|Heat}} damage with guaranteed procs.
**Ground slam can hit the same target multiple times.

==Acquisition==
The Blueprint for Sampotes can be purchased in [[Teshin's Cave]] for 60 {{Resource|Pathos Clamp}} or {{pc|275}}. Purchasing using the former will unlock the [[Drifter]]-compatible version and the blueprint for a [[Warframes|Warframe]]-compatible version; purchasing using the latter will unlock both versions.

Additional Warframe blueprint copies can be purchased from [[Cephalon Simaris]] for {{sc|100,000}}.

==Notes==
*Sampotes can apply multiple instances of [[Lifted]], resulting in enemies being [[Ragdoll]]ed.

===Shockwaves===
*The shockwaves created by its Slam and Heavy Slam attacks have extreme Lifting-Ragdolling properties, making the Sampotes an effective crowd control melee.
*Heavy Slam shockwave damage is unaffected by the [[Melee Combo]] multiplier. Shockwaves can still benefit from mods that scale with melee combo counter, such as {{M|Blood Rush}} and {{M|Weeping Wounds}}.
*Slam and Heavy Slam shockwave damage is unaffected by {{M|Condition Overload}}.
*Both light and heavy slam shockwaves have a base 10.00% [[Status Effect#Status Chance|Status Chance]].
