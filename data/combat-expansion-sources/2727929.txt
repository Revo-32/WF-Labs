'''Life Steal''' is a mechanic that causes a percent of damage dealt to enemies to heal players.

==Mechanics==
Life Steal heals the player based on a percentage of damage dealt when hitting enemies. The amount healed is based on damage dealt after any [[Damage Reduction]] an enemy possesses.
