*Baruuk foregoes his restraints to empower his fists with volatile energies, commanding the '''{{Weapon|Desert Wind}}''' and wielding it as his melee [[Exalted Weapon]]. While Serene Storm is active, Baruuk also receives {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''10% / 15% / 20% / 25%''' [[Damage Reduction]] against enemy attacks, and slows enemies within a '''3''' meter radius by '''50%'''.
**Exclusively uses the {{M|Serene Storm}} Stance.
**Damage reduction is capped at <u>'''40%'''</u>.
**Damage reduction only applies when Desert Wind is the active weapon.
**Slow amount and radius are <u>not</u> affected by mods.
*Rather than using [[energy]], Baruuk regenerates his [[Baruuk/Abilities#Passive|Restraint]], recovering <u>'''4%'''</u> Restraint on activation and then restores <u>'''0.80%'''</u> Restraint per second.
**Baruuk can still erode his Restraint while Serene Storm is active by using his other abilities.
**Baruuk does not need to have completely eroded his Restraint meter to use Serene Storm.  He is free to activate it as long as he has '''96%''' Restraint, and can deactivate it at any time to stop restoring his Restraint if he wishes.

<div class="tabber-borderless" ><tabber>
|-|Desert Wind =
*[[Melee#Normal Attack|Normal attacks]] deal {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''100 / 125 / 200 / 250''' damage within <u>'''1.2'''</u> meters.
**Damage is '''purely''' {{D|Impact}}.
**[[Melee#Wall Attack|Wall attacks]] and [[Melee#Slide Attack|slide attacks]] inflict {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''300 / 375 / 600 / 750''' damage for each strike.
**[[Melee#Aerial Attack|Aerial attacks]] inflict {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''200 / 250 / 400 / 500''' damage.
**[[Melee#Slam Attack|Slam attacks]] inflict {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''250''' damage within <u>'''8'''</u> meters.
**Attacks and wind waves have a <u>'''35%'''</u> [[critical chance]], <u>'''2.0x'''</u> [[critical multiplier]], and <u>'''15%'''</u> [[status chance]].
**Desert Wind can be equipped with melee mods and [[Arcane Enhancement]]s.
***[[Riven Mods]] are '''not''' generated for Desert Wind.
**Because Serene Storm is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as {{M|Steel Charge}} and {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}.
**Radial damage from slam attacks diminishes with distance, does '''not''' have a critical chance, is '''not''' affected by the Melee Combo Counter, and will cause enemies within range to suffer a [[knockdown]].
**[[Finisher#Ground Finisher|Ground finishers]] inflict '''1600%''' of the total modified damage from normal attacks. Prompted [[Finisher|finishers]] inflict '''6400%''' of the total modified damage from normal attacks as {{D|True}} Damage.
***The damage composition of a ground finisher is the same as a modified normal attack; however, {{D|Impact}} damage from ground finishers is multiplied by '''125%'''.
***The finisher multipliers for both attacks are affected by {{M|Finishing Touch}}.
**Each attack adds to the Melee Combo Counter; however, radial damage from slam attacks does '''not'''.
|-| Wind Waves = 
*Normal attacks will emit a wind wave in the direction of aim that travel '''20''' meters. Wind waves have the same base damage as the melee attacks that release them.
**The wind waves and Serene Storm itself are considered separate entities and will both deal damage separately.
**Wind waves will [[Punch Through]] and hit enemies and terrain up to their maximum range, but requires enemies to be in [[Line of Sight]] to deal damage.
**Wave damage does '''not''' fall off with distance.
**Wave range and flight speed are '''not''' affected by mods.
**Wave hits can increase [[Melee#Combo Counter|Melee Combo Counter]] and trigger [[Tennokai]].
**Enemies hit by the waves will suffer a stagger.
**Enemy projectiles hit by the waves have a '''100%''' chance to be redirected toward their originating direction.
*Serene Storm comes installed with its own unique [[Serene Storm (Stance)|stance]]. Additional combos will be unlocked as Serene Storm increases in rank.
**Forward melee combo thrusts Baruuk's fists toward his enemies to generate a powerful push that inflicts damage, stagger, and ragdoll effects. 
**Hold melee combo creates an upward draft that drifts forward and gradually lifts enemies high into the air.
**Block melee combo creates a suctioning pull to drag enemies toward Baruuk. 
**Slide melee combo bends gravity around Baruuk to radially lift all nearby enemies into the air. 
**Slam melee combo creates a radial blast that knocks back all enemies in range.
**Slam melee combo while blocking creates a radial vortex that pulls in all enemies in range.
*Any hits made directly by the impact of Baruuk's fist or the waves of energy caused by Desert Wind, will permanently {{D|Disarmed|Disarm}} the enemy '''only if''' applying any of the following status effects:
**{{D|Knockdown}}
**{{D|Lifted}}
**{{D|Ragdoll}}
**{{D|Stagger}}
