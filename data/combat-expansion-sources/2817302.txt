The '''Sancti Magistar''' is the [[Syndicate]] variant of the {{Weapon|Magistar}}, available from {{Faction|New Loka}}. It has the unique ability to heal the wielder and nearby allies when connecting with a [[Melee#Heavy_Attacks|heavy attack]], and also gives a chance to resist [[Status Effect|status effects]] when it is wielded.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Impact}} damage.
*Using [[Heavy Attack]] permanently grants <u>all</u> attacks from Sancti Magistar '''5%''' [[Life Steal]], plus an additional '''5%''' Life Steal per combo level. Healing is shared with all allies within '''15''' meters for the rest of the mission .
*When held, the wielder receives '''20%''' chance to resist a [[Status Effect]].
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Vazarin}} polarity, matching {{M|Shattering Storm}} and {{M|Crashing Havoc}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

==Acquisition==
*The Sancti Magistar can be acquired by reaching the Rank of '''Flawless''' with {{Faction|New Loka}} and purchasing from its offerings with {{sc|125,000}}. As with all Syndicate weapon offerings, the Sancti Magistar is '''not''' included as a free offering option for reaching its respective access rank.
*Syndicate weapons can also be acquired by [[Trade System|trading]] with other players. Note that in order to be tradable, a weapon must be unranked, unused, with no [[Forma]] or [[Orokin Catalyst]] installed.
*Players must reach [[Mastery Rank]] 8 or higher to acquire the Sancti Magistar, either by trading or through the Syndicates themselves.

==Notes==
*Executing an [[Heavy Attack]] '''permanently'''' grants <u>all</u> attacks from Sancti Magistar '''5%''' Life Steal, plus an additional '''5%''' Life Steal per combo level, for a maximum of '''60%''' Life Steal. Healing is shared with all allies within '''15''' meters for the rest of the mission .
**Affected allies includes all Warframes, [[Companions]], [[Eidolon Lure]]s, summoned allied units such as [[Specter]]s, allied [[Invasion]] units, [[Rescue|Hostages]], [[Kavor Defector]]s, [[Sortie]] and [[Arbitrations]] Defense Operatives, and [[File:IconMissionMarkerDefend(xWhite).png|22px|link=Defense Objects]][[Defense Objects]].
***[[File:IconMissionMarkerDefend(xWhite).png|22px|link=Defense Objects]][[Defense Objects]] will only be heal up to '''500''' health over '''5''' seconds which does not stack with similar restoration effects.
*** On [[File:IconMissionMarkerDefend(xWhite).png|22px|link=Defense Objects]][[Defense Objects]] {{Weapon|Sancti Magistar}} healing will bypass the restriction that prevent any restorative effects if their normal {{stat|Health|Health Regeneration}} is paused .
*When wielded, the Sancti Magistar gives a '''20%''' chance to resist most [[Status Effect|Status Effects]].
**Does not include {{D|Impact}}, {{D|Void}}, {{D|Knockdown}}, {{D|Lifted}}, {{D|Ragdoll}}, {{D|Stagger}}, or {{D|Stun}}. Includes {{D|Microwave}}.

==Trivia==
*The concept for the healing effect of the Sancti Magistar was designed by player (PS4)Sreza and the Design Council.
*Prior to {{ver|26}}'s removal of [[Channeling]] and charged attacks, unchanneled charge attacks with the Sancti Magistar, regardless of mods or crits, used to always be capped off at 200 health per enemy hit. Channeling allowed you to heal beyond that cap.

==Bugs==
*Healing pulse occasionally does not take critical damage and combo counter into account.
