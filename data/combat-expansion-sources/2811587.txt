'''Vinquibus''' is {{WF|Uriel}}'s [[Signature Weapon]] that functions as both a primary semi-automatic [[rifle]] and a melee [[bayonet]] weapon.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Puncture}} damage.
*Equipping Vinquibus will occupy both primary and melee weapon slots.
**Both forms gain an equal amount of [[Affinity]] and will thus rank up evenly.
***Vinquibus is counted as a single weapon in regards to earning [[Mastery Rank]].
**Primary and melee forms are modded separately, using rifle and melee mods respectively.
***[[Riven Mod]]s for primary and melee forms are generated separately.
**{{Resource|Orokin Catalyst}}, [[Focus Lens]], {{Resource|Exilus Weapon Adapter}}, and {{Resource|Forma}}/[[Polarization]] are applied to both forms.
**Does not appear as an available loadout option in [[Deep Archimedea]]/[[Temporal Archimedea]] or [[Duviri]].
*Innate one {{Pol|Madurai}} and one {{Pol|Aura}} polarities.

;Primary
*[[Enemy Body Parts|Headshots]] grant '''2''' stacks (max '''16''') of the '''Vinquibus Precision''' buff, increasing melee damage by '''250%'''.
**Stacks are indicated on the right side of the reticle.
**[[Multishot]] and [[Punch Through]] can generate more stacks.
**Stacks are expended once the attack connects with an enemy. Attacks with multiple hits or connecting with multiple enemies will only consume '''1''' stack.

Alternatively Vinquibus's blueprints can be purchased from [[Roathe]] in [[La Cathėdrale]], [[Sanctum Anatomica]] for {{Resource|Maphica}} obtained in The Descendia. Each component blueprint requires 25 and the main blueprint requires 35, totaling 135 Maphica for the whole set.}}

==Crafting==
Vinquibus's materials are obtained from [[The Perita Rebellion]]. A total of 2,500 {{Resource|Agnovidisc}}, 1,330 {{Resource|Laudavi}}, and 430 {{Resource|Servoris}} are needed to craft all parts.

==Notes==
*Since consumable upgrades are shared between both forms, it is possible to access the Melee [[Arcane Enhancement|Arcane]] slot prior to completing [[Whispers in the Walls]] by applying a [[Primary Arcane Adapter]] to the Rifle. This circumvents the need for a [[Melee Arcane Adapter]], and allows for extremely effective early-game melee builds long before players gain access to the [[Melee upgrade segment|Melee Upgrade Segment]] later in the story.
**While this behavior persists, it does not apply until you sign back in or otherwise re-sync.
*When {{D|Disarmed}} of the Vinquibus, the player will only drop the primary form, but is still capable of using the melee form.
*It is NOT advised to use the {{Resource|Umbra Forma}} on the Vinquibus, as polarities are shared between forms, unless the weapon has been polarized enough times. Since there are no {{Pol|Umbra}} Mods for Primary Weapons, it cannot benefit from reducing capacity drain on Vinquibus' Primary mode.
*The Vinquibus cannot be used in Melee Only missions, such as the [[Gift from the Lotus|Anniversary]] [[Alerts]].
*Renaming the Vinquibus through any open world weapons provider will only rename the Primary or Melee depending on which one you choose to rename. So it would cost 30 [[Platinum]] instead of 15 to rename both

==Trivia==
*When reloading from empty, the Vinquibus produces a distinct "ping" sound as the old magazine is ejected, akin to the famous [[wikipedia:M1 Garand|M1 Garand]] rifle used in World War 2.
*It is the second weapon to be named after the real-life [[wikipedia:Arquebus|Arquebus]], the first being the {{WF|Voidrig}}'s {{Weapon|Arquebex}}.
*When the Vinquibus is stowed in its rifle mode the sugatra will float in the air where it would be as a melee weapon
