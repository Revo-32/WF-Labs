'''Rubble Heap''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Atlas}}' [[Atlas/Abilities#Passive|passive]] that makes {{A|Landslide}} cost no energy, increases its damage, and increases its speed when his Rubble count is above a threshold of '''1400'''.

==Acquisition==
*This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''General''' under {{Faction|Steel Meridian}}, or the rank of '''Exalted''' under the {{Faction|Red Veil}}, and spending {{sc|25,000}} to purchase.
*This mod can periodically be acquired from [[The Circuit]].

==Notes==
*Damage bonus is additive to the one provided by [[Ability Strength]] (equivalent to +100% Ability Strength).
**This diminishes the effectiveness of modding for Ability Strength alongside the Augment.
*Landslide dash range is extended 2x further, targeting enemies up to 24m away.
**Speed is doubled, so travel time is the same.
*Rubble requirement, damage bonus and dash range are '''not''' affected by mods.
*Landslide attack speed and cast time is unchanged.
*Energy cost becomes zero, and no energy is needed to cast landslide.
*Synergizes well with {{M|Path of Statues}}, which eliminates the need to recast {{A|Petrify}} in target-rich environments. This can be used to remove any reliance on your energy pool.
