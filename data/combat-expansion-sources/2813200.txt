{{ArcaneBox}}'''Magus Aggress''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] for the [[Operator]] that, on [[Warframe Melee Transfer]], causes [[Heavy Blade]]s and [[Hammer]]s to deal bonus [[Critical_Hit#Critical_Damage_(Critical_Multiplier)|Critical Damage]] for the next 4 attacks, with a 20 second cooldown.

==Acquisition==
*May appear as a [[The Circuit]]'s [[The Steel Path]] Tier 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, or +11 reward on a weekly rotational basis.
*May be sold by [[Acrithis]] for 10 {{Resource|Pathos Clamp}} on a daily rotational basis, requiring [[Drifter/Intrinsics|Drifter Intrinsics]] [[Drifter/Intrinsics/Opportunity|Opportunity]] Rank 9 for the arcanes to appear in her shop.
*May drop from Undercroft portals in The [[Duviri]] Experience while on Steel Path.

==Notes==
*Bonus is additive to mods such as {{M|Organ Shatter}}.
*[[Exalted Weapon|Exalted Weapons]] don't benefit from the increased Critical Damage but also don't consume charges.
*[[Exalted Weapon#Pseudo-Exalted|Pseudo Exalted Weapons]] don't benefit from the increased Critical Damage but still consume charges after 5 seconds.

==Bugs==
*Works on Hammers despite specifying Heavy Blades.  Does not work on [[Ghoulsaw]] or [[Heavy Scythe|Heavy Scythes]], the other weapons classified as "heavy" by DE.
