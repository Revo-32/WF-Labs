''' Ceramic Dagger Incarnon Genesis''' is an item that is used to add [[Incarnon]] upgrades to the {{Weapon|Ceramic Dagger}}.

==Acquisition==
*Upon completion of [[The Duviri Paradox]] and [[Angels of the Zariman]], and unlocking [[The Steel Path]], players can earn {{Resource|1=Ceramic Dagger Incarnon Genesis}} from [[The Circuit]]'s Steel Path to place on their Ceramic Dagger with the help of [[Cavalero]] in the [[Chrysalith]]. This [[Incarnon Genesis]] form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
*Purchased from Cavalero for {{pc|120}}, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made '''once'''.

== Incarnon Genesis ==
*Installing the Ceramic Dagger Incarnon Genesis requires 20 {{Resource|Pathos Clamp|Pathos Clamps}}, 70 {{Resource|Dracroot}}, and 80 {{Resource|Connla Sprout|Connla Sprouts}}.
**When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s.

=== Overview ===
*Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''180''' seconds.
**Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

==Known Bugs==
*Direct hits of spectral daggers and explosions '''do not''' benefit from base damage sources like {{M|Primed Pressure Point}}, {{M|Spoiled Strike}}, {{M|Killing Blow}} and {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}.
**The '''+100''' and '''+120''' damage bonuses of Gun and Blade and Breacher's Opportunity do not apply either, however the '''+80''' damage from Breacher's Opportunity does.
**{{D|Impact}}, {{D|Puncture}}, {{D|Slash}} and elemental damage mods work as intended.
*{{M|Condition Overload}} is multiplicative to base damage bonuses on spectral daggers, while being additive to them on physical melee hits.
*Evolution II: '''Gun and Blade'''
**{{M|Condition Overload}} ignores the '''+100''' innate damage.
*Evolution II: '''Breacher's Opportunity'''
**{{M|Condition Overload}} ignores the '''+120''' innate damage.
*Evolution IV: '''Red Right Hand'''
**Does not affect spectral dagger explosions.
**Affects only one of the two spectral daggers.
