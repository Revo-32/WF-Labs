'''Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis''' is an item that is used to add [[Incarnon]] upgrades to the {{Weapon|Dual Ichor}}.

==Acquisition==
*Upon completion of [[The Duviri Paradox]] and [[Angels of the Zariman]], and unlocking [[The Steel Path]], players can earn {{Resource|Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis}} from [[The Circuit]]'s Steel Path to place on their Dual Ichor with the help of [[Cavalero]] in the [[Chrysalith]]. This [[Incarnon Genesis]] form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
*Purchased from Cavalero for {{pc|120}}, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made '''once'''.

==Incarnon Genesis==
*Installing the Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis requires '''20''' {{Resource|Pathos Clamp|Pathos Clamps}}, '''60''' {{Resource|Rune Marrow}} and '''80''' {{Resource|Kovnik}}.
**When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s.

===Overview===
*Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''180''' seconds.
**Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

==Notes==
*Clouds do '''not''' force toxin procs, nor will they proc toxin if the toxin damage is changed to something else. 
*Clouds cannot proc physical status effects like {{D|Impact}}, {{D|Puncture}}, and {{D|Slash}}.
*Status effects procced by the clouds can heal with {{M|Healing Return}}.
*Enemies killed by clouds can spawn another cloud, and repeat the process of chaining clouds. 

==Tips==
*While {{M|Lasting Sting}} does '''not''' increase the cloud duration, it can still increase DoT status effects dealt by the clouds, potentially causing tougher targets to take more damage overall and die, creating another cloud.
*Clouds can trigger {{Arcane|Melee Vortex}} and become self sustaining when modded with {{D|Magnetic}}.
**Pairs well with {{M|Furious Javelin}} and {{A|Roar}}.
