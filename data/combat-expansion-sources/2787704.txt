==Notes==
*Damage bonus is multiplicative to the one provided by {{M|Pressure Point}} and other Melee Damage buffs.
**Damage bonus is additive with many [[Universal Weapon Bonuses]] as it gives Weapon Damage, not Melee Damage.
*[[Punch through]] applies to bodies only, but not surfaces of any kind (e.g. it cannot pass through a [[Shield Lancer]]'s shield).
*Consecutive throw bonus refers to "buffering" by holding the melee button before the Glaive is caught, otherwise the buff will be lost. In addition to the Power Throw buff combo counter, the throw animation will also change; the base throw is an open-handed forward throw, while consecutive throws alternate between a backspin throw and a side-flip under throw.

==Trivia==
*Prior to {{ver|10.2.1}}, the mod granted 1.0m [[punch through]] per rank.
*In [[w:Dark Sector|Dark Sector]], another game published by [[Digital Extremes]], "Power Throw" is an ability for Hayden Tenno to deal quadruple damage with his Glaive.
*{{ver|13}} gave Power Throw the ability to create explosions when hitting an object or an enemy on its last bounce. This feature has since been removed in {{ver|26}}.

==Bugs==
*Cannot pass through [[Fusion MOA]]'s Osprey Attack Drone.
*Buff Icon will not show more than 1 stack despite damage increasing up to expected values at 3 stacks like normal.
