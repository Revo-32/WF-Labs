The '''Caustacyst''' is an {{Faction|Infested}} scythe that deals {{D|Corrosive}} damage, and its [[Melee#Heavy Attacks|heavy attacks]] create a trail of sludge that damages and staggers enemies who walk into it.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals {{D|Corrosive}} damage.
*[[Melee#Heavy Attacks|Heavy attacks]] throws a 5m wide {{D|Corrosive}} wave of up to '''30''' meters in the direction of the player's aim. The wave leaves a temporary trail on the ground, damaging and staggering enemies standing in it.
*{{Pol|Zenurik}} stance polarity matches {{M|Stalking Fan}}.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

==Notes==
*When performing a heavy attack, the Caustacyst throws a '''3m''' wide toxic wave in the direction of the player's aim. 
**Wave flies at a constant speed of '''15''' m/s until it dissipates after '''2.5s''', which corresponds to a maximum distance of '''37.5m'''
***Wave range is not affected by {{M|Reach}} or {{M|Primed Reach}}.
**Wave has base damage of '''225''' {{D|Corrosive}}, with '''9%''' Critical Chance, '''2x''' Critical Damage, and '''37%''' Status Chance.
***Wave loses '''40%''' of its damage per second; Wave damage falls off linearly with distance from 100% to 0% between 0 and 37.5m.
***Wave loses '''20%''' of its damage each time it hit an enemy; this decreases the projectile lifetime by 0.5s (range by 7.5m) for every enemy hit from the max lifetime of 2.5s (max range of 37.5m).
***Wave damage is affected by universal base damage increases such as {{M|Vigorous Swap}}, {{Arcane|Arcane Arachne}}, and abilities such as {{A|Amp}} and {{A|Vex Armor}}
***Wave damage is <u>not</u> affected by melee damage increases such as {{M|Pressure Point}}.
**Wave has infinite [[Punch Through]].
*The wave will leave behind a 20x2m trail of {{D|Corrosive}} pools on the ground that last '''8''' seconds.
**Pools deal '''98''' {{D|Corrosive}} damage every second to enemies that stand on it, with '''0%''' Critical Chance, '''1x''' Critical Damage, and '''50%''' Status Chance.
***Pool damage is affected by melee damage increases such as {{M|Pressure Point}}, but <u>not</u> {{M|Condition Overload}}.
***Pool damage is <u>not</u> affected by universal base damage increases such as {{M|Vigorous Swap}}, {{Arcane|Arcane Arachne}}, and abilities such as {{A|Amp}} and {{A|Vex Armor}}
***Status Chance is <u>not</u> affected by mods.
**Corrosive pools will deal damage the moment it contacts the enemy.
*The wave and pools inherit any elemental damage from mods and can proc their own status effects.
*Enemies that touch the pools are blinded for a short duration, making them vulnerable to [[Melee 2.0#Finishers|melee finishers]] (default {{Keybind|E}}) and susceptible to [[Stealth#Melee Damage Multiplier|stealth damage multipliers]].
**Enemies stunned by these pools do not have their alert status changed; enemies can be damaged by the pools and still be killed for stealth kills as long as their stun animation is still playing.
**This allows players to launch a pool, then launch up to 3 more charge attacks before the enemy can become alerted.
**Enemies can be staggered from one pool into another, triggering an additional immediate tick of damage.

==Tips==
*The Caustacyst is an excellent weapon against the [[Grineer]], allowing for {{D|Corrosive}} and {{D|Viral}} to be used at the same time, as well as additional {{D|Heat}} to attack their health or {{D|Electricity}} for extra stunning capabilities.

==Trivia==
*This is the first melee weapon with innate {{D|Corrosive}} damage.
