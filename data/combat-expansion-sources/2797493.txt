'''Dual Viciss''' are {{Faction|Scaldra}} dual sickles used by [[Scaldra Flayer]]s. Inflicting {{D|Gas}} status effects boosts the user's [[Movement Speed]] and [[Attack Speed]].

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Gas}} damage.
*Inflicting {{D|Gas}} status effect with this weapon grants a buff that increases [[Movement Speed]] by '''8%''' and [[Attack Speed]] by '''7%''' for '''10''' seconds and stacks '''5''' times.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Naramon}} polarity, matching {{M|Carving Mantis}}, {{M|Crossing Snakes}} and {{M|Dividing Blades}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

==Acquisition==
Dual Viciss's blueprints can be purchased from [[Minerva]] of {{Faction|The Hex}} for {{sc|Standing}}; 2 copies of the component blueprints for {{sc|5,000}} each and the main blueprint for {{sc|15,000}} to a total of {{sc|35,000}}, all requiring '''Rank 5 - Pizza Party'''.

This weapon is also part of the [[Scaldra Dominance Pack]] for {{pc|655}}.

==Notes==
*Bonus Movement Speed and Attack Speed buffs stack additively to weapon mods like {{M|Fury}} and {{M|Dispatch Overdrive}}.
*Buff refreshes its duration upon gaining a new stack.
*When timer runs out, all stacks are lost.
*Gives only one stack per melee hit, even if the hit procced more than one gas status. Striking several enemies gives one stack per each enemy.
*Activations of [[Truth]] via [[Truth's Flame]] will trigger a stack of the buff for each enemy affected. <nowiki>{{cite}}</nowiki>
