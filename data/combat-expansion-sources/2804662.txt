'''Wrath''' is {{WF|Orion}}'s [[Signature Weapon|signature]] Heavy Scythe. The weapon gains increased [[Critical Chance]] upon hitting enemies with a [[Slam]] Attack.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*[[Slam]] attacks grant the '''Gravitic Slash''' buff, increasing [[Critical Chance]] by '''15%''' (additive to mods such as {{M|True Steel}}) per enemy enemy hit, stacking up to '''10''' times for '''150%''' additional Critical Chance. Lasts '''10''' seconds and can be refreshed.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

Alternatively Wrath and the Pride Blade's blueprints can be purchased from [[Pontis Tower]] with {{Resource|Crimson Talent}} and {{Resource|Emerald Talent}}, awarded from Scoria's Angel and The Kuva Wytch respectively in quantities of 12-16 (18-22 on [[The Steel Path]]) from mission completion plus shooting down targets from the Ramsled/Orowyrm minigame. Each component requires 45 and the main blueprint requires 90, totaling 180 {{Resource|Crimson Talent}} and 45 {{Resource|Emerald Talent}} for the whole set.}}
