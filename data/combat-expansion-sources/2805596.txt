The '''Lesion''' is an [[Infested]] polearm based on the {{Weapon|Tipedo}}. Upon inflicting a [[Status Effect]] on its target, the Lesion gains bonuses to its attack speed, status chance, and bonus {{D|Toxin}} damage, which coupled with its primary focus on {{D|Slash}} damage makes it a dangerous weapon against lightly-armored targets.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Applying a status effect grants the '''Frenzy''' buff.
**Frenzy buff lasts for '''6''' seconds, and can <u>'''not'''</u> be refreshed while active.
**Frenzy grants the following buffs:
***'''+15%''' [[Attack Speed]].
****Bonus is multiplicative to other sources of attack speed.
***'''+100%''' {{D|Toxin}} damage.
***'''+25%''' [[Status_Effect#Status Chance|Status Chance]]
****Bonus is a flat amount applied after mods.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Zenurik}} polarity, matching {{M|Shimmering Blight}} and {{M|Argent Scourge}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*Has a {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

==Notes==
*On dealing status procs on an enemy, the Lesion has a unique feature whereby it gains '''+15%''' attack speed for '''6''' seconds, '''+100%''' {{D|Toxin}} damage, and '''+25%''' status chance.
**Attack speed buff is multiplicative to attack speed buffs.
**The {{D|Toxin}} damage is additive to other elemental mods on the weapon, and will combine with any primary elements (or secondary elements with {{D|Toxin}}) on the weapon.
**Status chance bonus is a flat amount applied after mods.
**This buff does '''not''' stack with repeated status procs.
**This buff duration is '''not''' refreshed upon inflicting procs while the buff is still active. The buff must first expire before another instance can be activated.

==Tips==
*The weapon's {{D|Toxin}} buff synergizes well with {{D|Corrosive}}, {{D|Gas}}, or {{D|Viral}} builds.
*Synergizes well with {{WF|Saryn}}: her passive increases the duration of status procs by 25% (adding to their total damage) and {{A|Toxic Lash}} guarantees procs along with adding even more {{D|Toxin}} damage.
*The attack speed buff along with the weapon's high status chance allows the Lesion to proc multiple statuses quickly, good for limited crowd control and/or {{M|Condition Overload}}.

==Trivia==
*A [[wikipedia:Lesion|Lesion]] is any abnormal damage or change in an organism's tissue, usually the result of injury or disease. It is derived from the Latin word ''[[wiktionary:laesio|laesio]]'', which simply means "injury".
*Though looking quite similar in design and length to the {{Weapon|Tipedo}},the Lesion's crafting component, the Lesion is labelled as a [[polearm]] while the Tipedo is a [[staff]].
*The Lesion has a non-static model; the "jaws" of the weapon close when not actively equipped, will open upon being wielded, and without a Stance equipped, the jaws will visibly "bite" on the thrusting second and fourth strikes of the combo.
