The '''Ceramic Dagger''' is a dagger with the highest [[attack speed]] of all [[Dagger|single daggers]] coupled with good [[status chance]], but has otherwise low stats and deals no {{D|Slash}} damage.

The Ceramic Dagger can be sold for {{cc|5,000}}.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Puncture}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Stinging Thorn}} and {{M|Pointed Wind}} stance.

{{Advantages|
*Grants a '''10%''' [[Movement Speed]] bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
}}

{{Disadvantages|}}

==Acquisition==
The Ceramic Dagger's blueprint can be bought from [[Nightwave]] [[Nightwave/Offerings|Offerings]] for {{Nc|50}}

{{BuildAutomatic|type=Weapon}}

==Notes==
*When equipped without a [[Stance]] mod, the Ceramic Dagger adds a 4th lunging stab to its normal attack combo, which propels the Warframe a good distance forward, which is useful in catching up to moving enemies. It also has a 100% chance of inflicting {{D|Slash}} bleed proc. This effect is lost if one uses the {{M|Pointed Wind}} stance, which replaces the lunge with a sweeping slash.
**If the lunge lands behind an unalerted enemy, a stealth attack will be performed instead.

==Trivia==
*Prior to {{ver|22.20}}, the Ceramic Dagger shared its model with the {{Weapon|Cronus}}, only scaled down and with a much lower-resolution texture. It was eventually given a similar-but-unique model that resembles a scaled-down [[Wikipedia:Scimitar|Scimitar]] in shape.
*The blueprint used to drop from [[Jupiter]]'s boss [[J3-Golem]], however this method of obtaining the blueprint is no longer available.
*Ironically, despite having a curved blade that would be ideal for slashing, the Ceramic Dagger deals mainly {{D|Puncture}} and no {{D|Slash}} damage.
