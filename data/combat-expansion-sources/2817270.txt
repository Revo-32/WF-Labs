The '''Dual Toxocyst''' is a pair of {{Faction|Infested}} sidearms oriented towards {{D|Puncture}} damage. Successfully achieving a headshot with this weapon temporarily grants it increased [[fire rate]], additional {{D|Toxin}} damage, reduced [[recoil]] and [[Ammo Efficiency|infinite ammo]]. However, its initial recoil is very high and initial fire rate is very slow.

This weapon can be sold for {{cc|7,500}}.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Puncture}} damage.
*Landing a successful headshot grants the '''Frenzy''' buff.
**Frenzy buffs lasts for '''3''' seconds, and can be refreshed on subsequent headshots.
**Frenzy grants the following buffs: 
***'''+150%''' [[Fire Rate]]
****Bonus is multiplicative to other sources of fire rate.
***'''+100%''' [[Ammo Efficiency]]
***'''-75%''' [[Recoil]]
***'''+100%''' {{D|Toxin}} damage
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} and {{Pol|Naramon}} polarities.

{{Advantages|
*Very good ammo efficiency.
}}

{{Disadvantages|
*High recoil without the innate buff active.
}}

==Notes==
*Frenzy buff triggers '''only''' on [[Enemy Body Parts|headshots]], and not other weakspots, such as the backpacks of most [[MOA]] variants. Similarly, shooting weakspots generated by either {{A|Sonar}} or {{M|Detect Vulnerability}} in places other than heads will have no effect either.
**Headshots on corpses will not grant the buff.
*The fire rate bonus from Frenzy stacks multiplicatively after any equipped fire rate mods or fire rate bonuses.
*The +100% {{D|Toxin}} from Frenzy is additive with elemental mods, and will combine with elemental mods equipped on the weapon, ex. having {{M|Heated Charge}} or {{M|Scorch|Scorch}} equipped will make the Dual Toxocyst deal {{D|Gas}} upon activating the buff.
**If the Dual Toxocyst is modded with a combined element such as {{D|Corrosive}}, the bonus toxin damage will be added to that element's damage.
*If Frenzy is triggered or if it disappears, this weapon makes a noise that will alert nearby enemies. This noise '''cannot''' be reduced with {{M|Suppress}} or {{M|Hushed Invisibility}}.
*Triggering the buff with the last bullet of the magazine does not require the user to reload the weapon before shooting again because it stops consuming ammo, and therefore is an effective candidate for {{M|Synth Charge}}.

==Tips==
*The headshot buff can completely counter the weapon's low innate fire rate and max ammo capacity.
*If modded with {{D|Gas}}, the gas clouds deals a tick of damage each second, each tick has a chance to refresh the "'''Frenzy'''" buff.
*Zoom bonuses (i.e. {{M|Hawk Eye}}) can make gaining the headshot buff easier, significantly increasing the weapon's damage output and versatility.
*The {{Weapon|Scourge}} synergizes well with these pistols as the speargun can debuff enemies with {{A|Magnetize}}-like fields on their heads when thrown and left to allow it to recast its debuff at fixed intervals.
*The added {{D|Toxin}} damage when landing headshots makes the pistols deceptively effective against any shielded {{Faction|Corpus}} enemies.
*While under the effects of the triggered buff, the fast fire rate and unlimited ammo, combined with the low [[critical chance]] and average critical multiplier make rapid fire bodyshots more viable than aimed headshots.
*{{M|Hydraulic Crosshairs}} can be triggered at the same time as the buff, though the low base [[critical chance]] makes this mod of limited utility without the Commodore's Fortune evolution of its Incarnon.
*Installing {{M|Steady Hands}} completely negates recoil while buff is active.

==Trivia==
*The Dual Toxocyst is the first infested dual pistol added to the game.
*Triggering the innate headshot buff or reloading the weapon will cause the Dual Toxocyst's barrel to open up like jaws, connected by infested tissue between its upper and lower parts.
