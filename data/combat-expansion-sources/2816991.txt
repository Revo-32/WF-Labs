'''Sleep''' is a unique [[Status Effect]] where enemies go into a trance, being unable to perform any actions while they stand still. Depending on the source that applied the Sleep effect, enemies may wake up prematurely before the duration ends.<br><br>
Sleep may also be used as a capture method on [[Conservation]] targets in lieu of using the [[Tranq Rifle]].
