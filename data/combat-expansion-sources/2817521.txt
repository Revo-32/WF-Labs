'''Riven Mods''' are special mods for [[Primary Weapon]]s, [[Secondary Weapon]]s, [[Melee Weapon]]s, [[Archguns]], and [[Robotic Weapons]].

Riven Mods are completely unique; no two are the same, even if they have the same attributes, as their numbers will differ.

'''Warning''': Weapon [[#Disposition|Dispositions]] are rebalanced every [[Prime Access]] release, resulting in ever-changing Riven Mod values. This should be kept in mind when investing in the Riven system.

==Properties==
Riven Mods are unique amongst other Mods in a variety of ways:
*Require the player to complete stated challenge to reveal its weapon assignment and attributes.
*Carry two or three different bonuses.
*Can carry a negative stat, the effects of which can be crippling but which greatly increases the potency of bonuses.
*Can have any of the Madurai, Naramon, or Vazarin [[polarity|polarities]], regardless of its attributes.
*Have their own [[Mastery Rank]] requirement independent of the weapon, ranging from 8 to 16.
*Can have its bonuses (but not the weapon or polarity) [[#Rerolling|rerolled]] with the use of [[Kuva]].
*Are assigned to a single weapon (and its variants) per Riven mod, similar to [[Weapon Augment Mods]].
*Cannot be equipped alongside another Riven mod.

Upgrade costs and Trading [[Credits|Credit]] taxes of Riven mods are equivalent to those of Rare mods.

Players start off with 15 inventory slots for Riven mods, which can be increased by purchasing one slot for {{pc|20}} or 3 slots for {{pc|60}} from the [[Market]], up to a maximum of 180 slots (total cost to buy additional 165 slots is {{pc|3,300}}). Achieving MR 30 earns an additional 30 slots for a total of 210.

==States==
[[File:RivenVeiledMod.png|thumb|A Veiled Riven mod.]]
When a player receives a Riven mod, it is listed as '''???'''. In this state, it will not take up a Riven slot.

Upon equipping a ??? Riven mod, it becomes '''Veiled'''. In this state:
*Reveals a challenge that must be completed in order to become Unveiled. The weapon with the equipped Riven must be in the player's equipped loadout in order to partake in the challenge.
*Will take up a Riven slot. Attempting to equip will prompt a confirmation screen warning this, as well as how many available Riven slots the player has.
*Will not take up mod capacity.
*Will not grant any bonuses or curses.
*Cannot be upgraded.
*Can only be equipped on a weapon of the same class that itself:
**Veiled Rifle Rivens can only be equipped on non-shotgun primaries.
**Veiled Shotgun Rivens can only be equipped on shotguns.
**Veiled Pistol Rivens can only be equipped on secondaries.
**Veiled [[Melee]] and [[Zaw]] Rivens can only be equipped on melee weapons.
**Veiled [[Kitgun]] Rivens can be equipped on either primaries or secondaries.
**Veiled [[Robotic Weapons|Robotic Weapon]] Rivens can be equipped on any Robotic weapon.
**Veiled [[Archgun]] Rivens can only be equipped on Archguns.

Once '''Unveiled''', the Riven's stats, polarity, and mod space are revealed, and can now be upgraded. It also now has a required [[Mastery Rank]] to equip.

==Challenges==
*The weapon used to complete a challenge is entirely irrelevant to the resulting Riven.
*It is possible to complete the challenges using ''any'' equipped weapon or [[Warframe]] ability, i.e. it does not need to be the weapon on which the Riven is currently equipped (however the Riven must be equipped on an item within the current loadout while attempting the challenge).
*You can unveil multiple Riven mods simultaneously during a single mission by equipping a veiled Riven mod on each applicable piece of gear (e.g., one on your primary weapon and one on your secondary). This is especially efficient if the Rivens share the same challenge requirements.
*If its conditions are met, the Riven will be revealed independently of mission success. It will also be automatically removed from the weapon used to reveal it if that weapon is not the Riven's dedicated type.
*Completing the challenges also gives affinity.
*Certain challenge modifiers are listed below for the sake of clarity:
**'''Being alone''' - No [[Companion]]s equipped. {{A|Venari}} is exempt from this modifier as she is instead tied to {{WF|Khora}}'s abilities. Not to be confused with [[Matchmaking#Solo|Solo mode]].
**'''With an active pet''' - Generally refers to [[Beast]]-type companions and [[Hound (Companion)|Hounds]]. Venari is excluded from this modifier.
**'''Having a [[Dragon Key]] equipped''' - ''any'' Dragon Key can be used for this challenge, unless specified.

Rivens are distinct from regular mods in that they are inconsistent in [[polarity]] (randomly chosen between {{Pol|Madurai}}, {{Pol|Naramon}}, or {{Pol|Vazarin}}), [[Mastery Rank|Mastery Rank requirement]] (chosen between MR 8-16), and attribute values, and they have between two and four attributes.<ref>{{Ref|title=This honestly feels worse than just getting an objective downgrade when cycling|author=Steelwrecker|year=2023|month=4|day=3|publisher=Reddit|url=https://www.reddit.com/r/Warframe/comments/12amnxh/this_honestly_feels_worse_than_just_getting_an/|access-date=2023-04-03|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230403192446/https://www.reddit.com/r/Warframe/comments/12amnxh/this_honestly_feels_worse_than_just_getting_an/|archive-date=2023-04-03|extra=Example of two Riven mods with the same name having different stats}}</ref> Two or three of these attributes will be positive, and one might be negative.

=== Naming ===
Riven mods draw their names from a pool of 31 options, indicated by the randomized/rerolled attributes that the new mod has. Names consist of a prefix and/or a core and suffix, and follow "Prefix-CoreSuffix" or "CoreSuffix" pattern.
*Whether a stat is shown as a prefix, core, or suffix is determined by the magnitude of the randomized modifier on that stat. The prefix and core are determined by the stats with the highest and second highest randomized modifier, respectively. The suffix is always the stat with the lowest randomized modifier.
**For example, Vectis Sati-critaata has [[Multishot]] as the highest stat, Critical Chance as the second highest stat, and Base Damage as the lowest stat.

== Disposition ==
{{Quote|Modifies the performance of an equipped Riven mod. More dots indicates stronger stats.|In-game Description}}
The randomized attributes for Riven mods are affected by '''Disposition''', a stat multiplier provided by the system that collates the usage popularity of a given weapon by players of high [[Mastery Rank]] and internal rankings based on the "strength" of a weapon.<ref>{{Ref|title=Riven Disposition Guidelines|first=Connor|last=Neumann|year=2022|month=3|day=28|publisher=Warframe Forums|url=https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1303780-riven-disposition-guidelines|access-date=2022-03-28|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220328171154/https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1303780-riven-disposition-guidelines/|archive-date=2022-03-28}}</ref> Disposition strength controls the range of the attributes available for each weapon. Disposition can be any value between 0.5 and 1.55, but in-game this is simplified to 5 categories:

*<span style="font-size: 1.5em">●●●●●</span> (1.31-1.55): '''Strong''': Assigned to the least frequently-used weapons. Rivens with this disposition provide the greatest attributes.
*<span style="font-size: 1.5em">●●●●○</span> (1.11-1.3): This Disposition is assigned to less popular weapons, Riven mods provide above-average attributes.
*<span style="font-size: 1.5em">●●●○○</span> (0.9-1.1): '''Neutral''': Weapons that see average usage within the player base, resulting in average attributes.
*<span style="font-size: 1.5em">●●○○○</span> (0.7-0.89): Slightly more popular weapons, these will yield a below-average attributes.
*<span style="font-size: 1.5em">●○○○○</span> (0.5-0.69): '''Faint''': Assigned to the most frequently-used weapons, Rivens with this disposition provide the most restrained attributes. '''All newly released weapons start with this disposition.'''<ref>{{Ref|title=Riven Disposition Procedure for New Weapons|first=Connor|last=Neumann|year=2020|month=6|day=8|url=https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1199009-riven-disposition-procedure-for-new-weapons/|access-date=2022-04-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220418160158/https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1199009-riven-disposition-procedure-for-new-weapons/|archive-date=2022-04-18}}</ref>

Disposition is '''not''' necessarily shared between all variants of a single weapon. For example, the {{Weapon|Telos Boltor}} shares the same disposition with its {{Weapon|Boltor Prime|Prime}} variant at 1.20. However, the standard {{Weapon|Boltor}} has a different disposition at 1.30.

For '''Modular Weapons''', the disposition is tied to the Main Component of the weapon, i.e. the '''Strike''' for a [[Zaw]], and the '''Chamber''' for a [[Kitgun]] Secondary or Primary. For Kitguns, the Disposition of the Chamber can be different for Secondary and Primary versions.

Disposition changes every three months, aligned with every new [[Prime Access]]. If a weapon's disposition is changed, the attributes on already unveiled Rivens for the weapon will be automatically adjusted to match the new disposition.

A weapon's Disposition can be seen in the [[Arsenal]] under the ''Upgrade'' tab.

==Cycling (Rerolling)==
Another unique aspect of a Riven mod's attributes is their ability to cycle (reroll) the attributes via the Cycle function in the [[Orbiter#Mods|Mods]] segment in the [[Orbiter]]. A Riven mod is cycled after spending [[Kuva]], found through [[Kuva Siphon]] objectives on planets close to the [[Kuva Fortress]] or as an uncommon reward for completing [[Bounty|Bounties]].

Cycling a Riven mod affects the number of attributes, as well as the final numerical attribute, but not the current weapon designation, mod rank, [[polarity]] or [[Mastery Rank]] requirement. Dispositions belong to the weapons and not the mods, and thus a cycled riven will not have attributes outside its own range of values.

After cycling a mod, both the old version of the mod and the new version are displayed, and players can choose to keep the version they prefer.

Cycling a Riven mod starts off with {{kc|900}} [[Kuva]] for the first cycle and increases on subsequent cycles of the same mod, capped at {{kc|3500}} Kuva. The number of cycles is indicated by a counter-/anti-clockwise arrow at the bottom right of the Riven mod.

==Trait Locking==
Traits can be locked when cycling Riven Mods to prevent them from being lost on subsequent cycles; however, the {{Resource|Kuva}} cycle cost is doubled if a trait is locked. This feature can only be accessed when in the '''''"Cycle Riven"''''' menu and only applies to the instance in which it is enabled; i.e., a locked trait will need to be re-locked each time the menu is opened. Locked traits will be displayed with grey text and [[File:IconLocked(xWhite).png|20px]] icons beside them. A Riven Mod with a locked trait will also prevent the number and layout of traits from changing on subsequent cycles, e.g., a Riven Mod with two positive traits and one negative trait will '''''always''''' have two positive traits and one negative trait until the lock is removed.

==Splicing==
[[File:SplicedRivenExample.png|thumb|right|250px|A Spliced Riven for the {{Weapon|Hema}}, showing the spliced trait as [[File:IconLocked(xWhite).png|18px|link=]]'''Locked''']]
With the introduction of the {{Resource|Riven Splicer}}, obtained exclusively in [[The Icebind]], certain traits can be combined to form new traits that are otherwise unobtainable within the normal trait pool. This feature can be accessed by selecting the '''''"Riven Splicing"''''' option when in the '''''"Riven"''''' section of the [[Mod]] menu. When selected, a menu of all owned Riven Mods with valid trait combinations will be displayed.

Some important distinctions of note:<section begin=Spliceable_Details/>
*Traits used to form a spliced trait are consumed.
**These traits can, however, reappear on the same Riven Mod in subsequent cycles. For example, it is possible to have a Riven Mod with {{D|Viral}}, {{D|Cold}}, and {{D|Toxin}}.
**A random trait will be added to maintain the number of traits on the Riven Mod; however, the layout of positive and negative traits is locked and will '''''not''''' change with subsequent cycles. For example, a Riven Mod with two positive traits and one negative trait will '''''always''''' have two positive traits and one negative trait until the spliced trait is removed.
**If one of the consumed traits is negative, the random trait will also be negative. The spliced stat will '''''always''''' be positive.
*It is possible for a Riven Mod to have multiple valid trait combinations, but '''only one spliced trait''' can be on a single Riven Mod.
*Spliced traits are locked and will '''''not''''' change with subsequent cycles, but are not considered locked traits and will not double the {{Resource|Kuva}} cycle cost.
**Since they are not considered locked traits, a separate trait can be locked.
**The lock on the spliced trait can be removed, allowing it to be cycled off if desired.
*The spliced trait inherits the higher random value out of the consumed stats, rather than generating a new one.
**Community tools for assessing these random values will often flip the positive and negative end for negative traits, making lower values the "positive" end. The true random value of the negative is used when splicing however.<section end=Spliceable_Details/>

==Transmutation==
If a player has excess Rivens and are comfortable with doing away with several of them, a {{Resource|Riven Transmuter}} allows them to sacrifice '''four''' Rivens in their inventory for a new, veiled Riven mod.

Two '''Riven Transmuters''' are acquired from the capture of an [[Eidolon Hydrolyst]]. Alternatively, they may be purchased from [[Loid (Original)|Loid]] in [[Sanctum Anatomica]] for 180 {{Resource|Vessel Capillaries}} each, or from [[Palladino]] in [[Iron Wake]] on [[Earth]] for 10 {{Resource|Riven Sliver}}s each.

The new Riven will always be of the same type as one of the transmuted Rivens. As a consequence, if four of the same type are transmuted (e.g. only rifle Rivens), then the new mod will be of the same weapon class. Upon transmutation, the four selected Riven mods and the '''Riven Transmuter''' will be consumed.

==Notes ==
*Riven mods are not saved in a player's [[Codex]].
*It is possible to complete multiple Riven mod challenges within a single mission, assuming the player qualifies for them simultaneously.
*While it is possible to equip a veiled Riven mod onto Robotic Weapons, the challenge will not appear for the player.
*Typing [Riven mod] in the in-game chat and selecting the mod will let other players in the same chat channel see its bonuses.
**Typing out the name of the actual Riven mod is an alternative way of representing the player's mod in chat.
**Players cannot link Riven mods that they do not own.
*Base (unranked) Endo value of Riven mods is affected by the [[Mastery Rank]] requirement, weapon type, and number of cycles.
*A Riven Mod's chosen weapon is determined upon its ''unveiling''; thus a Riven mod can be obtained, saved, and unveil itself to be for a weapon that did not exist when the Riven mod itself was first obtained.
*Riven mods can be used on variants of a particular weapon, including [[MK1]], [[Prime]], [[Vandal]], [[Wraith]], [[Dex]], [[Prisma]], [[Mara Detron|Mara]], and [[Syndicate]] variants.
**Being entirely separate weapons, Riven mods for the {{Weapon|Mutalist Cernos}} and the {{Weapon|Mutalist Quanta}} are not compatible with the normal {{Weapon|Cernos}} or {{Weapon|Quanta}} respectively, and vice versa. The same restriction applies to [[Dual Sidearms]] and [[Single Sidearm]]s. Several Melee weapons such as {{Weapon|Dex Dakra}} and {{Weapon|Dakra Prime}}, {{Weapon|Nikana}} ([[Nikana Prime|Prime]]) and {{Weapon|Dragon Nikana}} are also considered separate melee weapons.
*Riven mods will '''not''' generate for [[Exalted Weapon]]s.
*If a Riven mod includes multiple elemental damage bonuses, the elements listed are applied in ''bottom-up'' order.
** An elemental damage mod installed ''before'' the Riven mod will interact with the ''last'' element listed, rather than the first. 
** An elemental damage mod installed ''after'' the Riven mod will only interact with the Riven if it has 1 or 3 elements, and will combine with the ''first'' one listed. This applies to the Valence bonus from an [[Adversary Weapons|Adversary Weapon]] as well.
**For example, if a {{D|Heat}} mod is placed directly before a Riven mod that grants {{D|Electricity}} first and {{D|Cold}} second (which would provide {{D|Magnetic}} by itself), it will combine to provide {{D|Blast}} and {{D|Electricity}} rather than {{D|Radiation}} and {{D|Cold}}. Putting said {{D|Heat}} mod directly after the Riven mod will keep the {{D|Magnetic}} damage intact.
*Currently, the lowest Mastery Rank requirement to equip, trade, or sell a Riven mod is '''8''', and the highest is '''16'''.
*The total [[Endo]] gain from dissolving a Riven mod is equal to the following formula:<br />{{MathText|(100 {{mul}} (Riven Mastery Rank - 8)) + ⌊22.5 {{mul}} 2{{exp|Mod Rank}}⌋ + (200 {{mul}} Rerolls) - 7}}.
**For example, the base value for an unranked, unrolled mod with a Mastery Rank requirement of 13 would be: {{Math|(100 {{mul}} (13 {{sub}} 8)) + ⌊22.5 {{mul}} 2^0⌋ + (200 {{mul}} 0) - 7|mt=y}}.
* Since {{ver|23.8.2}}, trading Rivens requires mastery rank 8 or more.
*As of {{ver|24.8}} veiled [[Archgun]] Rivens can be purchased from [[Arbitrations]] Honors for 35 [[Vitus Essence]].
**Archgun Rivens can be equipped and unveiled with Atmospheric Archguns.
*Although Beast Companions had their weapons separated in [[Update 37#Update 37.0|Update 37.0]], Veiled Companion Riven mods can still only become mods for Robotic Weapons.

==Trivia==

*Riven mods feature a dynamic image, which constantly shifts between the images of the bonuses. For example, a Riven Rifle mod that affects Critical chance and Critical damage (positively or negatively) will shift between {{M|Point Strike}}'s image and {{M|Vital Sense}}'s image.
**Interestingly, Riven mods that affect Status Chance use the images of [[Thunderclap]], [[Crowd Control]], [[Stopping Power]], or [[Staggering Force]], as opposed to {{M|Rifle Aptitude}}, {{M|Shotgun Savvy}}, {{M|Sure Shot}}, or {{M|Melee Prowess}}.
**Riven mods for [[Kitgun]]s will use images of Secondary mods even when equipped on Primary Kitguns.
**Shotgun Riven mods that affect recoil will use {{M|Hydraulic Chamber}}'s image instead of {{M|Counterbalance}}'s, as the latter would not be introduced until {{ver|30.5}}.
*''Riven'' is the past participle of the Middle English verb ''[[wiktionary:rive#English|Rive]]'', which means "to violently tear apart". 
*Riven mods for [[Companion]] weapons were made unobtainable in {{ver|24}} but were brought back as [[Cephalon Simaris]]' offerings in {{ver|25.7}} for {{sc|100,000}} each. This was done to reduce reward pool dilution for Sortie-rewarded Riven Mods.
**Prior to this, in {{ver|19.10.1|fix}}, {{Weapon|Artax}} Riven mods were made '''unobtainable''' from [[Sortie]]s. Given the short 5-day period between the weapon's introduction and the hotfix and the low chance of getting a specific Riven mod, Rivens for Artax were the rarest ones in circulation.
*Taking into account the existence of 24 possible positive effects, the possibility of one negative effect, and that elemental damage and [[punch through]] cannot be negative attributes, also the fact that effects don't fall into the same category nor does order matter: there are '''5,083''' possible Riven mod stat combinations that have two positive effects and one or zero negative effects, and '''35,673''' possible Riven mod stat combinations that have three positive effects and one or zero negative effects, making a total of '''40,756''' different combinations for a single Rifle, Shotgun, or Pistol Riven.

== Bugs ==

*If you complete the objectives of 2 different Riven Mods in the same mission they can cancel each other and not open at all, but rather reset to the state they were in before the mission started.
*The unveiling may display inflated values. Toggling the Show Ranked tick box will rectify this.
*The challenge "Find [8-12] Syndicate Medallions" does not count Voidplume Quills dropped by [[Cephalon Melica]].
