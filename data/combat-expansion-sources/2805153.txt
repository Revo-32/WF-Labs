'''{{D|Electricity}} Damage''' is one of the four primary elemental damage types. Deals increased damage to {{D|Corpus Amalgam}}, {{D|The Murmur}}, and {{D|Anarchs}}. Its [[Status Effect]] creates an electric chain that stuns and deals [[Damage over Time]].

==[[Status Effect]]s==
===General===
The status effect of '''{{D|Electricity}}''' damage is '''Tesla Chain'''. It stuns the affected target and deals a tick of damage every second for '''6''' seconds to all enemies in a '''3'''-meter radius.

Enemies affected by an {{D|Electricity}} proc have their bodies covered in blue electricity, with electric lines connecting to each afflicted.

====Damage Over Time====
Each tick dealing {{D|Electricity}} damage equal to:

:<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Electricity Proc Damage per Tick} &= 0.5 \times \text{Modded Base Damage} \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Electricity Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Status Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times \text{Additional Multipliers}
\end{aligned}
}
</math>
::''Additional Multipliers include modded critical multiplier on [[Critical Hit]] and multipliers on [[Enemy Body Parts]]; these stack multiplicatively with each other.''
:''Modded Base Damage is not the same as normal damage calculations, ignoring physical and elemental damage bonuses and calculated as:''
::<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Modded Base Damage} &= \text{Base Damage} \times (1 + \text{Base Damage Bonuses}) \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses})
\end{aligned}
}
</math>

Multiple instances of the effect can stack with each instance having its own timer. If a proc occurs together with a headshot, stealth bonus, and/or critical hit, the damage is also affected by their respective multipliers. 

The Tesla Chain is able to hit heads and other [[Enemy Body Parts|body parts]] by itself. However, Tesla Chain itself has a '''1x''' damage bonus when headshotting. This can be increased with the use of mods such as {{M|Target Acquired}}, zoom bonuses on some [[Sniper Rifle|sniper rifles]], or {{Arcane|Primary Deadhead}}/{{Arcane|Secondary Deadhead}}.

For example, tick damage for a weapon with an innate 100 damage, {{M|Serration}}, {{M|Stormbringer}}, {{M|Bane of Grineer}}, and {{M|Rifle Elementalist}} will be:

:Modded Base Damage = {{Math|100 {{mul}} (1 + 1.65) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y}}
:Electricity Mod Multiplier = {{Math|(1 + 0.9) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y|m=y}}
:'''Tick Damage''' = {{Math|0.5 {{mul}} 344.5 {{mul}} 2.47 {{mul}} (1 + 0.9)|t = y|bf-a = y}}

As can be seen in the above calculations for tick damage, Faction Damage is applied twice, making their effective bonus {{MathText|{{eql}} (1 + Faction Damage bonus)<sup>2</sup>}}. Which is +69% for the 30% Faction Damage mods and +140.25% for the 55% Primed Faction Damage mods.

====Crowd Control====
Enemies affected by this [[Status Effect|status effect]] will also be standing up stunned, preventing them from taking any other action for about '''3''' seconds ([[Osprey]]s, [[Bosses]], and [[Tenno]] are immune to this). Note that only the original target will be stunned, and others around it will only take damage, and Status Duration mods will not extend this stun effect.

===Railjack===
{{Quote|Railjack <DT_ELECTRICITY>Electricity Damage results in a Scramble Status Effect which temporarily disables enemy ship flight controls.|In-Game Description}}
The status effect of {{D|Electricity}} damage against [[Railjack]] space enemies is '''Scramble''', causing enemy ships to spiral erratically in a forward direction while being unable to use weaponry for '''6''' seconds, with subsequent procs refreshing the duration. Against the Tenno's [[Railjack]], it causes an '''Electricity Hazard''' which scrambles the minimap and disables use of the [[Railjack/Tactical Menu|Tactical Menu]].

==Elemental Combinations==
{{D|Electricity}} damage mods can be combined with other elemental mods to create a new elemental damage type.
*{{D|Electricity}} + {{D|Heat}} = {{D|Radiation}}
*{{D|Electricity}} + {{D|Cold}} = {{D|Magnetic}}
*{{D|Electricity}} + {{D|Toxin}} = {{D|Corrosive}}

==Notes==
*Since {{ver|33.6}}, multiple procs on an enemy no longer deal their respective damage separately, like current {{D|Slash}} statuses, but once per second, similar to {{D|Heat}} status. However, they still maintain each own timer and will not refresh, unlike {{D|Heat}}.
*[[Sentinel]]s are extremely susceptible to heavy damage when the player is under an electric status effect, as they are always within range of the arc. On [[The Steel Path]] a single instance of an electric status effect on the player is sometimes enough to outright incapacitate a sentinel companion. 
**Grineer [[Powerfist]] deal heavy electric damage on their slams, stopping natural shield recharge on players for the duration of the electric proc and causing sometimes fatal damage to Sentinels.
