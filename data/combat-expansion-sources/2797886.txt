'''Okina Incarnon Genesis''' is an item that is used to add [[Incarnon]] upgrades to the {{Weapon|Okina}} or {{Weapon|Okina Prime}}.

==Acquisition==
*Upon completion of [[The Duviri Paradox]] and [[Angels of the Zariman]], and unlocking [[The Steel Path]], players can earn {{Resource|Okina Incarnon Genesis}} from [[The Circuit]]'s Steel Path to place on their Okina with the help of [[Cavalero]] in the [[Chrysalith]]. This [[Incarnon Genesis]] form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
*Purchased from Cavalero for {{pc|120}}, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made '''once'''.

==Incarnon Genesis==

*Installing the Okina Incarnon Genesis requires 20 {{Resource|Pathos Clamp|Pathos Clamps}}, 20 {{Resource|Maw Fang}}, and 80 {{Resource|Lamentus}}.
**When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s.

===Overview===
*Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''180''' seconds.
**Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

*Kills create a spectral dagger that orbits the player, then seeks an enemy within '''15''' meters. Daggers deal damage and apply '''10''' forced {{D|Cold}} stacks to the target, then explode in a '''4m''' radius, dealing AoE damage and applying '''1''' forced {{D|Cold}} stack to enemies hit. 
**Up to '''6''' daggers can be active at once.
**Dagger hits do '''not''' add to the melee combo counter.
**Kills from the daggers will not generate new daggers, but kills from status procs created by daggers will.
**Dagger direct hits and explosions are '''not''' affected by melee damage bonuses, such as Incarnon form's innate '''+100%''' melee damage, {{M|Pressure Point}}, or {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}.
***However, direct hits and explosions '''are''' affected by [[Universal Weapon Bonuses#Damage|universal base damage]] sources, such as {{Arcane|Arcane Arachne}} and {{A|Vex Armor}}.
***'''Direct hits''' of daggers '''are''' affected by {{M|Condition Overload}}, while the explosions are not.
****Condition Overload effects are '''multiplicative''' to sources of universal base damage.
**Dagger seek range and AoE radius are not affected by melee range mods such as {{M|Reach}}.
**Dagger direct hit and AoE damage '''are''' affected by faction damage, elemental damage, critical stats, status stats, and most other effects.
**Dagger damage type can be changed with elemental mods.
***Modded elements will not change the element type of the forced cold procs, which are applied separately from natural status procs from the daggers.
**Dagger direct hits and AoEs can activate {{Arcane|Melee Influence}}'s buff, but only statuses procced from the dagger AoE will be spread by the effect.
***Forced cold procs will '''not''' be spread.
**Daggers can benefit from {{Arcane|Melee Careen}}'s damage multiplier against frozen enemies.
