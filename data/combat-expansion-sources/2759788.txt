'''{{D|Lifted}}''' is a [[Status Effect]] caused by certain [[melee]] attacks that knocks enemies airborne and briefly suspends them in mid-air in a ragdolled state. Lifted enemies can be "juggled" with repeated procs, and certain equipment and abilities provide bonuses to Lifted enemies. Lifted procs last 1 second at base, increasing by 1 second for every [[Melee Combo]] tier, up to 12 seconds at 12x combo.

==Mechanics==
{{UpdateMe}}
*Any attack that procs Lifted while an enemy is in a Lifted state will prematurely end the state and [[ragdoll]]s the enemy.
**Any non-melee attack on an enemy in a Lifted state will also prematurely end it.

==Sources of Lifted==
*Some Melee [[Heavy Attack]]s.
*All Heavy [[Slam Attack]]s.
*Naramon's {{Focus|Void Levitation}}.
