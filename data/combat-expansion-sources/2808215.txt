'''{{D|Magnetic}} Damage''' is one of the six secondary elemental damage types, composed of {{D|Cold}} and {{D|Electricity}} elements. Deals increased damage to {{D|Corpus}}, {{D|Corpus Amalgam}}, and {{D|Techrot}} enemies, but reduced damage against {{D|Narmer}} enemies. It is particularly effective at destroying [[Nullifier Crewman|nullifier bubbles]], and its [[Status Effect]] increases damage dealt to [[Shield]]s and {{D|Overguard}}, negates shield regeneration, and inflicts an {{D|Electricity}} proc upon removing all defenses.

==[[Status Effect]]s==
===General===
{{for|the game mode|Disruption}}

The status effect of {{D|Magnetic}} damage is '''Disrupt'''. It amplifies damage to the [[Shield]]s and {{D|Overguard}} of the afflicted target by '''100%''' and reduces natural shield regeneration for '''6''' seconds. Subsequent procs add '''25%''' increased damage to Shields/Overguard up to '''325%''' in total after '''10''' stacks, with each stack having their own duration. Any stacks applied after the 10th will replace the oldest stack.

<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Resultant Damage to Shields} &= \text{Modded Damage} \times [2 + (0.25 \times (\text{Number of Magnetic Stacks} - 1) ) ]
\end{aligned}
}
</math>
::''Calculating resultant damage if at least one Magnetic proc is on target. [[Damage Vulnerability]] provided by Magnetic procs are multiplicative with other damage bonuses.''

Upon breaking of the Shields/Overguard the target receives a forced {{D|Electricity}} proc that deals a total of '''3%''' of the target's maximum Shields/Overguard per {{D|Magnetic}} status effect, to a maximum of '''30%''' at full stacks, over a period of '''6 seconds'''. 
The damage of the forced {{D|Electricity}} proc is determined by the weapon or ability that applied the magnetic status.
*Base damage mods like {{M|Serration}} mods have '''no''' effect.
*Status damage mods like {{M|Pistol Elementalist}} are applied '''twice''', multiplying the damage by '''3.61'''
*Faction damage modifiers like {{M|Expel Corpus}} are applied twice.
*Electric damage mods like {{M|Convulsion}} are applied like normal even if they are combined into another status effect.
*Status duration mods are applied like normal and are a final multiplier to the total damage.
*[[Archon Stretch]] Will apply when the forced [[electricity]] proc from breaking [[Overguard]] is applied but only from abilities that can apply [[Magnetic status]]

Effects that restore shields, such as {{E|Shield Osprey|Shield Osprey's}} and {{E|Orokin Drone|Orokin Drone's}} shield links, will bypass {{D|Magnetic}}'s recharge penalty.

Affected [[Tenno]] suffer additional effects: their [[HUD]]s will be distorted and have '''30''' {{D|Energy Drain}} damage dealt to them per second for '''3''' seconds; The first tick is dealt instantly, resulting in '''4''' ticks in total. {{D|Energy Drain}} is dealt directly to the players energy bar, resulting in a loss of '''30''' [[energy]] per tick, for a total energy loss of '''120''' energy over the duration of the status effect. Status cleanse will stop the energy drain and the HUD static.

{{D|Magnetic}} procs will show blue rings matching the shape of the affected target moving from top to bottom.

===Railjack===
{{D|Magnetic}} does not have a [[Railjack]] space counterpart and is thus not able to proc any status effect nor is it included in calculations for status proc weighting.

'''Magnetic'''

Magnetic has received a few buffs to increase its effectiveness against Shields and to hammer home the power fantasy of overloading enemy Shields.

'''Magnetic Changes:'''
*Damage to Nullifier Bubbles has been increased
**Minimum Damage raised to 300 Damage per shot.
**Maximum Damage raised to 1200 Damage per shot.
***Additionally, we now show the minimum and maximum damage numbers against Nullifier Bubbles, with Magnetic attacks displaying the buffed damage shown above, while non-Magnetic sources will display the original, unbuffed damage.
****Known issue: Clients see the incorrectly unbuffed damage from Magnetic sources, which is strictly a UI/visual bug. Magnetic sources will continue to deal increased damage towards Nullifier Bubbles despite the incorrect numbers displayed. We will look to resolve this in a future Hotfix/Update. 
*Reduced Shield Regeneration by a percentage per Magnetic stack against affected targets.
*Upon Shield/Overguard Breaking a target, the enemy will now be dealt Electric Status damage for a percentage of Max Shields/Overguard per Magnetic stack while also forcing an Electric Status Effect.
*Magnetic stacks on an enemy with Overguard now allow for bonus damage to Overguard, similar to how Magnetic affects Shields.

{{ver|35}}
*Reduced the duration of Magnetic Status Effects when applied to players from 6 to 3 seconds and decreased the total Energy drain from 120 to 90. 
**In addition to general feedback on Magnetic Damage being a bit harsh, the new tileset has many Magnetic Damage sources so the following changes were made to ensure it still had an impact without becoming unmanageable. 

{{ver|31.6}}
*Reduced the Energy drain from Magnetic procs from 50 to 20 per tick. 
**This not only applies to Thrax enemies, but to Magnetic damage across the game (laser doors, magnetic water in the Plains of Eidolon, etc.)! A default Magnetic proc used to drain 250 Energy, now it will do 100.

{{ver|27.2}}
*New Status Effect entirely: enhanced Damage! First Magnetic Status Effect deals 100% additional Shield damage, subsequent Magnetic Status add +25% for a total of 325% (capped). Enemies under a Magnetic Status Effect cannot regenerate Shields. Magnetic Status Duration is now 6 seconds.

{{ver|22}}
*Magnetic Damage Status Effects now drain energy over time as opposed to completely draining all energy.

{{ver|19}}
* Fixed an issue where a Magnetic Status Effect would typically lower the victim's Shields further upon expiry (rather than restore them properly) if it had re-triggered the Status Effect.

{{ver|11}}
*Introduced.
