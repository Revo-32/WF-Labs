'''Ack & Brunt Incarnon Genesis''' is an item that is used to add [[Incarnon]] upgrades to the {{Weapon|Ack & Brunt}}.

==Acquisition==
*Upon completion of [[The Duviri Paradox]] and [[Angels of the Zariman]], and unlocking [[The Steel Path]], players can earn {{Resource|Ack & Brunt Incarnon Genesis}} from [[The Circuit]]'s Steel Path to place on their Ack & Brunt with the help of [[Cavalero]] in the [[Chrysalith]]. This [[Incarnon Genesis]] form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
*Purchased from Cavalero for {{pc|120}}, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made '''once'''.

==Incarnon Genesis==
*Installing the Ack & Brunt Incarnon Genesis requires 20 {{Resource|Pathos Clamp|Pathos Clamps}}, 70 {{Resource|Dracroot|Dracroots}}, and 300 {{Resource|Ariette Scale|Ariette Scales}}.
**When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s.

===Overview===
*Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''180''' seconds.
**Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].

==Notes==
*Spectral axes embed and explode a second time, but this is only visual.

==Known Bugs==
*Direct hits of spectral axes and explosions '''do not''' benefit from base damage sources like {{M|Primed Pressure Point}}, {{M|Spoiled Strike}}, {{M|Killing Blow}} and {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}.
*{{M|Condition Overload}} is multiplicative to base damage on spectral axes, while being additive to it on physical melee hits.
*The '''+150''' damage of both Evolution II perks is '''not''' affected by [[Stance]] multipliers.
**Evolution IV challenge counts 9x combo instead of 10x.
