==Notes==
*When using this mod on {{Weapon|Zenistar}} the range bonus can be triggered both by the disc and melee attacks and the disc will update range without having to redeploy it.
*Range bonus is additive to other range mods.
*Can stack twice, for a max increase of +2m. The stacks have independent timers that decay separately.
