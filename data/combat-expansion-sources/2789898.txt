The '''Ekhein''' is an {{Faction|Entrati}} [[Hammer]]. Its [[Heavy Attack]]s increase its damage and [[Attack Speed]].

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals pure {{D|Impact}} damage.
*Landing a [[Heavy Attack]] grants the '''Heavy Insight''' buff for '''8''' seconds, increasing Damage by '''80%''' and [[Attack Speed]] by '''20%'''.
