{{Codex|The Thalys played a ceremonial role in the Zariman harvest festival, and was intended to reap the first fruits of Tau. Now twisted from this purpose, it plants Void shards in enemy flesh instead. Each new shard planted causes existing shards to inflict damage on their hosts.}}
'''Thalys''' is an [[Incarnon]] ceremonial [[Heavy Scythe]]. In its transformed state, struck enemies are embedded with a Void shard, with each new shard causing existing shards to inflict damage on their hosts.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*As an [[Incarnon]] weapon, Thalys has '''5''' evolution tiers with different perks that allow players to customize their Thalys' stats.
**Once unlocked, activate Incarnon Form by executing a [[Heavy Attack|Heavy Atta]][[Heavy Attack|ck]], a [[Slam|Slam Attack]] or a [[Heavy slam|Heavy Slam Attack]] with a [[Melee Combo]] of 6x or higher.
***When the transformation is ready, the combo counter will shimmer with Void energy.
***Incarnon Form lasts for '''90''' seconds and behaves as follows:
****Attacks embed a damaging shard in enemies.
****'''+3''' [[Range]]
****'''+40%''' [[Attack Speed]]
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity.

== Incarnon Form ==
When newly acquired from the [[Foundry]], players must acquire '''100''' kills with the Thalys in order to gain access to its Incarnon transformation. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below.

=== Overview ===
* Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]], a [[Slam Attack]] or a [[Heavy slam|Heavy Slam Attack.]] Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''90''' seconds.
** Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[Disarm|disarmed]]. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].
** Despite the in-game description only listing Heavy Attacks (and consequently Heavy Slams) as able to activate Incarnon Form, non-Heavy Slams may be used as well and may be preferred as they do not reduce your Melee Combo. This behavior is shared by all melee Incarnon weapons released since Thalys, including [[Obex Incarnon Genesis|Obex]] and [[Destreza Incarnon Genesis|Destreza]].

==Tips==
*Combining {{M|Corrupt Charge}} (or more ideally {{M|Galvanized Reflex}}) and '''Adept Reflexes''' (or [[Ready Steel]]) grants a passive minimum 3x [[Melee Combo]], allowing '''Swift Transmute''' to immediately transform Thalys at will.
*When using a ground finisher to embed a new enemy with a shard, the 12x damage multiplier of the finisher will be applied to shard trigger damage on all previously embedded targets.

== Trivia ==
*The name Thalys may be derived from "Thal", an older spelling of German Tal, meaning "valley", and French "Lys", meaning "lily".
*[[Wikipedia:Thalys|Thalys]] is also the name of a European high speed train service.
