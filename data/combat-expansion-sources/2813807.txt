When a player or enemy receives [[damage]] to their [[Hit Points]], that damage may be increased by sources of '''Damage Vulnerability'''. In other words, players may temporarily deal more damage than the listed [[Arsenal]] value due to [[debuffs]] applied to an enemy, making them receive more damage than usual.

Enemies affected by Damage Vulnerability possess a cracked health bar with a thin, white border.

==Mechanics==
Damage Vulnerability effects multiply the damage that a target receives. In other words, they stack multiplicatively with damage bonuses and buffs, including other sources of Damage Vulnerability.
*However some specific Damage Vulnerability effects stack additively (see [[#Notes]]).
*Damage Vulnerability effects can be conditional such as applying to [[Melee]] attacks only or specific damage types.
*Because Damage Vulnerability applies to the target and not the player, it does not "double dip" in [[Damage over Time]] calculations.

Damage Vulnerability is not same as positive [[Damage Type Modifier]]s which is an innate property to enemy health and vary between [[Factions]].

==Notes==
*Certain Damage Vulnerability effects stack additively instead of multiplicatively. The following effects stack additively with each other:
**{{A|Atomi-Barrage}}
**{{A|Containment Wall}}
**{{A|Petrify}}
**{{WF|Jade|Jade's}} Judgement
**{{M|Prey of Dynar}}
**{{Arcane|Magus Accelerant}}
**{{Arcane|Magus Destruct}}
**{{Arcane|Theorem Contagion}}
*Certain Damage Vulnerability effects only apply to certain damage types:
**{{A|The Vast Untime}} only applies to {{D|Void}} damage.
**{{A|Hakchum}} only applies to {{D|Cold}} damage.
**{{A|Lull}} only applies to {{D|Finisher}} damage.
**{{Focus|Sling Stun}} only applies to {{D|Finisher}} damage.
**{{M|Savage Silence}} only applies to {{D|Finisher}} damage.
**{{M|Radiant Finish}} only applies to {{D|Finisher}} damage.
**{{Arcane|Magus Accelerant}} only applies to {{D|Heat}} damage.
**{{Arcane|Magus Destruct}} only applies to {{D|Puncture}} damage.
**{{Arcane|Theorem Contagion}} only applies to the damage type of the generated orb.
