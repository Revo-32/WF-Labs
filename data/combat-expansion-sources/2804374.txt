'''Damage over Time''' (or '''DoT''') describes effects that periodically deal [[damage]] to targets over a set period of time. As opposed to applying damaging effects upon hitting the target's [[hitbox]], DoT effects will deal damage per tick until the DoT duration ends. A DoT tick typically lasts 1 second and may be applied upon DoT trigger without delay.

DoT damage can be ignored if the target becomes [[invulnerable]] during the DoT effect or if the associated Status Effect is cleansed prematurely.

The total damage a DoT deals from [[Status Effect]]s is indicated by a green indicator on the target's [[Health]]/[[Shield]]/{{D|Overguard}} bar, which darkens if enough DoTs are applied to kill the target.

==Calculating Damage Over Time==
{{Main|Damage/Calculation#Damage Over Time Final Calculations}}
{| class="wikitable"
|-
|
<math>{
\text{Total Ticks} = \lfloor \text{Tick Rate} \times (\text{Duration} - \text{Delay Time}) \rfloor + 1
}</math>
|
*Tick Rate for most effects is 1 tick per second.
|-
|
<math>{
\text{Total Damage over Time} = \text{Damage per Tick} \times \text{Total Ticks}
}</math>
|
*Damage per Tick is affected by sources of Status Damage bonus
|-
|}

For example, when an ability deals 600 damage per tick and it ticks twice per second, the ability will deal 1,200 damage per second. In other words, the ability has a DoT of 1,200.

Tick rates vary across DoT sources. In the example above the tick rate was 2s<sup>-1</sup>, which is a common rate for DoT from Warframe abilities. For {{D|Bleed}} DoT, damage ticks 6 times over a 6 second duration; this results in a tick rate of 1s<sup>-1</sup>, and the same applies to all DoT from Status Effects.

If the tick rate does not divide into the total duration evenly, the total number of ticks dealt is rounded down. For example, with a tick rate of 2s<sup>-1</sup> and a total duration of 3.4s, the first tick will occur at 0 seconds, with each subsequent tick occurring every 0.5 seconds until 3 seconds have passed. The last tick occurs here because the 3.4 second duration ends before the next tick can occur at the 3.5 second mark. As such, the total number of ticks that occurred is 7.

==DoT Damage Scaling==
If the initial hit is affected by the following, a resulting damage-dealing effect will also benefit accordingly:
*[[Buff & Debuff|Damage Buffs]] and relevant [[Mods]].
**This includes for example total damage multipliers (e.g. {{M|Furious Javelin}}, {{A|Eclipse}}), additive damage bonuses (e.g. {{M|Serration}} or {{A|Vex Armor}}), and enemy debuffs (e.g. {{A|Molecular Prime}}, {{A|Rest & Rage}}).
***Weakspots generated by {{A|Sonar}} or {{M|Detect Vulnerability}} only increase damage of the initial hit, '''not''' the resulting proc damage.
*Relevant elemental damage [[Mods]] (e.g. {{M|Hellfire}}, {{M|Malignant Force}}).
**This only applies to <u>elemental</u> status effects, mods like {{M|Sawtooth Clip}} do not buff {{D|Slash}} status damage.
**DoTs of <u>combined</u> status effects (i.e. {{D|Gas}}, {{D|Blast}}) are '''not''' buffed by their component mods (e.g. {{M|Thermite Rounds}}+{{M|Infected Clip}}).
***The only way to buff them is by using literal elemental damage (e.g. {{M|Leaded Gas}}, {{M|Valence Formation}}, {{M|Thermal Transfer}}).
***This also works in reverse. For example: using {{A|Toxic Lash}} with {{D|Corrosive}} mods (e.g. {{M|Infected Clip}} and {{M|Stormbringer}}) '''will''' buff the forced {{D|Toxin}} DoT.
*[[Critical Hit]]s and [[Stealth#Stealth Damage Bonus|Stealth Damage Bonus]].
*[[Status Damage]] bonuses (e.g. {{M|Rifle Elementalist}}).
*[[Melee#Combo Counter|Melee Combo Counter]] and [[Sniper Rifle|Sniper Combo Counter]].
*[[Enemy Body Parts|Body part multipliers]].
*[[Faction Damage Bonus]] (e.g. {{M|Bane of Infested}}, {{A|Roar}}).
**Due to the nature of Faction Damage Bonuses, they are applied once more during the calculation of damaging status effects, even though they already affect the initial hit. 
***For example: a weapon with an innate '''100''' damage modded with {{M|Serration}} and {{M|Bane of Grineer}}, a {{D|Slash}} proc will deal the following damage per tick:<!-- 
--><br/>'''{{Math|Bleed per tick {{eql}} 100 {{mul}} (1 + {{M|Serration|1.65}}) {{mul}} {{text||0.35|hover=0.35 is the multiplier for Bleed ticks.}} {{mul}} (1 + {{M|Bane of Grineer|0.3}}) {{text||^2|hover=Faction Damage gets applied twice for this.}} {{mul}} (1 + {{M|Rifle Elementalist|0.9}})|t=y}}'''
