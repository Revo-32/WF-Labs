{{Quote|'''DAMAGE EFFICACY'''<br/>
'''{{text|green|x1.5}}''' damage to Narmer

'''STATUS EFFECT'''<br/>
{{D|Toxin}} allows damage to bypass enemy Shields and deal damage directly to their Health over a short duration.|In-game Description}}

'''{{D|Toxin}} Damage''' is one of the four primary elemental damage types. Toxin deals increased damage to {{D|Narmer}}, and ignores enemy and player [[Shield|Shield]]s, but not [[Overguard|Overguard]]. Its [[Status Effect|Status Effect]] inflicts a poisonous [[DoT]].

==[[Status Effect|Status Effect]]s==
===General===
The status effect of {{D|Toxin}} damage is '''Poison'''. It applies a {{Tooltip|DoT|Damage over Time}} effect after a '''1''' second delay, dealing a tick of damage every second for '''6''' seconds. Each tick deals {{D|Toxin}} damage equal to:
:<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Toxin Proc Damage per Tick} &= 0.5 \times \text{Modified Base Damage} \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Toxin Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Status Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses})
\end{aligned}
}
</math>

Where '''Modified Base Damage''' is calculated as:
:<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Modified Base Damage} &= \text{Un-modded Weapon Damage} \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Base Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times \text{Additional Multipliers}
\end{aligned}
}
</math>
* Base Damage Bonuses include mods like {{M|Serration}} and abilities like {{A|Vex Armor|Vex Armor's}} Fury buff.
* Faction Damage bonuses include mods like {{M|Bane of Grineer}} and {{WF|Rhino|Rhino's}} {{A|Roar}}.
* Status Damage Bonuses include mods like {{M|Rifle Elementalist}} and the "Toxin Status Effects" option from {{Resource|Emerald Archon Shard|Emerald Archon Shards}}.
* Additional Multipliers include modded critical multiplier on [[Critical Hit]], [[Enemy Body Parts]] multipliers, [[Damage Type Modifier]]s, and [[Damage Vulnerability]], which all stack multiplicatively with each other.
* Note the lack of elemental bonuses in the Modified Base Damage formula. Mods that add {{D|Toxin}} damage only apply to the '''Toxin Proc Damage per Tick''' formula, and a mod like {{M|Magnetic Capacity}} has no effect on Toxin DoT damage at all.

To give a concrete example, a non-critical body shot that procs {{D|Toxin}} against a {{E|Runner}} from a weapon that has an innate 100 {{D|Puncture}} damage and that's modded with {{M|Serration}}, {{M|Infected Clip}}, {{M|Rifle Elementalist}}, and {{M|Bane of Infested}} will be cause DoT ticks that deal:
:Modified Base Damage = {{Math|100 {{mul}} (1 + 1.65) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y}}
:Toxin DoT Multiplier = {{Math|(1 + 0.9) {{mul}} (1 + 0.9) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y|m=y}}
:'''Tick Damage''' = {{Math|0.5 {{mul}} 344.5 {{mul}} 4.693|t = y|bf-a=y}}

As can be seen in the above calculations for tick damage, Faction Damage is applied twice to Toxin ticks, making their effective bonus to tick damage equal to (1 + Faction Bonus)^2. Which is +69% for the 1.3x Faction Damage mods, and +140.25% for the 1.55x Primed Faction Damage mods.

Multiple instances of the Toxin status effect can stack on the same target, with each instance having its own timer. To preserve game performance, a maximum of 10 tick numbers are shown at once.

Enemies afflicted by a {{D|Toxin}} proc have a green sickly substance leaking from their bodies.

===Railjack===
{{Quote|Railjack <DT_POISON>Toxin Damage results in an Intoxicate Status Effect which confuses enemy ships to attack the nearest target, including allies.|In-Game Description}}
The status effect of {{D|Toxin}} damage against [[Railjack|Railjack]] space enemies is '''Intoxicate''', changing the faction of the afflicted target for '''12''' seconds, allowing for '''[[Confusion|friendly fire]]''' between it and normally allied units, with subsequent procs refreshing the duration.

==Elemental Combinations==
*{{D|Toxin}} + {{D|Heat}} = {{D|Gas}}
*{{D|Toxin}} + {{D|Cold}} = {{D|Viral}}
*{{D|Toxin}} + {{D|Electricity}} = {{D|Corrosive}}
