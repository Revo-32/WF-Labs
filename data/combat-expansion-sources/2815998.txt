'''Condition Overload'''-type damage bonuses are a category of bonuses that are activated on transient enemy conditions, such as a [[Status Effect]] which can sometimes be applied, as opposed to an enemy [[Faction Damage Bonus|faction]] which is always applied. The current form of CO bonuses were introduced when the mod {{M|Condition Overload}} went through an iteration<ref>"Condition Overload now uses a stacking multiplier based on how many Status Effects are on the target at 120% damage per Status." - {{ver|26}} patch notes</ref> to make it additive with {{M|Pressure Point}} instead of an exponentially-scaling multiplier. Today, this same bonus is applicable to almost every weapon class, sometimes by multiple sources, but each source is categorically consistent.

;Community Terminology
*''[[CO]] (Condition Overload abbreviated. Used in this article to refer to the Condition Overload Mechanic)''
*''[[GunCO]] (Condition Overload for ranged weapons, e.g. primary and secondaries, abbreviated)''

;Writer(s) Notes
While much of this page may be considered and acknowledged by developers as bugs, this page exists to reflect the present state of the mechanic, as it has existed for multiple years, not been modified for a year, and becomes more relevant as the mechanics are utilized more by developers despite their state.
{{LatestEdit}}

==Sources of Condition Overload-Style Bonuses==
Although some sources are not related to status effects on targets, they are still based on transient conditions and are internally considered the same effect that will additively stack with each other in the list.
*{{M|Condition Overload}} (Melee, +80% per status)
*{{M|Galvanized Aptitude}} (Rifle, +40% per status, stacking up to 2 times for +80% per status)
*{{M|Galvanized Shot}} (Pistol, +40% per status, stacking up to 3 times for +120% per status)
*{{M|Galvanized Savvy}} (Shotgun, +40% per status, stacking up to 2 times for +80% per status)
*{{Arcane|Secondary Shiver}} (Pistol or [[Archgun]], +45% per {{D|Freeze}} stack)
*{{Weapon|Cedo}} and {{Weapon|Cedo Prime}} (+60% per status)
*{{Resource|Dual Toxocyst Incarnon Genesis}} (+33% per status | Evolution 2, Perk 1: Carnage Reign)
*{{Resource|Lato Incarnon Genesis}} (+40% per status | Evolution 4, Perk 3: Carnage Reign)
*{{Resource|Latron Incarnon Genesis}} (+30% per status | Evolution 2, Perk 2: Swift Punishment)
*{{Resource|Angstrum Incarnon Genesis}} (+40% per status | Evolution 4, Perk 3: Fatal Affliction)
*{{Resource|Despair Incarnon Genesis}} (+40% per status | Evolution 2, Perk 1: Fatal Affliction)
*{{Resource|Zylok Incarnon Genesis}} (+40% per status | Evolution 4, Perk 3: Fatal Affliction)
*{{Resource|Soma Incarnon Genesis}} (+40% per status | Evolution 4, Perk 2: Fatal Affliction)
*{{Resource|Kunai Incarnon Genesis}} (+100% vs <50% health | Evolution 2, Perk 1: Swift Conclusion)
*{{Resource|Dread Incarnon Genesis}} (+100% vs <50% health | Evolution 2, Perk 1: Hitman's Opportunity)
*{{Decree|Shattering Frost}} (+80% vs {{D|Freeze|Frozen}}, upgradeable to +240%. Affects non-exalted-weapon abilities, additive to Ability Damage effects such as those seen on [[Violet Archon Shard|Violet Archon Shards]].)

For example, Shiver would additively stack with {{M|Galvanized Shot}}, and {{Weapon|Dread}}'s low-HP Incarnon bonus would additively stack with the [[Shattering Frost]] decree.

{{A|Catalyze}} is not part of the category, as it breaks multiple rules such as being exponential per condition like the original pre-{{ver|26}} Condition Overload mod, and is a radial attack.

==Mechanics==
In-game, this bonus is commonly written as "+X% Direct Damage" in the EN [[localization]].

"Direct Damage" is intended language<ref>"Changed the description of Galvanized Aptitude to better reflect its current function (which clarifies that area of effect damage is not included)." - {{ver|30.9}} patch notes</ref> to specify that ''Radial Explosion Damage'' does not benefit from CO, but this is not completely true. Despite the spirit of this exclusion, many other forms of crowd damage still benefit.
*The following forms of damage apply CO:
**[[Hitscan]] - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Braton}})''
**Hitscan Explosion - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Trumna}} Main Fire)'' only on directly-hit target.
**Hitscan [[Ricochet]] - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Lato}} Incarnon Form) ''
**[[Projectile]]s - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Boltor}})''
***[[Homing]] - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Zymos}} Spores)''
***[[Bounce|Bouncing]] - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Cyanex}})''
***[[Punch Through|Punching Through]] - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Lanka}})''
***Wave Projectile - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Arca Plasmor}})''
**Embedded Cloud - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Pox}})'' only on directly-embedded target.
**Beam - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Spectra}})''
***Beam AoE - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Glaxion Vandal}})''
***Beam Chain - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Kuva Nukor}})''
***Multi-beam - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Ocucor}})''
**{{D|Blast}}, {{D|Electricity}} and {{D|Gas}} procs are generated based on the bonus to the initial target, but carry that proc damage to their radius.

*Forms that do '''not''' apply CO are:
**Projectile Explosion Radius - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Ogris}})''
**Hitscan Explosion Radius - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Ambassador}})'' on every non-directly hit target.
**Embedded Cloud Radius - ''(e.g., {{Weapon|Torid}} primary fire)'' on every non-directly hit target.
**Things that should work but don't:
***{{Weapon|Balefire Charger}} ''(Has no direct-hit damage)''
***{{Weapon|Stug}} ''(Despite embeds, is entirely "radial" damage)''
***{{Weapon|Ferrox}} family embedded alt-fire ''(Pulsing radius with 2s intervals instead of 1s)''
***{{Weapon|Azima}} turret ''(Should qualify as Hitscan. but not affected)''
***{{Weapon|Sonicor}} ''(Should qualify as wave, but not affected)''

====Math====
We can group the behaviors from the previous section into two categories based on how they stack with other damage bonuses:
*Behavior #1: "'''Multiplying'''". A multiplicative bonus that '''additively stacks''' with only CO-like damage bonuses (e.g. {{M|Galvanized Aptitude}}) AND '''multiplicatively stacks''' with other damage bonuses (e.g. {{M|Serration}}, {{A|Vex Armor}}, {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}). Its functionality is reliable and compatible with all weapon bonuses. This is the least common way on how CO-like effects stack with damage bonuses.
**'''Base Weapon Damage {{mul}} Most Damage Bonuses {{mul}} CO {{mul}} Other Damage Multipliers'''
**Example with {{M|Serration}} and 2 procs/1 stack of {{M|Galvanized Aptitude}} bonus:
***'''Final Damage = Base Weapon Damage {{mul}} (1 + {{M|Serration|1.65}}) {{mul}} (1 + {{M|Galvanized Aptitude|0.4}} + {{M|Galvanized Aptitude|0.4}})'''
<!--Mechanics below confirmed as of 2025-11-03-->
*Behavior #2/3/4: "'''Adding'''". A multiplicative bonus that '''additively stacks''' with ALL +% Damage mods, damage bonuses, AND CO-like damage bonuses (e.g. {{M|Serration}}, {{A|Vex Armor}}, {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}, {{M|Galvanized Aptitude}}). This type is an additive recalculation of all effects, but omits +X% Damage in the recalculation, thus making CO mathematically additive to +X% Damage. However, this recalculation inadvertently omits some other effects. This is the most common way on how CO-like effects stack with damage bonuses.
**'''(Base Weapon Damage {{mul}} Most Damage Bonuses {{mul}} Other Damage Multipliers) + (Base Weapon Damage {{mul}} CO )'''
***Or '''Base Weapon Damage {{mul}} [ (Most Damage Bonuses {{mul}} Other Damage Multipliers) + CO ]'''
***Damage multipliers or effects that are ignored with Additive Stacking CO-like bonuses:
****Final Damage Multipliers
*****[[Dragon Key#Variants|Extinguished Dragon Key]]
*****[[Damage Falloff#Range Falloff|Range-based Damage Falloff]] (but not Beam ramp-up)
*****{{Arcane|Longbow Sharpshot}}
*****{{Arcane|Primary Compression}} on projectile weapons
*****{{WF|Equinox}}'s {{M|Duality}}
*****{{WF|Excalibur}}'s {{M|Furious Javelin}}
*****{{WF|Garuda}}'s [[Garuda/Abilities/Passive|Passive]]
*****{{WF|Mirage}}'s {{A|Eclipse}}
*****{{WF|Mirage}}'s {{A|Hall of Mirrors}} damage modifier
*****{{WF|Nidus}}' {{A|Parasitic Link}}
*****{{WF|Vauban}}'s {{A|Minelayer|Overdriver}}
****Base Damage increases from [[Incarnon#Genesis|Incarnon Genesis Evolutions]].
****Some Melee [[Stance]] Multipliers, on inputs with multiple hits.
****Some natural weapon stats modifiers, such as [[Bow]] charging and more.<!--
**Example with {{M|Serration}} and 2 stacks of {{M|Galvanized Aptitude}} bonus:
***'''Final Damage = Base Weapon Damage {{mul}} (1 + {{M|Serration|1.65}})'''
-->

===What Counts As A Status Effect?===
All [[Status Effect]]s that have a damage type equivalent are counted for purposes of calculating the CO damage bonus. For thoroughness, this includes:
*Status effects from physical damage types
**{{D|Impact}} procs
**{{D|Puncture}} procs
**{{D|Slash}} procs
*Status effects from elemental damage types (single and combined types)
**{{D|Cold}} procs
**{{D|Electricity}} procs
**{{D|Heat}} procs
**{{D|Toxin}} procs
**{{D|Blast}} procs
**{{D|Corrosive}} procs
**{{D|Gas}} procs
**{{D|Magnetic}} procs
**{{D|Radiation}} procs
**{{D|Viral}} procs
*Status effects from unique damage types
**{{D|Void}} procs
**{{D|Tau}} procs
*Unique "hidden" status effects
**{{D|Lifted}} (cannot coexist with {{D|Knockdown}} on same target)
**{{D|Knockdown}} (cannot coexist with {{D|Lifted}} on same target)
**{{D|Microwave}}

*Changed the description of Galvanized Aptitude to better reflect its current function [which clarifies that area of effect damage is not included]
**... BUT WAIT! We want to explain why because it’s very important to us that everyone understands where we are coming from with our current design mindset. AoE Weapons are the dominant ones by every usage metric. We see this day after day. Having this bonus apply to the AoE instance felt dangerously close to a myopic choice concerning powering up player Arsenals that simply do not need it. This mod never worked on AoE, and the description now explains that to avoid confusion. We understand those seeking a different outcome will disagree with this choice, but ultimately we are not willing to further bolster AoE at this time. This is due to the increasing difficulty in creating content that serves to challenge the Tenno.
