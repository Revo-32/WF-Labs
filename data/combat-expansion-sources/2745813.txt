'''Galvanized Mods''' are alternative versions of some [[mod]]s. They have slightly lower base stats and higher maximum drain than their original counterparts, but provide additional stacking buffs from killing enemies.

==Acquisition==
Each mod can be purchased at rank 0 from the [[Arbitrations]] vendor for '''20 {{Resource|Vitus Essence}}''' in the [[Arbiters of Hexis]] room in any [[Relay]] (regardless of the player's personal standing with the Arbiters of Hexis). All of these mods can be [[Trading|traded]] between players.

==Notes==
*Cannot be equipped with their other counterparts (e.g. {{M|Galvanized Diffusion}} cannot be equipped with {{M|Barrel Diffusion}} or {{M|Amalgam Barrel Diffusion}}).
* In most Galvanized mods when their buffs time out, only ''one'' stack is lost and the buff duration resets.
**{{M|Galvanized Scope}} and {{M|Galvanized Crosshairs}} behave differently in that each stack has its own duration.
*Only kills from a weapon equipped with a Galvanized mod will give stacks, and only the weapon equipped with said mod will benefit from the buff. If the same mod is equipped on both a normal weapon and an [[Exalted Weapon]] (such as {{WF|Mesa}}'s {{Weapon|Regulators}} or {{WF|Hildryn}}'s {{Weapon|Balefire Charger}}), it will affect both weapons.
**However, for some weapons the buffs are maintained separately with only the most recent application visible; equipping [[Galvanized Reflex]] on both [[Gara]]'s [[Shattered Lash (Weapon)|Shattered Lash]] and a normal melee weapon will result in either weapon only benefiting from their own stacks. The same applies to [[Galvanized Steel]].
*Kills from status procs (including from {{M|Hunter Munitions}}) from a weapon equipped with a Galvanized mod will give stacks. 
** However, kills from status procs on the target's head do '''not''' count as a headshot kill, with the exception of {{D|Electricity}} and {{D|Gas}}.
*The shotgun mod {{M|Galvanized Acceleration}} currently has no rifle or pistol counterpart (for {{M|Terminal Velocity}} and {{M|Lethal Momentum}}, respectively). In the same manner, shotguns do not have a Galvanized version of {{M|Laser Sight}}, which the other two ranged weapons have (for {{M|Argon Scope}} and {{M|Hydraulic Crosshairs}}, respectively).
*[[Robotic|Robotic companions]] may also make use of Galvanized mods on their equipped weapons. However, the player has to build stacks for the mods on the companion's behalf; for example, if the companion's weapon is using {{M|Galvanized Chamber}}, the player will need to use a rifle equipped with the same mod and achieve a kill for the companion to receive a stack.
