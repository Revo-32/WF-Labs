'''{{D|True}} Damage''' is a hidden, unique damage type which has no [[Damage Type Modifier|damage modifier]] against any [[Factions|faction]]. This damage type ignores [[armor]]'s [[Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]. It is primarily available through some [[Warframes|Warframe]] abilities.

==Mechanics==
*{{D|True}} Damage can not be ''directly'' added to a weapon through mods.
**The {{Weapon|Basmu}} is currently the only weapon that deals it innately: fully depleting the weapon's magazine produces three '''10'''-meter pulses that deals up to '''10''' {{D|True}} Damage, staggers nearby enemies and heals the user's health by '''10x''' the damage done to each enemy per pulse.
*{{D|True}} Damage is not increased by buffs to physical or elemental damage.
* Due to its [[armor]]-ignoring properties, {{D|True}} Damage is very useful against high-level [[Grineer]] and most [[Boss]]es.
* [[Sentient]] enemies do not adapt to {{D|True}} Damage.
*{{D|True}} Damage does not bypass [[Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] provided by sources that are not armor.

==Status Effects ==
{{D|True}} Damage does not possess any applicable status procs on its own.

==Sources of True Damage==
*{{Weapon|Basmu}}'s 10 meter healing pulse.
