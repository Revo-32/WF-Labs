''' Skana Incarnon Genesis''' is an item that is used to add [[Incarnon]] upgrades to the {{Weapon|Skana}}, {{Weapon|Prisma Skana}} or {{Weapon|Skana Prime}}.
==Acquisition==
*Upon completion of [[The Duviri Paradox]] and [[Angels of the Zariman]], and unlocking [[The Steel Path]], players can earn {{Resource|1=Skana Incarnon Genesis}} from [[The Circuit]]'s Steel Path to place on their Skana with the help of [[Cavalero]] in the [[Chrysalith]]. This [[Incarnon Genesis]] form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
*Purchased from Cavalero for {{pc|120}}, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made '''once'''.

==Incarnon Genesis==

*Installing the Skana Incarnon Genesis requires '''20''' {{Resource|Pathos Clamp|Pathos Clamps}}, '''70''' {{Resource|Dracroot}}, and '''80''' {{Resource|Connla Sprout|Connla Sprouts}}.
**When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 [[affinity]] to the weapon, affected by [[Affinity Booster]]s.

===Overview===
*Incarnon Form is charged by building [[Melee Combo]] to 6x, then using a [[Heavy Attack]]. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for '''180''' seconds.
**Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [[bleedout]], or being [[disarm]]ed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon [[death]].
