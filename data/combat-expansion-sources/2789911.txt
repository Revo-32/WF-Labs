'''Harmony''' is {{WF|Jade}}'s [[Signature Weapon|signature]] scythe and counterpart to [[Stalker]]'s {{Weapon|Hate}}. [[Heavy Attack]]s detonate damage over time [[Status Effect]]s to instantly inflict their full damage, while Heavy Attack kills grant [[Status Duration]].

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Puncture}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Naramon}} polarity, matching {{M|Reaping Spiral}} and {{M|Shadow Harvest}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*Enemies hit by Heavy Attacks while debuffed with active {{D|Slash}}, {{D|Heat}}, {{D|Toxin}}, {{D|Electricity}}, and {{D|Gas}} [[Status Effect]]s will instantly receive the full damage from all damage ticks of the procs; status effects are removed after inflicting damage.
**Effect also applies to procs generated by the Heavy Attack itself.
*Heavy Attack kills grant '''Harmonic Resonance''', increasing [[Status Duration]] by '''30%''' for '''30''' seconds to all allies within [[Affinity Range]].
**When combined with the effects of {{Weapon|Evensong}}, and {{Weapon|Cantare}}, all three buffs merge into '''Jade's Ensemble''', which lasts '''60''' seconds and increases the Status Duration bonus to '''60%'''.
**Does not affect [[Companion|Companions]].

==Notes==
*Harmony "detonates" status procs immediately after it does damage, including any procs caused by the heavy attack itself hitting.
*Harmonic Resonance is a [[Universal Weapon Bonuses|universal status duration buff]] that also applies to warframe and companion abilities.

==Tips==
*Adding Status Duration will increase damage dealt on heavy attacks due to increasing how much total damage is condensed into a single hit.
**{{WF|Ash}} and {{WF|Saryn}} will deal increased damage on heavy attacks by default, as their passives grant {{D|Slash}}/Status duration boosts respectively.
**{{WF|Citrine}}'s {{A|Prismatic Gem}} will massively increase Status Duration.
**{{WF|Lavos}} can increase Status Duration on his weapons via [[Ability Duration]].
*{{WF|Garuda}}'s {{A|Seeking Talons}} causes a multitude of {{D|Slash}} procs for Harmony to utilize.
*{{WF|Koumei}}'s {{A|Kumihimo}} and {{A|Bunraku}} can inflict several different procs in an area, though this includes non-damaging ones.
*{{Arcane|Melee Influence}} and {{Arcane|Melee Afflictions}} can spread a lot of damaging status effects very quickly.
*Tennokai can be used to first prime enemies with status effects, then detonate those and apply + detonate the slash proc from the heavy attack without spending your combo.
