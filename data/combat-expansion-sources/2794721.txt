The '''Azothane''' is a [[Duviri]] [[Two-Handed Nikana]] wielded by [[Dax Equitem]]s. It has a unique [[Blocking]] combo that emits a {{D|Heat}} shockwave around the user.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Wise Razor}} stance.
*Unique Blocking Combo releases a '''10''' meter shockwave centered around the impact point, dealing '''85''' {{D|Heat}} damage with a guaranteed {{D|Impact}} proc.
===Drifter Combat===
*Power Strike (blocking + heavy attack, default {{Keybind|RMB}} + {{Keybind|MMB}}) plunges the blade into the ground to release five energy waves forward in a cone, dealing {{D|Cold}} damage with guaranteed procs.
**This indirectly increases the subsequent coming crit damage as Cold status enhances the critical multipliers received on the enemies.

{{Advantages|
*Tied with {{Weapon|War}} for the longest reach of all non-exalted melee weapons.
}}
{{Disadvantages|}}

==Acquisition==
The Blueprint for Azothane can be purchased in [[Teshin's Cave]] for 50 {{Resource|Pathos Clamp}} or {{pc|250}}. Purchasing using the former will unlock the [[Drifter]]-compatible version and the blueprint for a [[Warframes|Warframe]]-compatible version; purchasing using the latter will unlock both versions.

Additional Warframe-compatible blueprint copies can be purchased from [[Cephalon Simaris]] for {{sc|100,000}}.

==Notes==
===Shockwave===
*For each enemy hit, the shockwave will add 4 hits to the [[Melee Combo]]
*Shockwave damage is 50% of the '''Slam Radial Damage''' listed under the Primary section of the Arsenal.
*Shockwave does '''not''' have any damage fall-off.
*Damage is only affected sources of slam damage, such as {{M|Seismic Wave}}.

==Tips==

* The Azothane's shockwave passive will proc {{M|Tek Gravity}}.
