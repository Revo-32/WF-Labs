'''Hysteria''' is a unique [[Stance]] exclusive to {{Weapon|Valkyr Talons}} that can only be acquired and used by activating {{WF|Valkyr}}'s fourth ability, {{A|Hysteria}}. This Stance overrides the Stance on any currently equipped melee weapon for as long as Hysteria remains active.
<section end=intro />

{{StanceTable|Name=Hysteria}}

==Acquisition==
*Hysteria is a [[:Category:Special Stance|unique stance]] which can only be temporarily obtained through the use of {{WF|Valkyr}}'s {{A|Hysteria}}. There are no other possible methods to obtain this stance.

==Notes==
*During this stance mode, Valkyr has increased mobility mid-air, performing continuous aerial somersaults.
*The final four attacks of '''Fervor''' have Valkyr swipe around a full 360 degrees, allowing her to attack enemies around without needing to change directions. Due to Hysteria's high power nature, this can clear tightly-knit crowds quickly.
*Unlike other weapons that use this slide attack, '''Launching Spring''' makes Valkyr spin quickly 3 to 4 times before delivering an uppercut, which can deal up to a 5-hit combo against a single target, or 4 hits against multiple enemies.
*Unlike '''One Point''' utilized by other fist-using stances ([[Fist]]s, [[Claws]] and [[Sparring]]), '''Frenzy''' accelerates Valkyr downwards instead of letting her float in the air as she waits to strike the ground.
*Some attacks do bonus {{D|Impact}} damage.
**The first attack of '''Rise From Ashes''' does '''200%''' bonus {{D|Impact}} damage
**'''Roaring Drums''' does '''25%''' bonus {{D|Impact}} damage.
*Some attacks have increased [[Combo Count]]:
**'''Fervor''': 3rd through 5th hits grant 2 Combo and the 6th hit grants 3 Combo
**'''Delirium''': 3rd and 4th hits grant 2 Combo
**'''Rage''': 1st hit grants 2 Combo and 2nd through 4th hits grants 3 Combo
**'''Madness''': 1st through 4th hits grants 2 Combo and 5th and 6th hits grants 3 Combo
*This stance can not be ranked up manually and instead ranks up with the ability rank of {{A|Hysteria}}. There is also no benefit to ranking up the stance.

==Trivia==
*The stance was previously categorized under "{{Weapon|Furax}}" instead of "Valkyr Talons".
*Hysteria is one of five identified stance mods that are exclusive to a Warframe instead of a weapon type, the other four being {{WF|Excalibur}}'s {{A|Exalted Blade}}, {{WF|Wukong}}'s {{A|Primal Fury}}, {{WF|Baruuk}}'s {{A|Serene Storm}}, and {{WF|Sevagoth}}'s {{A|Exalted Shadow}}.
*Oddly enough, before {{ver|14.0.6}}, the mod card appeared to be a background-less version of {{M|Voltaic Strike}}. This was changed as of {{ver|14.0.6}}, where the image was replaced with a grey-grid.
*Although having lost characteristics where Valkyr would float mid-air when attacking during Hysteria pre-Update 14, if a player attacks off a downward slope, Hysteria will momentarily let Valkyr attack regularly while in mid-air, followed shortly with '''Frenzy'''.
