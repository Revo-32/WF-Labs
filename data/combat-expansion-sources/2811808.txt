'''Jet Stream''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Zephyr}} that grants {{A|Turbulence}} the ability to provide additional [[Movement Speed]] and [[Projectile Speed]] for {{WF|Zephyr}} and nearby allies.

==Acquisition==
*This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''Exalted''' under the {{Faction|Red Veil}}, or the rank of '''Flawless''' under {{Faction|New Loka}}, and spending {{sc|25,000}} to purchase.

==Notes==
*Both movement speed and weapon [[Projectile Speed]] are affected by [[Ability Strength]].
*Buff to allies is affected by [[Ability Range]].
*Players retain the Jet Stream buff for an additional '''3''' seconds after leaving {{A|Turbulence}}'s range.
*The charged attacks of thrown melee weapons like the {{Weapon|Glaive}} benefit from the [[Projectile Speed]] increase.
*The [[Projectile Speed]] increase affects the damage falloff distance of weapons that have it, such as shotguns, similarly to [[Projectile Speed]] mods.
*
