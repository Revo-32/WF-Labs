*Awarded for killing [[Whisper]]s in [[Albrecht's Laboratories]] missions.

*Can be bought from [[Bird 3]] of {{Faction|Cavia}} for {{sc|7,500}}, requiring '''Rank 4 - Scholar'''.}}

==Notes==
*[[Critical Chance]] bonus is additive to mods like {{M|True Steel}}.
*Only provides '''1''' stack per melee input, regardless of how many enemies are hit.
*At all ranks, it requires '''10''' hits to reach the maximum critical chance bonus listed.
*Only consumes stacks if the heavy attack connects with an enemy. Whiffing a heavy attack will preserve the critical chance bonus.
*The bonus only procs on normal melee attacks. Slams and heavy attacks do not.

==Bugs==
*[[Glaive (Weapon Type)]] throws count as heavy attacks, <u>ground throws</u> '''will''' benefit from the bonus but will '''not''' consume stacks.
**Same applies to explosions that do not use the heavy attack input (e.g. {{M|Volatile Rebound}}).
