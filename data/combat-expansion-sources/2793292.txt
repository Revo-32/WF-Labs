{{ArcaneBox}}
'''Exodia Might''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] for [[Zaw]]s that has a 50% chance to grant [[Life Steal]] on [[Finisher]]s for 8 seconds.

==Effect==
{| class="emodtable"
|-
! Rank
! Life Steal
|-
| 0 || 7.5%
|-
| 1 || 15%
|-
| 2 || 22.5%
|-
| 3 || 30%
|}

==Acquisition==
Can be bought from [[Hok]] for {{sc|10,000}} per arcane upon reaching the rank of '''Kin''' with the [[Ostron]]s.

==Notes==
*While the buff is active, only melee damage will siphon health. This includes damage over time status effects such as {{D|Slash}}, {{D|Toxin}}, {{D|Gas}}, and {{D|Heat}} status effects.
*Ground finishers will not grant life steal buff.
*The initial finisher that triggers the buff will only grant healing if the player is the host.
*Synergizes well with {{M|Dizzying Rounds}} as close range finishers will be guaranteed after hitting an enemy with the augment-modded {{Weapon|Bronco}}/{{Weapon|Bronco Prime}}.

We’ve adjusted the values of older Arcanes with the goal of making lower rank Arcanes more viable, and more desirable while collecting as a result. In short, there are three stats that each Arcane tends to have (speaking generally) -- for example, a percentage chance, a stat buff, and the duration of the buff. 

Certain Arcanes increase all of these stats per rank (making them scale exponentially), but we are changing it so that only one stat is affected by rank (making it scale linearly). The other stats will now be at the max-rank value regardless of the Arcane Rank, thereby buffing lower-rank Arcanes as a result.
