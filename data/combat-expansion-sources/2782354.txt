'''Amanata Pressure''' is a [[Weapon Augments#Weapon Augment Mods|Weapon Augment Mod]] variant of {{M|Pressure Point}} for the {{Weapon|Amanata}} applies a random Status Effect to enemies upon Heavy Attacks when reaching 8x [[Melee Combo|combo]], as well as adding additional Melee Damage.

==Acquisition==
*'''Amanata Pressure''' can be obtained by purchasing it from [[Koumei's Shrine]] on [[Cetus]], [[Earth]] for 150 {{Resource|Fate Pearl}}. The mod will only appear in the shop if the player has access to [[The Steel Path]].

==Notes==
*Cannot be equipped alongside {{M|Pressure Point}}, {{M|Primed Pressure Point}}, or {{M|Sacrificial Pressure}}.
*The additional random status effect is only applied to the first enemy hit during the heavy attack animation.
*Cannot inflict {{D|Void}} or {{D|Tau}} status effects.

==Tips==
*Inflicted status effects are spread by {{Arcane|Melee Influence}}.
*{{Arcane|Melee Afflictions}} activates after the status is applied, immediately granting additional stacks.
*{{M|Discipline's Merit}} synergizes well with the {{Weapon|Amanata}}'s innate attack speed buff and can enable consistent heavy attacks while preserving combo and thus more frequent applications of this mod's unique effect.
