'''Volatile Quick Return''' is a [[mod]] for [[Glaive (Weapon Type)|Glaives]] that reduces the number of thrown [[bounce]]s, increases explosion radius, and adds a chance for the glaive to explode on bouncing but disables [[punch through]].

==Notes==
*Explosions do '''50%''' of [[Heavy Attack]] damage, doesn't benefit from the combo count damage multiplier, and does not consume it. 
*Explosions from this mod do not inflict guaranteed procs unlike manual detonations. (bug? except the guaranteed impact proc from {{Weapon|Glaive Prime}}.) It can apply regular status proc depending of status chance.
*Enemies that are hit only by the explosion ''but not the weapon itself'' do not increase the [[Melee Combo|combo count]].
*After hitting and getting the regular explosion from this mod, the glaive can still be manually detonated for a Heavy Attack explosion during the same throw.
*Cannot be equipped on {{Weapon|Deconstructor}} ({{Weapon|Deconstructor Prime|Prime}}), due to its lack of explosive properties.
*Cannot be equipped with {{M|Quick Return}}, since it is considered a variant of this mod.
*Cannot be equipped with {{M|Volatile Rebound}} or {{M|Rebound}}.
