'''{{D|Tau}} Damage''', also known as '''Sentient Damage''', is a special damage type exclusive to only a few [[Sentient]]-related equipments. Similar to {{D|Void}} Damage, it is neutral to all health types, meaning its damage is neither increased or decreased against any target, though Warframes are able to gain {{D|Tau}} Resistance from the [[Set Mods#Umbral Mod Set|Umbral Mod Set]]. Its [[Status Effect]] applies [[Status Chance]] vulnerability.

==[[Status Effect]]s==
The status effect of {{D|Tau}} damage is '''Status Chance Vulnerability'''. It increases [[Status Chance]] received from all other status sources by '''10%''' per stack for up to '''10''' stacks and lasts for '''8''' seconds, with each stack having its own duration. Any stacks applied after the 10th will replace the oldest stack.

*For example: Enemies with 5 stacks (50% Status Chance Vulnerability) will receive 150% the amount of status procs when they are attacked. Enemies with 10 stacks will receive double the amount of status procs. If attacked with a weapon with 210% chance status this would result in a hit with 305% chance of status.

[[Status Effect#Forced Procs|Forced Procs]] (such as {{Weapon|Hystrix}}'s forced elemental procs) will not create additional procs due to this vulnerability.

'''Introducing a new Status Effect: Tau Damage'''

Being of half-Sentient origin, Caliban brings a new element to the fight. He’ll inflict '''Tau Damage''' and '''Tau Status Effects''' upon the battlefield with many of his abilities (specifics listed below for affected abilities).

Tau’s Status Effect inflicts '''Status Chance Vulnerability*''' to enemies affected by it, with a max of 10 Stacks culminating in 100% Status Chance Vulnerability (10% per Stack), with each stack individually lasting 8 seconds!

<nowiki>*</nowiki>This effect was originally introduced by Dante and his Pageflight Paragrimms, which applies Status Chance Vulnerability to enemies. This mechanic increases the likelihood an enemy receives a Status Effect when being hit.
