'''Exodia Triumph''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] for [[Zaw]]s that grants a chance to gain additional Combo Count from melee attacks.

==Acquisition==
Can be bought from [[Hok]] for {{sc|10,000}} 
per arcane upon reaching the rank of '''Kin''' with the [[Ostron]]s.

==Notes==
*Melee-based warframe abilities, such as [[Atlas|Atlas's]] [[Landslide]], can generate extra combo through the use of this arcane.
