'''Chromatic Blade''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Excalibur}}'s {{A|Exalted Blade}} that increases {{Weapon|Exalted Blade}}'s [[status chance]] while changing its damage from physical damage to a [[Damage#Primary Elemental Damage|primary elemental]] damage depending on Excalibur's chosen [[Emissive Color|primary emission color]].<section end=intro />

==Acquisition==
*This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''General''' under {{Faction|Steel Meridian}}, or the rank of '''Maxim''' under the {{Faction|Arbiters of Hexis}}, and spending {{sc|25000}} to purchase.

==Notes==
*[[Status chance]] bonus is additive with other status chance mods and is affected by {{stat|Ability Strength}}.
**{{MathText|Total Status Chance {{eql}} Base status chance {{mul}} [1 + (Chromatic Blade Bonus {{mul}} (1 + Strength Mods)) + Status Mods]}}
**For example, using a maxed {{M|Intensify}}, {{M|Drifting Contact}}, and Chromatic Blade results in:<br />{{Math|0.15 {{mul}} [1 + ({{M|Chromatic Blade|3}} {{mul}} {{M|Intensify|1.3}}) + {{M|Drifting Contact|0.4}}]|p=y|t=y|bf=y}} status chance<br>
**Achieving 100% status chance without any status chance mods requires '''189%'''{{stat|Ability Strength}}.
*Chromatic Blade's color selection is based on Excalibur's [[Warframe Cosmetics#Colors|primary emission color]]:
**White, grey, black, and some faded colors make {{D|Cold}}.
**Blue and purple hues make {{D|Electricity}}.
**Red, magenta, brown, orange, and bright yellow hues make {{D|Heat}}.
**Green, lime, teal, and dark yellow hues make {{D|Toxin}}.
**Purchasing color palettes is '''not''' required to unlock all the elemental options.
**While customizing Excalibur's emission color, Chromatic Blade's active element is indicated by an icon ({{D|Heat|}}, {{D|Electricity|}}, {{D|Toxin|}}, {{D|Cold|}}). This [http://imgur.com/a/FVUu2 imgur album] can serve as a reference for future color palette purchases.
**Customizing the secondary emission and the energy colors will have no effect.
**The [[Arthur Gemini Skin]] swaps between appearance configs, allowing the player to color for two separate elements and swap between them with the Gemini [[Emote]].
*Chromatic Blade's elemental damage replaces the innate physical elements and will combine with any elemental damage modded on {{Weapon|Exalted Blade}}.
*Physical damage mods will no longer affect Exalted Blade's damage with Chromatic Blade equipped.

==Bugs==
*In the [[Simulacrum]], Chromatic Blade's bonus is applied twice, i.e., at 100% Ability Strength it gives '''+600%''' status chance instead of only '''+300%'''.
*For unknown reasons, the element type given by the augment is sometimes shown as Electricity (and forms electric-based compound elements) regardless of emissive color.
*Chromatic Blade's elemental change does not affect Exalted Blade's slam attacks.

==Tips==
*Although Chromatic Blade removes all Physical Damage, it is still possible to inflict {{D|Impact}} procs through the '''Cutting Poise''' combo of Exalted Blade's [[Exalted Blade (Stance)|stance]].
*Due to the large [[status chance]] boost this augment gives, it pairs well with {{M|Condition Overload}}. Furthermore, players can forgo dual status-elemental mods, such as {{M|Volcanic Edge}}, and use mods that only increase Elemental Damage, like {{M|Molten Impact}}, to further increase Exalted Blade's total damage.
*The status chance boost also simplifies creating a Crit-Status hybrid build, as status chance mods are no longer necessary.

==Trivia==
*Chromatic Blade is the only one of Excalibur's augments not to feature him with his [[Immortal Skin Bundle|Immortal Skin]] and [[Excalibur Pendragon Helmet|Pendragon Helmet]] equipped on the mod card.
*The name of the augment comes from the Greek word ''Chroma'', meaning colour.
