'''Primal Rage''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Wukong}} that increases the [[critical chance]] of {{A|Primal Fury}} with every kill, up to a maximum of 150%. The additional [[critical chance]] gradually falls off after {{WF|Wukong}} stops killing for 5 seconds.<section end=intro />

==Acquisition==
*This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''Maxim''' under the {{Faction|Arbiters of Hexis}}, or the rank of '''Flawless''' under {{Faction|New Loka}}, and spending {{sc|25,000}} to purchase.

==Notes==
*Primal Rage has a bonus cap of +150% critical chance. At full stacks {{Weapon|Iron Staff}}'s critical chance will be {{Math|25% * (1+150%)|bf-a=y|p=y}}.
**This Augment '''only''' provides bonus critical chance to Iron Staff.
**The critical chance bonus is additive with critical chance mods.
***For example, with a max-ranked {{M|True Steel}} and {{M|Primal Rage}}'s bonus at 150%, Iron Staff will have:<br />{{MathText|Base Critical Chance {{mul}} (1 + Critical Chance Mods + Primal Fury) {{eql}}}}<br />{{Math|25% {{mul}} (1 + 1.2 + 1.5)|round|3|mt = y|p=y}} critical chance
*Critical chance per kill is affected by [[Ability Strength]] while the decay rate is affected by [[Ability Duration]].
**The maximum bonus critical chance cap of 150% is '''not''' affected by mods.
**For example, with a max-ranked {{M|Intensify}} and {{M|Primal Rage}}, Iron Staff will gain:<br />{{MathText|Base Critical Chance per Kill {{mul}} (1 + Strength Mods) {{eql}}}} {{Math|15% {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|mt=y|p=y}} critical chance per kill
**With a max-ranked {{M|Continuity}}, the critical chance decay rate becomes:<br />{{MathText|Base Decay Rate {{div}} (1 + Duration Mods) {{eql}}}} {{Math|1% {{div}} (1 + 0.3)|round|4|mt=y|p=y}}/sec
*{{WF|Wukong}} retains Primal Rage's effects as long as Primal Fury remains active, and the bonus will reset if Primal Fury is deactivated by any means.
*Primal Rage's current percentages are displayed beside {{WF|Wukong}}'s shield and health indicators.
