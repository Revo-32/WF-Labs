'''Arcane Eruption''' is an [[Arcane Enhancement]] that provides a chance to {{D|Knockdown}} nearby enemies on [[Energy Orb]] pickups. The knockdown goes through walls and is not restricted by [[Line of Sight]].

Can be sold for {{cc|500}}.

===History===
*Arcane Eruption was first introduced in the [[Trial]] mission [[The Law of Retribution]], which was retired in {{ver|22.14}}.
*The [[Operation: Scarlet Spear]] event sold Arcane Eruption from [[Little Duck]] for 1,500 {{Resource|Scarlet Credits}}.
*The [[Operation: Orphix Venom]] event sold Arcane Eruption from [[Father]] for 300 {{Resource|Phasic Cells}}.
*The [[Operation: Gargoyle's Cry]] event sold Arcane Eruption from the [[Jahu Gargoyle|Vigile Jahu Gargoyle]] for 5 {{Resource|Grotesque Splinter}}.
*The [[Operation: Belly of the Beast]] event sold Arcane Eruption from [[Ordis]] for 3 {{Resource|Volatile Motes}} in the first run of the event, and 4 Motes in the second, with a purchase limit of 42.
*The [[Operation: Eight Claw]] event sold Arcane Eruption from [[Dominus Thrax]] in bundles of 7, costing 2 {{Resource|Dominus Aureus}}, with a purchase limit of 3 (21 Arcanes in total).

==Notes==
*Universal Orbs created by {{WF|Lavos}}' {{A|Transmutation Probe}} can trigger Arcane Eruption.
*{{M|Equilibrium}} Health Orb conversion and Energy Motes spawned by {{Arcane|Emergence Dissipate}} can '''not''' trigger Arcane Eruption.
