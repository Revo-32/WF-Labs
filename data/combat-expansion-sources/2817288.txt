The '''Ack & Brunt''' is an axe & shield set used by [[Tyl Regor]] that boasts a high critical hit chance in exchange for a low attack speed. It also has the unique ability to absorb [[Damage#Primary Elemental Damage|elemental]] attacks on [[Melee#Blocking|block]] to empower the weapon's next attack.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Blocking [[Damage#Primary Elemental Damage|elemental damage]] attacks stores a charge that grants '''+17.5%''' of the absorbed elemental damage to the next attack. The charge can stack up to '''4''' times before being consumed.
*Can use the Ack & Brunt-exclusive {{M|Electromagnetic Shielding}} mod.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Eleventh Storm}}, {{M|Final Harbinger}} and {{M|Last Herald}} stance.

==Notes==
*Blocking elemental damage attacks creates a stack that increases the damage of the next attack by '''17.5%''' stacks. The effect can stack up to '''4''' times before being consumed, increasing the damage up to '''70%'''.
*The damage bonus increases additively with elemental mods, which can also combine with elemental mods from the weapon to became a combined elements. For example, if your Ack & Brunt is modded with {{D|Electricity}} damage (e.g. {{M|Shocking Touch}}), blocking {{D|Heat}} damage from a [[Flameblade]]'s {{Weapon|Twin Basolk}} will combine it into {{D|Radiation}} damage on the next attack.
*Elemental damage taken by nearby allies via {{M|Electromagnetic Shielding}} can be converted to stacks.

==Tips==
*{{M|Final Harbinger}} can offset the Ack & Brunt's low attack speed with its quick combos.
*Using the Ack & Brunt with {{M|Final Harbinger}}, {{M|Blood Rush}} and/or [[Set Mods#Gladiator Mod Set|Gladiator Mods]] allows players to quickly gather hits for the [[Melee#Combo Counter|Combo Counter]], rapidly boosting the Ack & Brunt's critical chance.
*Absorbing enemy attacks allows players to almost double the amount of damage they can deal.
**This is useful when focusing on taking down heavy units in a crowd.
**Do note however that stacks are expended if an enemy melee attack is parried.
*([[Corrupted Bombard|Corrupted]]) [[Bombard]] rockets can add up to two stacks of elemental damage, allowing players to collect stacks faster.

==Trivia==
*This is the weapon of choice for Tyl Regor following {{ver|17}}, though instead of holding the weapon, Tyl Regor is able to attach the axe and shield to the ends of his cybernetic limbs in place of his {{Weapon|Knux}} hands. Regor, however, explicitly refers to Ack as a gavel.
**Before {{ver|17}}, his weapon of choice was the {{Weapon|Skana}}.
*Tyl Regor uses a different design for the shield, as it is normally part of his helmet/headdress (or perhaps vice-versa). The Tenno version presumably adapts the shield to collapse so that it does not take up as much room when stowed.
*The Grineer text on the inner part of the shield translates to "MEAT PROTECTION".

==Bugs==
*When in its [[Incarnon]] form, the shield will never leave the players hand during stances that normally throw the shield.
