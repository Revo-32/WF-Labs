{{D|Gas}} '''Damage''' is one of the six secondary elemental damage types, composed of {{D|Heat}} and {{D|Toxin}} elements. Deals increased damage to {{D|Infested Deimos}} and {{D|Techrot}} enemies but reduced damage against {{D|Scaldra}} enemies. Its [[Status Effect]] creates a damaging cloud that deals {{D|Gas}} [[Damage over Time]].

==[[Status Effect]]s==
===General===
The status effect of {{D|Gas}} damage is '''Gas Cloud'''. It produces a gas cloud that deals a tick of damage each second to all enemies within a '''3'''-meter radius; subsequent procs increase the radius by '''0.3''' meters up to '''6 '''meters in total after '''10''' stacks. The cloud has a '''6''' second duration. Each tick deals {{D|Gas}} damage equal to:

:<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Gas Proc Damage per Tick} &= 0.5 \times \text{Modded Base Damage} \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Gas Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times (1 + \text{Status Damage Bonuses}) \\
&\qquad \times \text{Additional Multipliers}
\end{aligned}
}
</math>
::''Gas Damage Bonuses include {{M|Valence Formation}}.''
::''Additional Multipliers include modded critical multiplier on [[Critical Hit]] and multipliers on [[Enemy Body Parts]]; these stack multiplicatively with each other.''
:Modded Base Damage is not the same as normal damage calculations, ignoring physical and elemental damage bonuses and calculated as:
::<math>
{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Modded Base Damage} &= \text{Base Damage} \times (1 + \text{Base Damage Bonuses}) \times (1 + \text{Faction Damage Bonuses})
\end{aligned}
}
</math>

As can be seen from the formula, tick damage scales with {{D|Gas}} mods ({{D|Heat}} and {{D|Toxin}} mods have no effect). Up to '''10''' instances of the effect can stack on the same target, with each instance having its own timer. Any instances after the 10th will replace the oldest instance.

The Gas Cloud is able to hit heads and other [[Enemy Body Parts|body parts]] by itself. However, the Gas Cloud itself has a '''1x''' damage bonus when headshotting. This can be increased with the use of mods such as {{M|Target Acquired}}, zoom bonuses on some [[Sniper Rifle|sniper rifles]], or {{Arcane|Primary Deadhead}}/{{Arcane|Secondary Deadhead}}.

Example 1 ({{D|Heat}} and {{D|Toxin}} mods), tick damage for a weapon with an innate 180 damage, {{M|Hornet Strike}},  {{M|Scorch}}, {{M|Pathogen Rounds}}, {{M|Expel Grineer}}, and {{M|Pistol Elementalist}} will be:

:Modded Base Damage = {{Math|180 {{mul}} (1 + 2.2) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y}}
:'''Tick Damage''' = {{Math|0.5 {{mul}} 748.8 {{mul}} (1 + 0.3) {{mul}} (1 + 0.9)|t=y|bf-a=y}}

Example 2 ({{D|Gas}} mod), tick damage for the {{Weapon|Vesper 77}} with {{M|Hornet Strike}}, {{M|Leaded Gas}}, {{M|Expel Grineer}}, and {{M|Pistol Elementalist}} will be:

:Modded Base Damage = {{Math|180 {{mul}} (1 + 2.2) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3)|t=y|bf-a=y}}
:'''Tick Damage''' = {{Math|0.5 {{mul}} 748.8 {{mul}} (1 + 3) {{mul}} (1 + 0.3) {{mul}} (1 + 0.9)|t=y|bf-a=y}}

As can be seen in the above calculations for tick damage, Faction Damage is applied twice, making their effective bonus {{MathText|{{eql}} (1 + Faction Damage bonus)<sup>2</sup>}}. Which is +69% for the 30% Faction Damage mods and +140.25% for the 55% Primed Faction Damage mods.

If the host target dies, {{D|Gas}} will continue to tick damage on all enemies caught in the host's radius for its remaining duration.

A {{D|Gas}} proc briefly creates a dark green cloud on the afflicted.

===Railjack===
{{D|Gas}} does not have a [[Railjack]] space counterpart and is thus not able to proc any status effect nor is it included in calculations for status proc weighting.

==Bugs==
*{{D|Gas Cloud}} is misaligned on all units with a {{E|Heavy Gunner}}-type body, both the procs applied through a headshot and the lingering clouds from dead enemies often miss the head.

==Trivia==
*Gas damage, with its brown color, damage over time, and its extra lethality to Deimos {{Faction|Infested}}, is likely a reference or callback to [https://darksector.fandom.com/wiki/Enferon Enferon], a gas that is highly lethal to creatures infected by the [https://darksector.fandom.com/wiki/Technocyte_Virus Technocyte Virus] but not uninfected humans in Digital Extremes' earlier title, [[wikipedia:Dark_Sector|Dark Sector]].
**This is further expanded with the weakness {{Faction|Techrot}} possess, as well as the existence of [[Efervon Sample|Efervon]], a very similarly named substance used by {{Faction|Scaldra}} against the Techrot. Scaldra are also resistant to Gas damage.
