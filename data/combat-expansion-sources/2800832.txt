==Acquisition==
The Rabvee blueprint can be acquired after reaching the Rank of '''Neutral''' with the [[Ostron]]s and buying it from [[Hok]] for {{sc|1,000}}.

==Characteristics==
This Strike primarily deals {{D|Impact}} damage.

* '''60%''' {{D|Impact}}
* '''5%''' {{D|Puncture}}
* '''35%''' {{D|Slash}}

Compatible weapon types:
*Machete
*Hammer

== Trivia ==

* The text on the neck of this Zaw strike roughly translates as "Crush" from the Ostron language

==Bugs==

*When built with onehanded grip (Machete), it is not holstered properly, staying in hand when equipping primary or secondary weapon. It is also impossible to change holster style, which is set to "Nikana Standard Holster".
