| info = *Wukong expends {{Stat|Ability Efficiency|icon=only}}'''10''' [[energy]] to equip his legendary '''{{Weapon|Iron Staff}}''' as his melee [[Exalted Weapon]]. [[Melee#Normal Attack|Normal attacks]] inflict {{Stat|Ability Strength|icon=only}}'''100 / 200 / 250 / 300''' damage with a <u>'''3.5'''</u> meter range.
**Exclusively uses the {{M|Primal Fury}} Stance.
**Damage is distributed between '''68%''' {{D|Impact}} damage and '''32%''' {{D|Slash}} damage.
**Iron Staff's range increases by <u>'''0.5'''</u> meters per combo level, capping at <u>'''+5.5'''</u> meters of range at 12x combo.
**Iron Staff has a <u>'''2x'''</u> [[critical multiplier]], <u>'''25%'''</u> [[critical chance]], and <u>'''30%'''</u> [[status chance]]. 
**Iron Staff can be equipped with melee mods and [[Arcane Enhancement]]s.
***[[Riven Mods]] are '''not''' generated for Iron Staff.
**Because Primal Fury is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as {{M|Steel Charge}} and {{Arcane|Arcane Fury}}.
**Primal Fury consumes {{Stat|Ability Efficiency|icon=only}}{{Stat|Ability Duration|icon=only}}'''5''' energy per second while active and will remain active until Wukong's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
***Wukong '''cannot''' replenish energy using {{A|Energy Vampire}}, {{A|Rally Point}}, [[Rift Plane]]'s innate energy regeneration, [[Squad Energy Restore]]s, {{M|Energy Siphon}}, {{M|Energy Nexus}}, {{M|Dreamer's Bond}}, and/or {{Focus|Wellspring}} while Primal Fury is active.
***[[Energy Orb]]s, [[Orokin Void]] [[Orokin Tower#Death Orbs|Death Orb]]'s energy restores, {{M|Rage}}, {{M|Hunter Adrenaline}}, {{M|Spellbound Harvest}}, {{Arcane|Arcane Energize}}, and {{Arcane|Emergence Dissipate}}'s Energy Motes can still replenish Wukong's energy while Primal Fury is active.
***Primal Fury's energy drain is '''not''' converted into shields by [[Set Mods#Augur Mod Set|Augur Mods]].
*While Primal Fury is active, Iron Staff is the only melee weapon Wukong can use. However, his other weapons and abilities can still be cast normally.
*Primal Fury comes installed with its own unique [[Primal Fury (Stance)|stance]]. Additional combos will be unlocked as Primal Fury increases in rank.
*The ends of Iron Staff have portions made out of energy.
**These portions are affected by Wukong's energy color.
**With the range bonus active, these portions are elongated.
*Primal Fury's attacks can damage enemies across the [[Rift Plane]].
