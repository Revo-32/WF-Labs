'''Coda Pathocyst''' is the [[Coda]] variant of the Infested {{Weapon|Pathocyst}} glaive. In addition to higher damage, [[critical chance]], [[critical multiplier]], and [[status chance]], it can spawn stronger [[Techrot Miasmite]]s with a larger explosion radius.

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Viral}} damage.
*Can be wielded in-tandem with a single-handed secondary weapon.
*Holding the melee button throws the glaive, which can [[bounce]] up to three times or travels up to '''30''' meters before returning to the user, damaging anyone in its path. Meleeing while the glaive is in mid-flight commands it to return.
**Primary and secondary weapons can be fired and reloaded normally with a thrown glaive, and retrieving the glaive does not interrupt these actions. Additional throws can also be "buffered" while the glaive is in mid-flight.
**Thrown glaive is [[silent]].
**Thrown glaive has no [[punch through]].
**[[Heavy Attack]]s while the glaive is in mid-flight produce a '''4.9''' meter explosion and forces the glaive to return.
***Explosion inflicts a guaranteed {{D|Viral}} proc.
***Explosion does not need direct line of sight to deal damage and will penetrate walls.
***No [[Damage Falloff]].
***Explosion inflicts [[Stagger|self-stagger]].
***Explosion has a [[Enemy Body Parts|headshot]] multiplier of 1x and cannot trigger headshot conditions.
**Cannot use melee attacks or block while the glaive is in mid-flight.
**During flight or when striking an enemy, the glaive drops Infested pods which quickly hatch into [[Techrot Miasmite|Maggots]] that last for 7 seconds and will fight alongside the player before exploding in a '''?''' radius.
***Maggots can latch onto enemies and stagger them.
***Maggots may also appear on any normal attack done to enemies.
***Maggots alerts enemies, despite the weapon being silent.
*Can use the Glaive-exclusive mods {{M|Combo Fury}}, {{M|Combo Killer}},{{M|Mark of the Beast}} {{M|Power Throw}}, {{M|Quick Return}} ({{M|Volatile Quick Return|Volatile}}), {{M|Rebound}} ({{M|Volatile Rebound|Volatile}}), and {{M|Whirlwind}}.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Naramon}} polarity, matching {{M|Gleaming Talon}}, {{M|Astral Twilight}} and {{M|Celestial Nightfall}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*Innate {{Pol|Madurai}} and {{Pol|Vazarin}} polarities.
*Adversary Weapon:
**Comes with an additional bonus {{D|Impact}}, {{D|Heat}}, {{D|Cold}}, {{D|Electricity}}, {{D|Toxin}}, {{D|Magnetic}}, or {{D|Radiation}} damage, which increases the listed base damage of the weapon by 25%-60%.
***[[Valence Fusion]] can optionally change the resulting output damage type, and can upgrade the bonus increase (up to 60%).
**[[Polarization|Polarizing]] the weapon increases its max [[rank]] by '''2''', capping at rank '''40''' after '''5''' polarizations, granting the weapon additional mod capacity.
**{{Resource|Elemental Vice}} can be applied to the weapon after it has reached rank 40, allowing the bonus damage type to be swapped freely.
**Unlike [[Kuva (Variant)|Kuva]] and [[Tenet]] weapons directly acquired from a [[Kuva Lich]] or [[Sisters of Parvos]], the listed [[Mastery Rank]] requirement '''is''' enforced.
