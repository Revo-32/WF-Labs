'''Hall of Malevolence''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Mirage}}'s {{A|Hall of Mirrors}} that makes her doppelgangers deal more damage with every enemy killed.

==Acquisition==
*This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''Maxim''' under the {{Faction|Arbiters of Hexis}}, or the rank of '''Genius''' under {{Faction|Cephalon Suda}}, and spending {{sc|25,000}} to purchase.

==Notes==
*The damage increase per kill can only stack a maximum of '''10''' times.
**At full rank, doppelgangers will deal '''50%''' more damage at 10 stacks.
*The damage boost is applied '''multiplicatively''' and '''not''' affected by [[Ability Strength]].
*Damage increase is reset once Hall of Mirrors expires.
