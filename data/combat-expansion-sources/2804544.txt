'''Keratinos''' are [[Infested]] claws that grow in length to perform a deadly spin attack for [[Heavy Attack]]s. The claws stay in their increased range for 3 minutes when a heavy attack is executed at full [[Melee#Combo|combo]].

==Characteristics==
*This weapon deals primarily {{D|Slash}} damage.
*Stance slot has {{Pol|Madurai}} polarity, matching {{M|Vermillion Storm}}, {{M|Four Riders}}, {{M|Malicious Raptor}} and {{M|Scarlet Hurricane}} ([[Conclave]] only) stance.
*It has a unique [[Heavy Attack]]:
**The first attack consists of a spin with 6 hits. The first 3 hits do '''100% damage''', the next 3 do '''100%''' damage and have forced {{D|Slash}} procs.
**The second attack is a lunge with one hit that does '''500%''' damage and has a forced {{D|Slash}} proc.
**Heavy Attacking with 12x [[Melee#Combo|combo]] grants '''+2.8m''' range to all melee attacks for '''180''' seconds.

{{Advantages|
*Third highest base damage of all claws, behind {{Weapon|Garuda Talons}} and {{Weapon|Garuda Prime Talons}}.
*Second highest [[status chance]] of all claws, behind Garuda Talons and Garuda Prime Talons.
}}

{{Disadvantages|}}

==Acquisition==
Keratinos are acquired by reaching '''Rank 0 - Neutral''' with the [[Entrati]]. [[Father]] sells the main blueprint for {{sc|1,000}} and component blueprints for {{sc|500}}; a total of {{sc|3,000}} is needed for all parts.

==Trivia==
*The Keratinos' name is most likely derived from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keratin Keratin], a fibrous protein which makes up the claws, nails, hair, feathers, and hooves of most animals.
