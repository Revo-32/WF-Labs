'''Reactive Storm''' is a [[Warframe Augment Mod]] for {{WF|Baruuk}}'s {{A|Serene Storm}} to increase {{Weapon|Desert Wind}}'s [[status chance]] and alters its damage type to match enemy weaknesses.<section end=intro />

==Acquisition==
*This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of '''Flawless''' under {{Faction|New Loka}}, or the rank of '''Maxim''' under the {{Faction|Arbiters of Hexis}}, and spending {{sc|25,000}} to purchase.
*This mod can periodically be acquired from [[The Circuit]].

==Notes==
*The [[status chance]] increase granted by this augment is affected by [[Ability Strength]] and additive with any other status chance mods.
**For example, using a maxed {{M|Intensify}}, {{M|Drifting Contact}}, and Reactive Storm results in:<br />{{MathText|Base status chance {{mul}} [1 + (Reactive Storm Bonus {{mul}} (1 + Strength Mods)) + Status Mods] {{eql}} 0.15 {{mul}} [1 + (2.5 {{mul}} 1.3) + 0.4] {{eql}} 69.7% status chance}}
**To achieve 100% status chance without any status chance mods, Ability Strength must be at least '''227%'''.
*Only Desert Wind's {{D|Impact}} damage is altered to match as enemies weakest damage type.
**Melee {{D|Impact}} mods such as {{M|Primed Heavy Trauma}}, increase the amount of altered elemental damage, but do not retain an {{D|Impact}} damage type after alteration.
**If damage becomes a primary element, it will not combine with other modded primary elements.
*To adapt damage types, Baruuk can strike with both his fists/feet at close range or {{Weapon|Desert Wind}}'s long-range waves.
**Once set (by the last enemy struck by fists), the new damage type will affect the waves.
**Damage type will reset to {{D|Impact}} when {{A|Serene Storm}} deactivates.
*Damage type will prioritize combined and non-physical elements and the ''first'' element a given faction is vulnerable to based on the in-game UI, limiting its potential damage conversions to the following:
**{{D|Heat}} ({{D|Infested}})
**{{D|Electricity}} ({{D|Anarchs}})
**{{D|Toxin}} ({{D|Narmer}})
**{{D|Corrosive}} ({{D|Grineer}}, {{D|Kuva Grineer}}, {{D|Zariman}}, and {{D|Scaldra}})
**{{D|Magnetic}} ({{D|Corpus}}, {{D|Corpus Amalgam}}, and {{D|Techrot}})
**{{D|Viral}} ({{D|Orokin}})
**{{D|Radiation}} ({{D|Sentient}} and {{D|The Murmur}})
**{{D|Gas}} ({{D|Infested Deimos}})
*Damage Resistance reduction by {{Arcane|Magus Accelerant}}, {{Arcane|Magus Destruct}} or {{A|Petrify}} will influence the new damage type.
*Damage type does not change in response to [[Sentient]] damage adaptation. It will adapt as normal to a Sentient's weaknesses, but will not change if the Sentient adapts.
*Damage remains {{D|Impact}} as when adapting to an enemy without any health type, or while {{D|Overguard}} is present.

==Tips==
*Due to the large [[status chance]] boost this augment gives, it pairs well with {{M|Condition Overload}}. Furthermore, players can forgo dual status-elemental mods, such as {{M|Volcanic Edge}}, and use mods that only increase Elemental Damage, like {{M|Molten Impact}}, to further increase Desert Wind's total damage.
*The status chance boost also simplifies creating a Crit-Status hybrid build as status chance mods are no longer necessary.
